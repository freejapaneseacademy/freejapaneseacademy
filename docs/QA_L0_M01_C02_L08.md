# L0-M01-C02-L08 — speaking and shadowing QA handoff (2026-11-03)

**Can-do:** distinguish Japanese's five vowel qualities and meaningful mora
timing; rehearse practical corrections in authentic spoken situations.
Speaking and audio recording are optional. Written five-item multiple choice
checks **preparation knowledge only**, never pronunciation or fluency.

Nothing is regenerated, replaced or uploaded.

## Interactions and data
New VowelSpeakingPractice React island uses existing real source media and
turn start/end timestamps. User may play full tracks, play individual turns,
adjust speed, reveal transcripts, shadow aloud and check a five-criterion
**ungraded** speaking rubric. MediaRecorder is requested ONLY after clicking
Start, and local blobs are discarded on unmount or on user request; download
is optional. Denial or unsupported devices do not block completion.
Five original multiple-choice preparation items use the existing Dexie v1
recordAttempt system with unique stable ID l0-m01-c02-l08-check-v1.
Completion is separately user-initiated and not a speaking certificate.
