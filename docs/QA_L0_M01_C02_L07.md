## Content and question checks

The ten original constrained prompts assess written hiragana production and taught word distinctions only. All are version 1 with stable IDs `ex-l0-m01-c02-l07-001` through `010`, objective and knowledge references, a finite exact accepted answer and individual explanation. Accepted forms in order: `あ`, `い`, `う`, `え`, `お`, `かさ`, `いえ`, `いいえ`, `おばさん`, `おばあさん`. A Japanese IME or complete on-screen kana palette can enter every target. NFC plus surrounding whitespace is the only normalization; no missing vowel is silently accepted.

The lesson teaches `いえ` (house, two morae) versus `いいえ` (no, three morae) and, **in a fictional family chart**, `おばさん` (aunt, four morae) versus `おばあさん` (grandmother, five morae). The chart meanings are contextual rather than universal: both kinship expressions can also describe women of different ages. The `ん` mora is counted. The five vowel kana are treated as writing targets, not claims of measured pronunciation. A written example with `こうこう` explicitly prevents an inaccurate universal doubled-vowel spelling rule.

The guided stage provides four context-dependent choices and feedback. Open writing asks for two polite fictional classroom responses and an English explanation; a model and four-criterion self-review are provided. **No automatic score is assigned to free composition.** A text export is learner-initiated; drafts are not uploaded or silently saved.
