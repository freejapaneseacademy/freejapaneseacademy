## Learning design
Two original dialogues: before-class kana practice and after-class study planning. Six scored single-choice listening questions (three per track). Every key has literal evidence in the corresponding job turn. Answers do not require seeing Japanese writing; transcripts are optional, and the reusable player separates assisted practice from blind scores. Learners can replay, use keyboard controls and complete separately from scoring.

## Language checks (author review, not independent sign-off)
Track A: ひらがなを れんしゅうします is a natural response to なにを れんしゅうしますか. ノートは ありますか asks about having a notebook. ちいさい「つ」が むずかしいです expresses a specific difficulty. Track B contrasts カタカナは だいじょうぶです with ながい おとが むずかしいです and gives an explicit next-day action. English translations preserve the practical meaning. The Japanese is an original, simple classroom exchange; punctuation is not a scored auditory feature.
