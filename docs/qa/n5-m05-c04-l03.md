## Japanese accuracy review checklist
- 午前九時に会います: に marks a specific time.
- 駅で会います: で marks the location of meeting.
- 電車で行きます: で marks transport means.
- 博物館へ行きます: へ marks destination, pronounced え.
- 駅から行きます: から marks origin.
- 友達と行きます: と marks companion.
- Days may take に; avoid claiming this is obligatory in all contexts.
- Fictional fares and plans are labelled as examples, not live transport data.
