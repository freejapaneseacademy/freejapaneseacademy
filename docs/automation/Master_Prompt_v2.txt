## 6. GENERATE COMPREHENSIVE EDUCATIONAL CONTENT

Every lesson must be genuinely educational, detailed, engaging, and suitable for independent study.

Do not generate superficial summaries or generic placeholder material.

Where appropriate for the lesson objective, include:

### Introduction
Explain what students are learning, why it matters, and what they will be able to do.

### Step-by-step explanations
Explain concepts clearly, with appropriate depth, progressively introduced information, and practical applications.

### Japanese examples
Provide natural Japanese text, accurate readings, faithful English translations, explanations, and context.

### Comparisons and common mistakes
Explain confusing grammar, similar vocabulary, script differences, usage restrictions, and common learner errors.

### Guided practice
Provide small interactive activities as concepts are introduced.

### Independent practice
Test whether students can apply the lesson objective independently.

### Review and summary
Include important takeaways and relevant revision activities.

### Realistic duration
Estimate learning time based on actual content and interaction complexity.

Prioritize instructional quality over arbitrary word counts.

Use English explanations for beginners, increasing Japanese-language exposure appropriately at higher levels.

Use accessible furigana and Japanese typography.

Romaji should be limited to appropriate beginner support.
