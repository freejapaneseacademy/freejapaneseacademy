## Check your Chapter 03 skills

You have practised the M row (ま・み・む・め・も), Y row (や・ゆ・よ), R row (ら・り・る・れ・ろ), and W-related kana (わ・を), plus **ん**. This checkpoint combines written forms, a short original reading and two previously published Japanese audio conversations. **Lesson 08 speaking is optional and is not a prerequisite.**

### Review the two tricky characters

**ん** counts as one mora, including at the end of **ほん** (*hon*, book). It is not a silent letter. In **よんで** (*yonde*, “read” in the request よんでください), ん remains its own mora.

**を** is normally written as the direct-object particle and pronounced *o* in modern standard Japanese. In **ももをふたつください。** (“Two peaches, please”), the word **もも** is the requested item and **を** marks it as the object. Do not replace the written particle with お.

### Read before answering

A small classroom notice says:

> **やまのほん** — a book about mountains  
> **ほんをみてください。** — Please look at the book.  
> **ほんをよんでください。** — Please read the book.

A fruit-shop label says:

> **もも** — peaches  
> **みかん** — mandarins  
> **ももをふたつください。** — Two peaches, please.

Notice the difference between **みて** (“look”) and **よんで** (“read”). Both examples are polite requests with **ください**; their verb meanings differ.
