## Your optional speaking goal

Imagine helping a classmate find an umbrella, then discussing lunch. These are **two fictional conversations** using the actual, unchanged AivisSpeech WAV recordings from Lesson 06. This is **speaking practice**, not a new listening exam. You may read the model text while shadowing; there is no blind-listening score.

The fifteen kana you know already include <span lang="ja">かさ</span> (*kasa*, umbrella), <span lang="ja">ここ</span> (*koko*, here) and <span lang="ja">すし</span> (*sushi*, sushi). The complete conversations contain additional beginner Japanese. You do not need to master those new forms to rehearse the short featured words.

## Step 1 — Listen, notice, repeat

**Track A: asking for an umbrella.** Listen to the woman's first turn, <span lang="ja">すみません。かさはありますか。</span> (“Excuse me. Do you have an umbrella?”). The important familiar word is <span lang="ja">かさ</span>. Listen to the man's reply, <span lang="ja">はい。ここにあります。</span> (“Yes. There is one here.”). The familiar location word is <span lang="ja">ここ</span>. The particles and polite endings are useful extra language, not part of the fifteen-kana writing test.

**Track B: choosing lunch.** Listen to the woman's reply <span lang="ja">すしをたべます。</span> (“I'll eat sushi.”). Say <span lang="ja">すし</span> with the model, not by guessing an English “she” sound for <span lang="ja">し</span>. The rest of the phrase is an extension beyond the taught fifteen kana.

Use the full recording first, then the **Play this turn** controls. Pause after each model and imitate the short phrase at a comfortable pace. The transcript is provided deliberately for speaking support. No automatic claim is made that a printed romaji cue captures exact Japanese phonetics.

## Step 2 — A tiny supported role-play

You are asking for an umbrella. Your partner has one nearby. Practise the exchange:

- <span lang="ja">かさはありますか。</span> — “Do you have an umbrella?”
- <span lang="ja">はい。ここにあります。</span> — “Yes. There is one here.”

Both lines are present within Track A. Rehearse the question and response with a pause between turns. Then imagine a lunch plan and say <span lang="ja">すしをたべます。</span> — “I'll eat sushi.” This line is in Track B. You can listen and shadow without enabling a microphone.

## Step 3 — Self-review, not a speech score

Compare what you said against the recordings. Ask: Did I say the intended word? Could I distinguish <span lang="ja">し</span> from <span lang="ja">す</span>? Did I give each short kana beat enough time? Did I pause naturally between the question and answer? Did I keep a polite, comfortable pace?
