## Chapter Can-do: decode short hiragana sentences

You can now read connected beginner hiragana without romaji, locate a question, and find an exact phrase that supports an answer. This checkpoint brings together **written kana and particles**, **independent reading**, and **real spoken comprehension**. It does not grade pronunciation, handwriting or speaking.

## Part 1 — sentence clues

Compare <span lang="ja">えほんはありますか。</span> (Do you have picture books?) with <span lang="ja">えほんをみます。</span> (I will look at picture books). The topic marker <span lang="ja">は</span> is written *ha* but pronounced *wa* when used as a particle. The object marker <span lang="ja">を</span> follows the object of an action. Polite question-final <span lang="ja">か</span> asks a question. A Japanese full stop <span lang="ja">。</span> ends a sentence; a comma <span lang="ja">、</span> separates phrases in <span lang="ja">はい、あります。</span>.

**Independent check:** Identify each particle in the two connected sentences before looking at the answer feedback. Japanese does not ordinarily insert spaces between words.

## Part 2 — read a library exchange

Read this original written conversation without romaji. It is not a recording.

| Speaker | Japanese |
| --- | --- |
| Yui | <span lang="ja">すみません。ねこのほんはありますか。</span> |
| Sora | <span lang="ja">はい、あります。</span> |
| Yui | <span lang="ja">どこですか。</span> |
| Sora | <span lang="ja">あそこです。あおいほんのとなりです。</span> |
| Yui | <span lang="ja">ありがとうございます。ねこのほんをみます。</span> |
| Sora | <span lang="ja">どうぞ。</span> |

**Word support (after your first read):** <span lang="ja">ねこ</span> cat; <span lang="ja">ほん</span> book; <span lang="ja">あそこ</span> over there; <span lang="ja">あおい</span> blue; <span lang="ja">となり</span> next to; <span lang="ja">みます</span> look at. The cat book is **next to** a blue book; the passage does not say the cat book itself is blue. It also does not say Yui buys or borrows anything.

For reading questions 5–8, identify the exact Japanese phrase that proves your answer. Distinguish explicit statements from guesses. You may revisit the passage, but answer explanations appear only after submission.
