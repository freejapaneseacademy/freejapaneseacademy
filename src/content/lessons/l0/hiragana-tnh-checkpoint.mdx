## What you can demonstrate

This checkpoint tests **twelve written and reading items** and **four audio-only comprehension items**. Written kana recognition is not listening comprehension; the two scores are recorded separately. Completion does not certify mastery or require an 80% score. Review errors and practise again later.

### Fifteen hiragana in three rows

| Row | Kana | Common romanization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T | た ち つ て と | ta · **chi** · **tsu** · te · to |
| N | な に ぬ ね の | na · ni · nu · ne · no |
| H | は ひ ふ へ ほ | ha · hi · **fu** · he · ho |

The exceptions **ち (chi)**, **つ (tsu)** and **ふ (fu)** are commonly taught with these romanizations. Compare the entire shapes **ぬ (nu)** and **ね (ne)**, and **は (ha)** and **ほ (ho)**. The extra horizontal stroke helps distinguish ほ from は.

### Original classroom label cards

- **CARD A: ぼうし — hat.** The familiar voiced ぼ appears in this word; it is not one of the fifteen unvoiced focus kana.
- **CARD B: ふね — boat.** Find ふ at the start and ね at the end.
- **CARD C: なつ — summer.** Find な at the start and つ at the end.
- **CARD D: ほし — star.** Find ほ at the start and し at the end.

Read the printed cards before the written questions. Cite the exact card word when reviewing a mistake. Individual kana represent sounds; they do not individually mean *boat*, *summer*, or *star*.

### Optional handwriting and typing

Write た・ち・つ・て・と, then な・に・ぬ・ね・の and は・ひ・ふ・へ・ほ without looking. Check against the table and rewrite **ふね**, **なつ** and **ほし** using a Japanese IME or pen. Compare legibility and distinguishing strokes yourself. There is no automatic handwriting grade.

### Genuine audio comprehension

The unchanged, licensed AivisSpeech recordings from Lesson 06 contain a **hat-shopping exchange** and a **tea conversation**. Some spoken words go beyond the fifteen focus kana. Listen to both and answer only what is audible: the requested item, selected color, offered drink and temperature. Japanese transcripts and English translations are hidden until submission; opening them early marks the result as *assisted* rather than blind listening. Printed kana are not used as the listening answer.

### Review and spaced practice

After scoring, revisit missed kana in the table or word cards. For listening errors, replay the cited spoken turn with the transcript after submitting. Review weak items tomorrow and several days later. Local progress records your attempt but does **not** automatically schedule spaced-repetition cards.
