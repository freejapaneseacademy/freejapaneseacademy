## Optional speaking: from familiar words to a connected phrase

The chapter goal is to read connected all-hiragana words with increasing accuracy. You have read word cards, listened to two short situations and typed classroom requests. Now you may **shadow** those same situations: listen, pause, repeat and compare. Speaking is optional. This activity does not grade accent, and a microphone is never required.

The model audio comes from the **unchanged original licensed AivisSpeech recordings in Lesson 06**. The two situations are fictional, and the voices are synthetic rather than people conversing live. You may read the Japanese and English lines while rehearsing: unlike the listening check, this is not a blind comprehension test.

## Track A — classroom picture cards

Listen to the woman say **きょうは、えのカードをみます。** — “Today we're going to look at picture cards.” Then listen to the question **どのカードをみますか。** — “Which card will you look at?”

Start with the short animal words **ねこ** (*cat*) and **いぬ** (*dog*). Say ね・こ and い・ぬ as two successive morae, keeping both sounds. Then rehearse these complete turns:

- **ねこのカードをみます。** — “I'll look at the cat card.”
- **いぬのカードもありますよ。** — “There's also a dog card.”
- **では、いぬのカードもみます。** — “Then I'll look at the dog card too.”

The particle **の** links the animal with its card; **も** adds “also.” The particle **を** marks what the speaker looks at, normally pronounced *o*. The phrase is not a claim that a real animal is present; the classroom objects are **picture cards**. If you see a look-alike kana such as ね versus れ or ぬ versus め, return to the written word card and then shadow the audio again.

## Track B — a library nature book

The man begins **このほんは、やまのほんです。** — “This is a book about mountains.” Repeat **やま** (*mountain*) and **ほん** (*book*). The final **ん** in ほん is a full mora. Next rehearse:

- **そらのえもありますか。** — “Are there pictures of the sky too?”
- **はい。そらとかわのえがあります。** — “Yes. There are pictures of the sky and a river.”
- **ありがとうございます。ここでよみます。** — “Thank you. I'll read it here.”

**と** connects the nouns そら (*sky*) and かわ (*river*). The speaker's **ここで** means “here” in the library scene. Read the entire expression before repeating it, not just the first kana of each word. Keep **そら** and **かわ** as two-mora words, and avoid treating **ほん** as only one beat.

## A repeatable shadowing routine

First play each whole track at normal speed to hear its conversational rhythm. Then use the turn buttons to replay a single speaker turn; pause and repeat it aloud. Try a second repetition without looking at the written text. Use the optional 0.85× speed only when useful; slowing playback can change the sound quality. Finish by playing the whole exchange and speaking one person's part during the pauses.

If audio cannot be played, use the transcript as reading practice but do not claim to have heard a model. You may optionally record your voice **only after pressing Start and granting microphone permission**. The recording stays in this browser tab, can be discarded and is not uploaded or stored in progress history. The five checkboxes are a self-review rubric, not a qualified pronunciation assessment.
