## Picture cards at the reading club

Yui and Sora are preparing a picture-card matching game. Yui reads a word card, Sora chooses its picture, and Aki checks the **small** <span lang="ja">ゃ・ゅ・ょ</span>. These are **written reading cues**, not a test of your spoken pronunciation. You will hear real recordings in Lesson 06.

A contracted combination such as <span lang="ja">きゃ</span> (*kya*) uses two written signs for **one mora** (a rhythmic beat). By contrast, <span lang="ja">きや</span> (*ki-ya*) has two full-sized kana and two morae. A small kana changes the reading, not just the appearance. The comparison <span lang="ja">きや</span> is a decoding demonstration, not a common standalone word.

## Ten word cards

Read the Japanese first. Cover the meaning column and retrieve the picture clue. Most cards are commonly written with kanji in adult Japanese; hiragana is used here for beginner decoding. The kanji notes are **optional recognition only**, not new kanji to memorize.

| Word card | Reading cue | Picture or meaning | Everyday writing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">きゃく</span> | kyaku | guest or visitor at a door | 客 |
| <span lang="ja">おちゃ</span> | ocha | tea in a cup | お茶 |
| <span lang="ja">じしょ</span> | jisho | dictionary on a desk | 辞書 |
| <span lang="ja">しゃしん</span> | shashin | photograph in an album | 写真 |
| <span lang="ja">じてんしゃ</span> | jitensha | bicycle by the gate | 自転車 |
| <span lang="ja">びょういん</span> | byouin | hospital building | 病院 |
| <span lang="ja">りょこう</span> | ryokou | trip or travel | 旅行 |
| <span lang="ja">ぎゅうにゅう</span> | gyuunyuu | milk in a carton | 牛乳 |
| <span lang="ja">きょう</span> | kyou | today on a calendar | 今日 |
| <span lang="ja">しゅくだい</span> | shukudai | homework notebook | 宿題 |

**Timing note:** <span lang="ja">びょういん</span> contains the blended <span lang="ja">びょ</span>, followed by <span lang="ja">う・い・ん</span>; the long vowel does not make the word a single beat. <span lang="ja">ぎゅうにゅう</span> contains two separate contracted pairs, <span lang="ja">ぎゅ</span> and <span lang="ja">にゅ</span>. Reading cues are simplified romanizations; they do not replace audio practice.

## Find the small kana

| Word | Contracted pair | What to notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">きゃく</span> | <span lang="ja">きゃ</span> | Small ゃ after き |
| <span lang="ja">おちゃ</span> | <span lang="ja">ちゃ</span> | Small ゃ after ち |
| <span lang="ja">じしょ</span> | <span lang="ja">しょ</span> | Small ょ after し |
| <span lang="ja">しゃしん</span> | <span lang="ja">しゃ</span> | Small ゃ after し |
| <span lang="ja">じてんしゃ</span> | <span lang="ja">しゃ</span> | Small ゃ near the end |
| <span lang="ja">びょういん</span> | <span lang="ja">びょ</span> | Small ょ after び |
| <span lang="ja">りょこう</span> | <span lang="ja">りょ</span> | Small ょ after り |
| <span lang="ja">ぎゅうにゅう</span> | <span lang="ja">ぎゅ・にゅ</span> | Two small ゅ signs |
| <span lang="ja">きょう</span> | <span lang="ja">きょ</span> | Small ょ after き |
| <span lang="ja">しゅくだい</span> | <span lang="ja">しゅ</span> | Small ゅ after し |

A common mistake is to read <span lang="ja">しゃ</span> as separate *shi-ya*. In <span lang="ja">しゃしん</span> the first pair is one blended mora, not two. In a full-sized contrast such as <span lang="ja">しや</span>, the two signs would be read separately. Do not treat these invented comparison strings as everyday words.

## A useful classroom exchange

Aki says <span lang="ja">しゃしんをみてください。</span> — “Please look at the photograph.” Yui asks <span lang="ja">じしょはどれですか。</span> — “Which one is the dictionary?” Sora points to the book. The first is a polite request with <span lang="ja">を</span> marking the object; the second asks which item matches the topic <span lang="ja">じしょ</span>. The particle <span lang="ja">は</span> is pronounced *wa* here. Both examples are short classroom expressions, not claims about the sound of an unseen recording.

**Controlled practice:** Point to <span lang="ja">ちゃ</span> in <span lang="ja">おちゃ</span> and <span lang="ja">しょ</span> in <span lang="ja">じしょ</span>. **Guided practice:** Cover the English meanings and find the bicycle, photograph, milk and hospital. **Independent recall:** Read the two classroom lines, then select the correct word card for each clue without looking at the table.
