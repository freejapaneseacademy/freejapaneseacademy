## The reading club's sound-family board

At the reading club, Yui and Sora hang up a board so beginners can find the correct written labels. **Read the row and column headers before answering.** The chart is a *written* reference, not a pronunciation recording.

| Base kana | small ゃ (a) | small ゅ (u) | small ょ (o) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **き** | **きゃ** | **きゅ** | **きょ** |
| **し** | **しゃ** | **しゅ** | **しょ** |
| **ち** | **ちゃ** | **ちゅ** | **ちょ** |
| **に** | **にゃ** | **にゅ** | **にょ** |
| **ひ** | **ひゃ** | **ひゅ** | **ひょ** |
| **み** | **みゃ** | **みゅ** | **みょ** |
| **り** | **りゃ** | **りゅ** | **りょ** |
| **ぎ** | **ぎゃ** | **ぎゅ** | **ぎょ** |
| **じ** | **じゃ** | **じゅ** | **じょ** |
| **び** | **びゃ** | **びゅ** | **びょ** |
| **ぴ** | **ぴゃ** | **ぴゅ** | **ぴょ** |

Each chart cell has a base kana followed by **small** ゃ, ゅ or ょ. In the standard beginner pattern, the contracted pair counts as **one mora**. Compare **きゃ** (one mora) with **きや** (two full-sized kana and two morae). **きや** here is only a constructed spelling contrast, not a vocabulary word. The chart records common combinations, not every possible Japanese syllable.

## Labels on the club's table

| Card | Written label | English meaning | Evidence to point to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | **おちゃ** | tea | **ちゃ** is in the **ち** row, small **ゃ** column |
| B | **しゃしん** | photograph | **しゃ** is in the **し** row, small **ゃ** column |
| C | **じしょ** | dictionary | **しょ** is in the **し** row, small **ょ** column |
| D | **きゃく** | guest | **きゃ** is in the **き** row, small **ゃ** column |
| E | **しゅくだい** | homework | **しゅ** is in the **し** row, small **ゅ** column |
| F | **りょこう** | trip | **りょ** is in the **り** row, small **ょ** column |
| G | **ぎゅうにゅう** | milk | **ぎゅ** and **にゅ** appear in two different rows, small **ゅ** column |
| H | **びょういん** | hospital | **びょ** is in the **び** row, small **ょ** column |

The label **おちゃ** divides into **お・ちゃ** (two morae). **しゃしん** divides into **しゃ・し・ん** (three morae), including **ん** as one mora. **じしょ** divides into **じ・しょ** (two morae). **りょこう** divides into **りょ・こ・う** (three morae). **ぎゅうにゅう** divides into **ぎゅ・う・にゅ・う** (four morae). A written **う** after a contracted pair is a *separate mora*, even when it lengthens the preceding vowel.

## Find the evidence, not a guess

**Controlled practice:** Find the intersection of row **し** and column small **ゅ**. It reads **しゅ**. Cite the row and column, not only a romaji guess.

**Guided practice:** The club needs the word for a dictionary. Find **じしょ** on card C. Point to the **し** row and small **ょ** column for its final **しょ**. The initial **じ** is a separate mora.

**Independent practice:** Without looking at the answers, find the card for milk, then count its morae. Check against card G and the written division **ぎゅ・う・にゅ・う**. If you chose four, you used both contracted pairs and both separate **う** characters.

A useful sentence on the table is **しゃしんをみてください。** — “Please look at the photograph.” **しゃしん** means “photograph,” **を** marks its object role, and **みてください** is a polite request. The sound-family chart helps decode **しゃ** within the word; it does not itself teach pronunciation.
