## Make a reading-club card

A fictional reading club is making labels for a picture table. You will write the words, not merely choose their printed forms. The small **ゃ**, **ゅ** and **ょ** follow an *i*-row kana to form a contracted sound: **きゃ** (*kya*), **しゅ** (*shu*) and **ちょ** (*cho*). The full-sized forms **きや**, **しゆ** and **ちよ** are two separate kana and are **not** interchangeable spellings.

## Step 1 — Observe and type

| Meaning or sound | Hiragana model | Common romaji IME input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kya | **きゃ** | kya |
| shu | **しゅ** | shu |
| cho | **ちょ** | cho |
| tea | **おちゃ** | ocha |
| dictionary | **じしょ** | jisho |
| photograph | **しゃしん** | shashin |
| milk | **ぎゅうにゅう** | gyuunyuu |
| together | **いっしょ** | issho |

In **ぎゅうにゅう**, both **ぎゅ** and **にゅ** contain small ゅ. In **いっしょ**, small **っ** marks a consonant pause and **しょ** contains small ょ. Japanese IME layouts vary; use the converted **hiragana** as your final answer, not unconverted Latin letters. Check that the small kana stays small after conversion.

## Step 2 — Build a supported message

For the club's fictional table, a short label is **おちゃが あります。** (*There is tea.*). The particle **が** marks the thing that exists. A polite invitation is **いっしょに よみましょう。** (*Let's read together.*). Here **いっしょに** means *together*; **よみましょう** is a polite invitation to read. Spaces are provided as beginner-friendly word boundaries; ordinary Japanese writing often omits them.

Cover the models and type the eight constrained responses below. The spelling checks accept surrounding whitespace and canonically equivalent Unicode forms, but they do not grade creative prose. For the two sentence items, use the punctuation and spacing shown in the models.

## Step 3 — Compose, compare and revise

Write your own two-line invitation for a fictional reading club. You may use this model:

**おちゃが あります。**

**いっしょに よみましょう。**

Compare your draft against four criteria: (1) contracted kana are small where required, (2) words are complete, (3) particles **が** and **に** express the intended meaning, and (4) the invitation ends with **よみましょう。** You can revise freely; the checklist is *self-review*, not teacher or machine grading.
