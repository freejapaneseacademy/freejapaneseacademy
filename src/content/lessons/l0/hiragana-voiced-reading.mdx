## The picture-card sorting board

Mika and Ren have finished printing the cards. Before the class sorts them, they check the **written contrast board**. The chart is original classroom reading material. Read the printed characters carefully; this is **not** an audio or pronunciation test.

| Board row | Base kana | With two strokes (dakuten) | With a circle (handakuten) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | **か** | **が** | — |
| B | **き** | **ぎ** | — |
| C | **す** | **ず** | — |
| D | **た** | **だ** | — |
| E | **は** | **ば** | **ぱ** |
| F | **ひ** | **び** | **ぴ** |
| G | **ふ** | **ぶ** | **ぷ** |
| H | **ほ** | **ぼ** | **ぽ** |

The **two strokes** change か to が and は to ば. The **circle** changes は to ぱ. On rows E–H, both marked columns are possible, but they produce different written kana. A dash means the board does not list a circle-marked counterpart in that row; it is not a missing answer to guess.

## Labels below the board

Each picture-card label is written in hiragana. The English descriptions tell you what the classroom picture shows; they are not printed on the Japanese card.

| Card | Japanese label | Picture description | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **かぎ** | key | **ぎ** has two strokes |
| 2 | **みず** | water | **ず** has two strokes |
| 3 | **ぶた** | pig | **ぶ** has two strokes |
| 4 | **ともだち** | friend | **だ** has two strokes |
| 5 | **ぱん** | bread | **ぱ** has a circle |
| 6 | **えんぴつ** | pencil | **ぴ** has a circle |
| 7 | **てがみ** | letter | **が** has two strokes |
| 8 | **かばん** | bag | **ば** has two strokes |

**Spelling note:** Bread is usually written **パン** in ordinary Japanese; **ぱん** is deliberately in hiragana for this script practice. Do not infer a new pronunciation from the Latin reading cues. **みず** uses ず, not づ, in standard spelling.

## Three classroom reading clues

Mika writes: **かぎは １ばん。** — The key is number one. Here **は** is the topic particle; the space is a learning aid. Ren writes: **ぱんは ５ばん。** — The bread is number five. The third note says **えんぴつは ６ばん。** — The pencil is number six. These are short written labels in a classroom sorting context, not an invitation to change standard word spellings.

The key's number is supported by **かぎは １ばん。**, not merely by a guess about the picture. The bread's number is supported by **ぱんは ５ばん。**. When a question asks for evidence, find and quote the exact Japanese text, including the marked kana.

## Guided reading

First cover the English picture column and match **かぎ**, **みず**, **ぶた**, **ともだち**, **ぱん**, **えんぴつ**, **てがみ**, and **かばん** to their pictures. Next find **は / ば / ぱ** in row E and explain which symbol has no mark, two strokes, or a circle. Finally read the three numbered notes and underline the exact phrase proving each card number.

## Independent reading check

Answer ten original written comprehension questions. Each explanation quotes the supporting board row, label or numbered note. Re-read the evidence before submitting; there is no timer. Your result is a local written-reading score, **not** a listening, pronunciation or official JLPT score. Paired sound recordings and genuine listening discrimination belong to Lesson 06.
