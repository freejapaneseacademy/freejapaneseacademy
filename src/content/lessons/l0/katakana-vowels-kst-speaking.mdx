## At the kiosk: listen, pause, repeat

**Can-do:** Read and write katakana vowel, K, S and T rows. This optional speaking extension lets you rehearse the same symbols aloud while looking at a kiosk label. The source recordings are the two original Chapter 01 L06 AivisSpeech WAVs, **not** browser text-to-speech. They must be generated and checksum-verified before this lesson can publish. Synthetic model audio is a practice reference, not a human pronunciation assessment.

Imagine that you are helping a friend read a snack and stationery display. Your friend points to **ココア**, **カカオ**, **アイス** and **テキスト**. You can read each label slowly and identify its sounds.

## Sound map: read each written mora

| Vowels | K row | S row | T row |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ア a | カ ka | サ sa | タ ta |
| イ i | キ ki | シ shi | チ chi |
| ウ u | ク ku | ス su | ツ tsu |
| エ e | ケ ke | セ se | テ te |
| オ o | コ ko | ソ so | ト to |

A *mora* is a timing unit in Japanese. For these simple syllables, use one even beat per kana. **ココア** has three kana beats (*ko-ko-a*), **カカオ** three (*ka-ka-o*), **アイス** three (*a-i-su*), and **テキスト** four (*te-ki-su-to*). Do not stretch one beat into two. These written examples are short rehearsal prompts; check the actual L06 recording before claiming you heard any particular word.

## How to shadow without rushing

1. Play scene A once without reading. Notice pauses and repeated sounds; don't guess unseen words from the printed labels.
2. Play again with the printed map. Pause after a short segment and repeat only what you actually heard. Do not claim to have heard a sample if it is absent from the recording.
3. Replay scene B and practise the sounds in the recording. You may separately read the written kiosk labels aloud as *additional unrecorded prompts*.
4. Compare **シ** (*shi*) and **ツ** (*tsu*) visually. They are different syllables, not interchangeable romanization.
5. Repeat at a comfortable volume. You may stop at any time. Recording your own voice is **not required**, and the site does not automatically record or grade your pronunciation.

## Survival expression: point and read

Point to **ココア** and say its three beats slowly: *ko-ko-a*. Point to **アイス** and say *a-i-su*. These are **label readings**, not a complete Japanese request sentence. To ask for an item politely you would need additional language beyond this beginner sound focus.
