## A label-maker with two tiny marks

Mika is preparing picture cards for a classroom sorting game. Ren checks the characters on each card before printing. The upper-right **two short strokes** (dakuten, ゛) and the **small circle** (handakuten, ゜) are part of the kana. The mark changes the written sound; it is not an extra mora or a separate word.

| Base hiragana | With dakuten | Reading cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| か き く け こ | が ぎ ぐ げ ご | ga gi gu ge go |
| さ し す せ そ | ざ じ ず ぜ ぞ | za ji zu ze zo |
| た ち つ て と | だ ぢ づ で ど | da ji zu de do |
| は ひ ふ へ ほ | ば び ぶ べ ぼ | ba bi bu be bo |
| は ひ ふ へ ほ | ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ | pa pi pu pe po |

Look closely at **は → ば** (two strokes) and **は → ぱ** (circle). They are distinct characters. Similarly, **ほ → ぼ** and **ほ → ぽ** must not be swapped. These marks are small, so zoom the screen if necessary; never rely on colour alone.

## Read the picture-card labels

| Printed card | Meaning | What to notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **かぎ** | key | ぎ is き with dakuten |
| **みず** | water | ず is す with dakuten |
| **ぶた** | pig | ぶ is ふ with dakuten |
| **ともだち** | friend | だ is た with dakuten |
| **ぱん** | bread | ぱ has a circle; **パン** is the usual katakana spelling |
| **えんぴつ** | pencil | ぴ is ひ with a circle |
| **てがみ** | letter | が is か with dakuten |
| **かばん** | bag | ば is は with dakuten |

These are written practice labels, not pronunciation recordings. A word can be recognizable in hiragana even when its usual real-world spelling differs. In particular, **ぱん** is a hiragana practice spelling of **パン**.

## Type the kana, then inspect the result

On a Japanese IME, romaji input commonly produces **ga → が**, **za → ざ**, **da → だ**, **ba → ば**, **pa → ぱ**, **gi → ぎ**, and **pi → ぴ**. Check the **actual Japanese character**, not just the Latin letters. Depending on your keyboard, input mode and operating system, conversion steps vary.

Try typing **かぎ**, **みず**, **ぶた**, **ぱん**, and **えんぴつ** in a scratch field or notebook. If you cannot enable an IME, select the correct kana in the written check below; that option is equally accessible for completing this lesson. For optional handwriting, trace **が**, **ば**, **ぱ**, and **ぴ** on paper, keeping each mark at the upper right. Tracing is self-directed and is **not** automatically scored.

## A spelling caution

In standard contemporary Japanese, **じ / ぢ** and **ず / づ** often share pronunciations, but their conventional spellings are not freely interchangeable. **つづく** (“continue”) uses **づ**, while **みず** (“water”) uses **ず**. Look at the written word rather than choosing a character from a sound cue alone.
