## Your optional shop rehearsal

**Chapter Can-do:** Complete basic katakana and voiced sound families. Imagine visiting a bakery and then asking about a bag in a gift shop. The clerk and visitor are fictional. The **two real model tracks** are those authored for Lesson 06; this speaking lesson must remain unpublished until that lesson's original AivisSpeech WAVs, manifest, SHA-256 hashes and redistribution rights have been verified. No browser speech synthesis is used.

You can complete this lesson **without using a microphone**. Listen and speak privately, or silently rehearse by following the printed turns. The practice does not judge accent or speech quality.

## Read before you speak

| Model phrase | Meaning | What to notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">パンをふたつください。</span> | Two breads, please. | パ has a circle (handakuten), not the two strokes of バ. |
| <span lang="ja">ジュースもありますよ。</span> | We also have juice. | ジ carries dakuten; ュ is small. |
| <span lang="ja">このバッグはだれのですか。</span> | Whose bag is this? | バッグ has バ, voiced by two small strokes. |
| <span lang="ja">ミナさんのバッグです。</span> | It is Mina's bag. | ン is the nasal symbol; do not substitute ソ. |
| <span lang="ja">プレゼントですか。</span> | Is it a present? | The final か marks a question. |

The shop phrases mix hiragana grammar with katakana product names. In **パン**, the circle is part of パ; in **バッグ**, the two marks are part of バ. These are not interchangeable. The word プレゼント contains ン, not ソ. The Japanese /r/ row is not simply the English R sound; avoid assuming a one-to-one English pronunciation.

## Model A — bakery counter

1. Clerk: <span lang="ja">いらっしゃいませ。パンはいかがですか。</span> — Welcome. Would you like some bread?
2. Visitor: <span lang="ja">はい。パンをふたつください。</span> — Yes. Two breads, please.
3. Clerk: <span lang="ja">はい。ジュースもありますよ。</span> — Certainly. We also have juice.
4. Visitor: <span lang="ja">では、ジュースもひとつください。</span> — Then one juice too, please.

Play the full model. Replay and pause after each turn. Speak the visitor's request, then swap roles. Keep **ふたつ** (two) and **ひとつ** (one) distinct.

## Model B — gift shop

1. Visitor: <span lang="ja">このバッグはだれのですか。</span> — Whose bag is this?
2. Clerk: <span lang="ja">それはミナさんのバッグです。</span> — That is Mina's bag.
3. Visitor: <span lang="ja">ああ。プレゼントですか。</span> — Oh. Is it a present?
4. Clerk: <span lang="ja">はい。ミナさんへのプレゼントです。</span> — Yes. It is a present for Mina.

Play the model and shadow one role, then both. Keep the question ending **か** audible. **ミナ** is a fictional name; you do not need to reveal your own name or voice.

## Self-review without automated voice grading

After shadowing, compare your rehearsal with the model: (1) Did you say **パ** in パン and **バ** in バッグ distinctly? (2) Did you keep **ン** in プレゼント? (3) Did you keep the request **ください** and question **ですか** complete? (4) Did you pause at sentence boundaries? Tick observations in the interactive practice. These checkboxes are for reflection only, **not a verified pronunciation score**.

If you explicitly start a microphone recording, it stays only in this browser tab as a temporary blob URL, is never uploaded, and can be discarded. You can also rehearse without recording. The six scored items below check written meaning and sound-mark knowledge, not speaking quality. Local attempts are not JLPT grades.
