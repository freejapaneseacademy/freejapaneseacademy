## Optional speaking: a visit to the library

**Chapter Can-do:** Decode short hiragana sentences without relying on romaji. Now use those sentences in an optional spoken exchange. This is a **rehearsal**, not a pronunciation certification. The model recordings belong to Chapter 04 Lesson 06; this lesson must not be published until those genuine AivisSpeech WAV files, rights records and manifest have passed the release checks.

### First, understand what you will say

At the library desk, a visitor asks for a book. The librarian points to a shelf. In the second conversation, a visitor asks to borrow a book and is asked to write a name. You can rehearse either role. Listen to each complete model, then repeat one speaker's turns at a time. Do not record unless you explicitly choose to.

| Useful Japanese | Meaning and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">ほんをさがしていますか。</span> | Are you looking for a book? A polite question. |
| <span lang="ja">ねこのほんはありますか。</span> | Do you have a book about cats? The topic は is written は, pronounced *wa*. |
| <span lang="ja">あのたなにあります。</span> | It is on that shelf. に marks the location of existence. |
| <span lang="ja">このほんをかりてもいいですか。</span> | May I borrow this book? を marks the object. |
| <span lang="ja">なまえをかいてください。</span> | Please write your name. A polite request. |
| <span lang="ja">ここにかきますか。</span> | Shall I write it here? か signals a question. |

**Sentence boundaries matter:** Pause briefly at <span lang="ja">。</span>, but do not pronounce the punctuation as a word. Japanese is written without teaching spaces; listen for the natural grouping of a whole phrase rather than separating every kana. You are not required to read romaji.

### Guided shadowing

1. Play **Track A** at normal speed. Follow the scene, not individual letters. Then play each turn, pause and repeat the Japanese. Say the librarian's question <span lang="ja">ほんをさがしていますか。</span> with a natural questioning rhythm.
2. Switch roles. Repeat the visitor's <span lang="ja">ねこのほんはありますか。</span> and the librarian's answer <span lang="ja">あのたなにあります。</span>. Notice that <span lang="ja">あります</span> is a statement here, while <span lang="ja">ありますか</span> asks a question.
3. Play **Track B**. Practise <span lang="ja">このほんをかりてもいいですか。</span> as a permission request, not an announcement that borrowing has already happened. Reply with <span lang="ja">はい。なまえをかいてください。</span>.
4. Shadow a complete exchange without reading English. Optionally make a private recording, replay it and compare rhythm and clarity against the real model. You may stop or discard it at any time.

### Common misunderstandings

- <span lang="ja">ほんをさがしていますか。</span> is **Are you looking for a book?**, not “I am looking for a book.” The ending <span lang="ja">か</span> changes its function.
- <span lang="ja">このほんをかりてもいいですか。</span> **asks permission**; it does not mean the book has already been borrowed.
- <span lang="ja">なまえをかいてください。</span> requests writing a name, not reading it aloud. Never type your real name or other personal information into this practice.

### Self-review, not grading

Check whether you made the question endings audible, kept particles attached to their phrases, paused at sentence boundaries and completed a polite exchange. Your observations are not a native-speaker evaluation. The optional knowledge check tests **meaning and conversational function**, not voice quality. Completion never requires a microphone, a knowledge score or disclosure of personal data.
