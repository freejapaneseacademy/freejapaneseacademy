## What you can demonstrate

This checkpoint revisits **fifteen hiragana** from the vowel, K and S rows. A printed kana can be matched to its sound, but a written symbol quiz is **not** a listening test. You will complete twelve written and reading questions, then four audio-only meaning questions. The two scores are saved separately in your browser.

### Review the three rows

| Row | Five kana | Common readings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vowels | あ　い　う　え　お | a · i · u · e · o |
| K | か　き　く　け　こ | ka · ki · ku · ke · ko |
| S | さ　し　す　せ　そ | sa · **shi** · su · se · so |

**Common trap:** し is *shi*, not *si* in the usual Hepburn-style teaching transcription. Do not assume every consonant row is mechanically regular. Kana are sound symbols, not words by themselves.

### Original reading cards: the classroom table

Read these three fictional word cards. For each written question, locate the **exact kana** that supports your answer.

- **CARD A: かさ | umbrella.** Two symbols: か (ka) and さ (sa).
- **CARD B: すし | sushi.** Two symbols: す (su) and し (shi).
- **CARD C: ここ | here.** The same こ (ko) appears twice.

The cards are a simplified practice grid, not complete conversational Japanese. Do not mistake a word's meaning for the meaning of each individual kana. Other familiar words include あお (blue), いえ (house) and かお (face). All are written with already studied characters.

### Guided recall before scoring

Without looking above, write the vowel row and then the K and S rows on paper. Compare against the model. Check that each character is recognizable, in the correct order, and that し is paired with *shi*. If you use a Japanese keyboard, confirm your IME has produced kana rather than Latin letters. Handwriting is **self-assessed**; this page does not pretend to recognize pen strokes or grade free composition.

### Listening without printed hints

The two **unchanged, licensed AivisSpeech WAVs from Lesson 06** contain an umbrella request and a lunch conversation. Their Japanese scripts include some additional expressions beyond these fifteen kana. You are being assessed on the **meaning of spoken requests and replies**, not asked to choose a printed kana by ear. Listen through both recordings first. Transcripts are hidden until submission; revealing them early marks the attempt as *assisted*.

### After your attempt: error review and spaced practice

Check each rationale. If you confused し and す, practise that pair on two later days. If you missed かさ or すし, revisit the printed cards, then test yourself from memory tomorrow and several days later. For a listening error, replay the cited turn after revealing the transcript. Keep a short personal review list; this checkpoint records local quiz attempts, **not** a claim that an automatic spaced-repetition scheduler has added cards.

Write one new two-kana word using the studied set, such as かお (face), and compare every symbol with the chart. This free production is for **self-review**, not a scored writing grade. You can complete the chapter without microphone access.
