## Can-do: recognize and rehearse a first Japanese sentence

At a study table, you can ask what an object is, identify it, and ask whether a person has a particular role. This **optional** speaking lesson uses the original L06 question-and-answer dialogues. The scored questions below check **written comprehension**, not pronunciation. You can complete the lesson without using a microphone.

### 1. First listen, then take a role

**Track A — At the study table.** A learner points to something near them and asks <span lang="ja">これは、なんですか。</span> (“What is this?”). Their partner answers <span lang="ja">それは、ほんです。</span> (“That is a book.”). The difference between **これ** (“this near me”) and **それ** (“that near you”) reflects the speakers' viewpoints, not a change in object.

Next, the learner asks <span lang="ja">たなかさんは、せんせいですか。</span> (“Is Tanaka a teacher?”). The reply is <span lang="ja">はい。たなかさんは、せんせいです。</span> (“Yes. Tanaka is a teacher.”). Notice how the question ends in **か**, while the answer does not.

Listen once without reading the turns below. Then pause after each question and answer aloud as the other speaker. Switch roles and ask the questions yourself.

### 2. Correct a mistaken guess politely

**Track B — At the classroom door.** A visitor asks <span lang="ja">すみません。これは、ノートですか。</span> (“Excuse me. Is this a notebook?”). The reply is <span lang="ja">はい。ノートです。</span> (“Yes, it is a notebook.”). The answer leaves out “this” because it is already clear.

The visitor then asks <span lang="ja">あのひとは、がくせいですか。</span> (“Is that person a student?”). The reply is <span lang="ja">いいえ。あのひとは、せんせいです。</span> (“No. That person is a teacher.”). **いいえ** rejects the proposed identification; the following statement supplies the correction.

Rehearse the exchange in two roles. As a controlled variation, point to a *fictional* pen and say <span lang="ja">これは、ペンですか。</span> (“Is this a pen?”). Your partner may reply <span lang="ja">はい。ペンです。</span> (“Yes, it is a pen.”). Do not substitute a personal name or disclose personal information.

### 3. Pronunciation and spelling reminders

The particle **は** in <span lang="ja">たなかさんは</span> is pronounced **wa**, even though the kana normally represents *ha*. The particle **を**, previewed earlier, is usually pronounced *o*; it does **not** occur in these two dialogues. Avoid inserting を into the noun-identification frame **AはBです**. Pause briefly at turn boundaries, but do not assume written commas represent a fixed timing in speech.

### 4. Review your own rehearsal

Use the optional recording controls only if you want to compare your own voice. The recording remains in this browser tab and can be discarded. Review whether you (1) asked a question with **か**, (2) answered with **はい** or **いいえ** appropriately, (3) used the topic particle **は** as *wa*, and (4) left time for the other speaker. This is a self-check, not a pronunciation score or independent teacher evaluation.

If the genuine L06 WAVs are missing, use the written dialogues as reading practice only; do **not** count that as audio shadowing. Return to L06 for listening and L07 for writing. This L08 is optional and is not required for the checkpoint.
