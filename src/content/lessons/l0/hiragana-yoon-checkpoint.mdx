## Chapter Can-do: recognize and produce contracted sounds

In **きゃ**, **しゅ** and **ちょ**, the second kana is a **small** ゃ, ゅ or ょ. Each pair forms **one mora**. Compare **きゃ** (one mora) with the constructed spelling contrast **きや** (two morae). The latter is not a vocabulary item here. In **ぎゅうにゅう** (milk), count **ぎゅ・う・にゅ・う**: four morae, because each following **う** adds a mora.

### Reading evidence: the reading club chart

| Base row | Small ゃ | Small ゅ | Small ょ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| き | きゃ | きゅ | きょ |
| し | しゃ | しゅ | しょ |
| ち | ちゃ | ちゅ | ちょ |
| り | りゃ | りゅ | りょ |
| ぎ | ぎゃ | ぎゅ | ぎょ |
| に | にゃ | にゅ | にょ |

The original Lesson 05 reading-club cards include **おちゃ** (tea), **しゃしん** (photograph), **じしょ** (dictionary), **りょこう** (trip), and **ぎゅうにゅう** (milk). Find the chart row and small-kana column before choosing. For example, **りょこう** begins with **りょ**, row **り**, small **ょ** column. **おちゃ** divides into **お・ちゃ** (two morae).

### Writing and typing self-check

Type or write **きゃ・しゅ・ちょ**, then **じしょ・おちゃ・ぎゅうにゅう**. Compare your work with the models. Check small-kana size and placement; do not confuse **しゅ** with **しゆ**. This optional writing check is ungraded: the academy does not claim to evaluate handwriting automatically.
