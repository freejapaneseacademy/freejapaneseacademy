## Chapter Can-do: read small っ and long vowels accurately

A small **っ** marks a held consonant before the following consonant, and counts as **one mora**. It is not pronounced as an ordinary full-sized **つ**. Compare **きて** (き・て, two morae) with **きって** (き・っ・て, three morae). **きて** is a request form of “come”; **きって** means “postage stamp.” The checkpoint tests both visual recognition and evidence-based interpretation, not an automatic pronunciation judgment.

## Read the library's paired labels

| Pair | Printed labels | What the writing shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | **きて** / **きって** | Small **っ** appears only in the second |
| B | **つき** / **きっぷ** | Full-sized **つ** versus small **っ** |
| C | **さし** / **ざっし** | Different words; **ざっし** has voiced ざ and small **っ** |
| D | **こうえん** / **おおきい** | Written **おう** versus **おお** |
| E | **とけい** / **せんせい** | Both have written **えい** |
| F | **とおい** / **がっこう** | **おお** versus **っ** and **こう** |
| G | **ここ** / **こうこう** | Two **こう** sequences in the second |

The labels are a **written reading task**, not an audio transcription. Common long-*o* spellings **おう** and **おお** are word-specific, not freely interchangeable. The written **えい** in **とけい** and **せんせい** often corresponds to a long *e* in ordinary speech; identify the spelling rather than assuming that every sound uniquely determines its letters.

For a second evidence check, read **きってをみてください。** (“Please look at the postage stamp.”) and **こうえんはとおいです。** (“The park is far.”). The first sentence contains the object **きって** before **を**; the second describes **こうえん** using **とおい**. In **がっこう**, count **が・っ・こ・う** (four morae), and in **こうえん**, count **こ・う・え・ん** (four morae).
