## Reading mission: the fictional みどり駅カフェ

**Can-do:** Read everyday loanwords, long-vowel bars and borrowed-sound combinations in a simple menu. Find an exact printed phrase before answering a question. This is a written reading lesson; spoken announcement listening is in L06.

| メニュー (menu) | ねだん (price) |
| --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">コーヒー</span> | 400円 |
| <span lang="ja">ティー</span> | 300円 |
| <span lang="ja">ケーキ</span> | 350円 |
| <span lang="ja">サンドイッチ</span> | 450円 |
| <span lang="ja">セット：コーヒー＋ケーキ</span> | 650円 |

These are original fictional prices. 円 (えん) is the yen unit. The set includes coffee and cake: **＋** connects the two printed words. Buying them separately costs 750円; the displayed set costs 650円.

## Read the signs beside the menu

| Original station-area sign | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">チケット 500円</span> | Ticket: 500 yen |
| <span lang="ja">タクシー → 東口</span> | Taxi toward the east exit |
| <span lang="ja">ファイル 200円</span> | File: 200 yen |
| <span lang="ja">チェーン店ではありません</span> | This is not a chain store |

**東口** (ひがしぐち) means east exit. You need only recognize its meaning here; you are not being tested on kanji writing. The arrow shows direction, not a price. The final negative sentence is provided as reading support, not a new grammar target.

## Spot the spelling evidence

- **ー** lengthens the preceding vowel in コーヒー, ティー, ケーキ, タクシー and チェーン. コーヒー has two long-vowel marks.
- **Small ィ** combines with テ in ティー; **small ァ** combines with フ in ファイル; **small ェ** combines with チ in チェーン.
- **Small ッ** in サンドイッチ marks a consonant closure. It is not a long-vowel bar.
- A number followed by 円 gives a price. Compare the number on the **same row** as the item.

## Worked evidence answer

**Question:** Which item costs 350 yen?

**Answer:** Cake.

**Printed evidence:** <span lang="ja">ケーキ 350円</span>.

**Reason:** ケーキ names cake and is paired directly with 350円. A plausible item elsewhere on the menu is not evidence for this price.

## Guided reading and self-check

First locate the requested noun, then scan its row for the price or symbol. To compare coffee and tea, read **コーヒー 400円** and **ティー 300円** together: tea costs 100 yen less. To identify a direction, look for **タクシー → 東口**, not a food row.
