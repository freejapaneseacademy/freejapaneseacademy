## Can-do: read everyday borrowed spellings accurately

You are reading an **original fictional station café menu** before catching a train. The menu shows **コーヒー** (coffee), **ケーキ** (cake) and **ティー** (tea). A station sign says **タクシー** (taxi). You can read these labels more reliably when you notice vowel length and the *size* of each kana. This lesson assesses **written interpretation**, not listening accuracy.

## 1. The long-vowel mark ー

The mark **ー** is called the *chōonpu* (長音符). In ordinary horizontal katakana, it extends the **preceding vowel** by one mora; it is not a hyphen or an English letter. The preceding kana determines the vowel being extended.

| Written word | Reading cue | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">コーヒー</span> | kōhī | coffee |
| <span lang="ja">ケーキ</span> | kēki | cake |
| <span lang="ja">タクシー</span> | takushī | taxi |
| <span lang="ja">チーム</span> | chīmu | team |

Compare **タクシー** with **タクシ**: removing ー changes the written vowel length and is not the conventional spelling of *taxi*. The symbol does not always represent the same vowel: **コー** extends /o/, **ヒー** extends /i/.

## 2. Small kana represent borrowed sound combinations

A **small** vowel after a full-size kana can create a single borrowed-sound unit. The small character is not interchangeable with its full-size counterpart.

| Combination | Structure | Reading cue | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">ティ</span> | テ + small ィ | ti | <span lang="ja">パーティー</span> (party) |
| <span lang="ja">ファ</span> | フ + small ァ | fa | <span lang="ja">ファイル</span> (file) |
| <span lang="ja">チェ</span> | チ + small ェ | che | <span lang="ja">チェス</span> (chess) |

**ティ ≠ テイ**, **ファ ≠ フア**, and **チェ ≠ チエ**. A full-size vowel is a separate kana, not the small-vowel combination used here. Japanese borrowed words are adapted to Japanese phonology; these spelling cues are not guarantees of identical English pronunciation.

## 3. Spelling conventions and a common mistake

In **パーティー**, the small ィ forms **ティ**, and the final ー lengthens its vowel. In **ファッション** (fashion), small ァ forms **ファ**, while small ッ marks a held consonant before ショ. Do not confuse small ッ (a consonant timing marker) with ー (vowel length). Many loanwords have established conventional spellings: consult the whole word, rather than mechanically transliterating every English letter.

**Common misuse:** writing **パーテイー** with full-size イ when you mean パーティー. On a Japanese keyboard, select the intended katakana conversion and check whether ィ is small.

## 4. Guided café and station reading

Original printed scene:

> **駅のカフェ (station café)**  
> コーヒー / ケーキ / ティー  
> **駅前 (in front of the station)**  
> タクシー

The menu establishes that coffee, cake and tea are **listed**. It does **not** establish prices, purchases or availability. The station label indicates taxis; it does not state a departure time.

Try these without scoring: (1) point to each ー in コーヒー; (2) underline small ィ in ティー; (3) explain why ファ begins with a combination rather than two full-size kana; (4) type パーティー using Japanese IME, checking the small ィ.
