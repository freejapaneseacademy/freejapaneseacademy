## Read a small station kiosk card

**Chapter Can-do:** Read and write katakana vowels and the K, S and T rows. In this lesson, you will read a short, **original fictional kiosk display** and point to the exact printed evidence for your answers. This is a written reading task, not a listening test.

A kiosk at a station has three sections. The headings are shown in **hiragana** to help you orient yourself; the product labels use katakana already introduced in this chapter.

### Kiosk card — read before answering

| Section heading | Product labels |
| --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">のみもの</span> — drinks | <span lang="ja">ココア</span> — cocoa; <span lang="ja">カカオ</span> — cacao |
| <span lang="ja">つめたいもの</span> — cold treats | <span lang="ja">アイス</span> — ice cream in this context |
| <span lang="ja">べんきょうのもの</span> — study supplies | <span lang="ja">テキスト</span> — textbook / text |

The headings are learner-friendly descriptions rather than a claim about wording on a real Japanese shop sign. No prices, availability or brands are shown.

## How to read with evidence

1. **Find the section.** For a drink, look below <span lang="ja">のみもの</span> (*nomimono*, drinks).
2. **Match the whole label.** <span lang="ja">ココア</span> (*kokoa*, cocoa) and <span lang="ja">カカオ</span> (*kakao*, cacao) differ at the first two characters. Do not guess from the shared final <span lang="ja">オ</span>.
3. **Check the final character.** <span lang="ja">アイス</span> (*aisu*) ends with <span lang="ja">ス</span> (*su*); <span lang="ja">テキスト</span> (*tekisuto*) ends with <span lang="ja">ト</span> (*to*).
4. **Quote the evidence.** “The drinks section prints <span lang="ja">ココア</span>” is supported. “All drinks cost the same” is not: the card shows **no prices**.

### Character spotlight

| Katakana | Sound cue | In the card |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">コ</span> | *ko* | <span lang="ja">ココア</span>, twice |
| <span lang="ja">カ</span> | *ka* | <span lang="ja">カカオ</span>, twice |
| <span lang="ja">ス</span> | *su* | <span lang="ja">アイス</span>, <span lang="ja">テキスト</span> |
| <span lang="ja">ト</span> | *to* | <span lang="ja">テキスト</span> |

The two written shapes <span lang="ja">コ</span> and <span lang="ja">カ</span> do not mean the same thing. Compare their strokes, not just the English-looking word.

## Guided reading: show your evidence

Try these before the graded questions. Which heading contains <span lang="ja">テキスト</span>? It is <span lang="ja">べんきょうのもの</span>, the study-supplies heading. What does the card tell you about the price of <span lang="ja">アイス</span>? **Nothing**: the card does not print prices.

For independent practice, cover the English glosses, locate <span lang="ja">ココア</span>, <span lang="ja">カカオ</span>, <span lang="ja">アイス</span> and <span lang="ja">テキスト</span>, then uncover the meanings. Re-read the section heading to check each answer. These are written recognition skills; the next listening lesson uses real audio for sound discrimination.

## Check your reading

Answer eight questions using the **kiosk card above**. Each question has one supported answer. Explanations and the correct evidence are revealed **after** submission, so do not use them as hints during the first attempt. Your score measures this written reading exercise, not pronunciation or an official JLPT score.
