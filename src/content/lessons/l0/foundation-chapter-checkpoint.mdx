## Your final foundation chapter checkpoint

**Can-do:** Read familiar kana with increasing confidence, notice a spelling error, and choose a manageable review habit before moving into N5-aligned study. This is an **academy-authored practice checkpoint**, not an official JLPT exam, a verified fluency certificate or a replacement for speaking and listening practice.

Try the twelve written questions without looking back at your notes. If you need to check the story, use it as a learning resource, but remember that an open-book attempt does not show unaided recall. The score records **written recognition and reading comprehension only**.

## 1. Reading evidence: Mina's correction diary

Recall the original L05 story **きょうの ふくしゅう**. Mina goes to school in the morning, greets her teacher, and finds a book and notebook on the desk. She writes **きょう** and **コーヒー**, forgets one **ー** in コーヒー, then checks and rewrites the word. At home, she plans a little more review tomorrow.

Before answering, find evidence in the original story:

> あさ、みなは がっこうへ いきました。

> つくえに ほんと ノートが あります。

> 「コーヒー」の ーを ひとつ わすれました。

> みなは もういちど みて、ただしく かきなおしました。

The story does **not** say which of the two long-vowel marks Mina forgot. Good reading means knowing what the text *does not* establish.

## 2. Kana and sentence patterns

| Form | What to notice |
| --- | --- |
| これは ノートです。 | Topic particle **は** is pronounced *wa* but written は. |
| これは ノートですか。 | **か** after です forms a polite question in this pattern. |
| きって | Small **っ** distinguishes this word for a postage stamp from きて. |
| コーヒー | Both **ー** marks are required in the conventional spelling. |
| きょう / あした | Today / tomorrow: useful time cues in a review plan. |

The object particle **を** is a different grammatical marker; it is not a substitute for topic は. No new kanji is required at this Level 0 checkpoint.

## 3. Listening review is a separate skill

The earlier L06 conversation contrasts what feels manageable with what is **むずかしいです** (“difficult”), and discusses **あした** (“tomorrow”) and **すこしずつ** (“little by little”). This checkpoint asks about the **meaning** of familiar listening-review expressions, but does **not** play or grade audio. For a genuine unaided listening attempt, return to L06 and use its authentic recordings only after the audio assets are available and verified. Reading a transcript is not equivalent to hearing speech.

## 4. Controlled, guided and independent practice

**Controlled:** complete the twelve mixed questions below, selecting one response for each. Feedback explains the precise evidence or spelling distinction.

**Guided:** after checking, select one missed item. Write the correct form, underline the part that caused trouble, and explain why the tempting option was wrong. For example: **これわ → これは**, because topic *wa* is written は.

**Independent:** use the private review-plan field to name a target, an action and a time. Model: “Tomorrow, read コーヒー, cover it, type it from memory, check both ー marks, and try again in three days.” Your plan is not scored. You may save it locally; avoid entering identifying personal details.

## 5. Interpret your result carefully

A strong written score means you recognized these particular forms today. It does not establish durable recall, native-reviewed pronunciation, listening comprehension or general N5 readiness. After this checkpoint, return to L05 for evidence reading, L06 for actual listening, and L07 for independent typing. A short, repeated repair loop is more useful than treating one score as a permanent label.
