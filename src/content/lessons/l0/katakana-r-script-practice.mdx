## At the fictional shop: read the whole label

**Can-do:** Complete basic katakana plus voiced sound families. Today you practice the remaining **R row** and check written marks in short product labels. These are **written script** exercises, not a listening or pronunciation test. Japanese katakana is not simply English written in a different alphabet; learn the established spelling of each word.

| Character | Sound cue | Copy model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">ラ</span> | ra | ラ ラ ラ |
| <span lang="ja">リ</span> | ri | リ リ リ |
| <span lang="ja">ル</span> | ru | ル ル ル |
| <span lang="ja">レ</span> | re | レ レ レ |
| <span lang="ja">ロ</span> | ro | ロ ロ ロ |

Read the row **ラ・リ・ル・レ・ロ** left to right, then in reverse. The sounds here are romanization cues, not English pronunciations. リ is two separate strokes; ロ is a rectangular katakana character, distinct in identity from the kanji 口 even when fonts make them look similar.

## Compare shapes before copying

- <span lang="ja">ラ / フ</span>: ラ has a short upper stroke and a longer lower shape; フ has a different upper structure.
- <span lang="ja">リ / ル</span>: リ has two simple descending strokes; ル has a different finishing shape.
- <span lang="ja">レ / ル</span>: the long bent shape of レ is not the two-part shape of ル.
- <span lang="ja">ロ / コ</span>: ロ closes its rectangular outline; コ is open on the left.
- <span lang="ja">ン / ソ</span>: ン (*n*) and ソ (*so*) are different symbols. Compare their stroke direction and the whole printed character; confirm with the word rather than relying on one font.

**Guided copy:** On paper, write ラ リ ル レ ロ once. Then type the same five characters using a Japanese keyboard or kana input method. Compare each output to the model above. If your device cannot type Japanese, copy by hand instead. No typed result is automatically graded as handwriting.

## Reading a fictional product shelf

| Printed card | Reading | Meaning or role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">ラジオ</span> | rajio | radio |
| <span lang="ja">ペン</span> | pen | pen |
| <span lang="ja">パン</span> | pan | bread |
| <span lang="ja">カバン</span> | kaban | bag |
| <span lang="ja">ミラ</span> | Mira | fictional person's name |

Notice the dakuten in **ジ** of ラジオ and **バ** of カバン: two small strokes mark a voiced sound. **パ** in パン has a small circle (*handakuten*) for *pa*, not the two strokes for *ba*. ペン ends in **ン**, not **ソ**. ラジオ uses ジ in its standard spelling; do not substitute ヂ just because a romanization looks similar.

## A practical written check

Cover the model table. Type **ラ リ ル レ ロ** in order, then type **ラジオ** and **ペン**. Compare your characters one by one with the printed labels. Award yourself one check per exact character, including the small marks. If the input method is unavailable, write them on paper and compare visually. This is self-review, not verified handwriting recognition.

**Three-point self-review rubric:** (1) correct character identity; (2) correct dakuten or handakuten where required; (3) correct ン versus ソ in context. If one criterion fails, return to the relevant model and repeat that word. Optional tracing is permitted on paper; no tracing data or microphone is collected.
