## Your chapter can-do

You can recognize simple **topic–comment statements** and **polite questions**. This checkpoint brings together written Japanese, a short self-introduction, and two classroom Q&A recordings. It assesses recognition and comprehension, not spontaneous speaking fluency.

### 1. The sentence frame

**A は B です** introduces a topic and says something about it. In <span lang="ja">わたしは がくせいです。</span> (“I am a student”), <span lang="ja">わたし</span> is the topic and <span lang="ja">がくせいです</span> is the comment. The topic marker **は** is pronounced *wa* in this grammatical use, although the kana は otherwise normally represents *ha*.

To ask a polite yes/no question, add **か**: <span lang="ja">たなかさんは せんせいですか。</span> (“Is Tanaka a teacher?”). A response can be <span lang="ja">はい、せんせいです。</span> (“Yes, [Tanaka] is a teacher.”). The topic can be omitted in the reply because it is understood from context. Do not assume every は in a Japanese word is a topic particle.

### 2. Preview the object marker

In <span lang="ja">ほんを よみます。</span> (“[Someone] reads a book”), **を** marks the direct object. It is normally pronounced *o*. Contrast **は** (topic) and **を** (object): they have different jobs and cannot be interchanged. You are only recognizing the pattern here; conjugating verbs is not the goal of this checkpoint.

### 3. Read four original mini-profiles

These fictional classroom notes were written specifically for this checkpoint. Read the Japanese first; use the English hints afterward.

| Card | Japanese | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | <span lang="ja">わたしは ミナです。がくせいです。</span> | I am Mina. I am a student. |
| B | <span lang="ja">わたしは たなかです。せんせいです。</span> | I am Tanaka. I am a teacher. |
| C | <span lang="ja">これは ノートです。あれは ほんです。</span> | This is a notebook. That over there is a book. |
| D | <span lang="ja">これは えんぴつですか。</span> | Is this a pencil? |

**Reading method:** underline the phrase that directly supports your answer. For Card C, <span lang="ja">これは ノートです</span> identifies the nearby notebook; <span lang="ja">あれは ほんです</span> refers to a more distant book. Do not infer extra facts such as anyone's age or nationality.

### 5. Write and self-correct

Optional ungraded IME practice: type <span lang="ja">わたしは がくせいです。</span> and <span lang="ja">これは ノートですか。</span>. Compare against these models: (1) topic particle written は, (2) question particle か after です, (3) correct katakana ノート, (4) a readable sentence ending. If your IME produces <span lang="ja">わたしわ</span>, revise it to <span lang="ja">わたしは</span>.
