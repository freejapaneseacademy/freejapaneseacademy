## Your chapter goal

Read connected words written with the **46 basic hiragana** accurately, without relying on romaji. This mixed checkpoint revisits visual look-alikes, short-word segmentation, word-card reading and real listening. Lesson 08 shadowing is optional and is **not** required to finish the checkpoint.

### Read the original word-card board

In a classroom, a teacher shows two animal picture cards:

> **ねこのカード** — a cat picture card  
> **いぬのカード** — a dog picture card  
> **ねこのカードをみます。** — I'll look at the cat card.  
> **いぬのカードもありますよ。** — There's also a dog card.

Notice **ね** in **ねこ** and **ぬ** in **いぬ**. Compare the entire shape before choosing an answer. **の** connects an animal word to the card; **も** adds *also*. A picture card is not the animal itself.

In a library, a visitor opens a nature book:

> **やまのほん** — a book about mountains  
> **そらのえ** — a picture of the sky  
> **かわのえ** — a picture of a river  
> **そらとかわのえがあります。** — There are pictures of the sky and a river.

Read **やま**, **そら** and **かわ** as complete words. In **そらとかわ**, **と** links two nouns; it is not part of either noun. **ほん** ends in the separate nasal mora **ん**.

### Writing self-check

Type **ねこ**, **いぬ**, **そら**, **かわ**, and **やま** using a Japanese IME, or write them on paper. Check each kana against the cards. Then write **ねこのカード** and **そらとかわ**. Use a simple rubric: correct kana order, distinguishable ね/れ and ぬ/め shapes, intact word boundaries, and legibility. This handwriting self-check is not automatically graded.
