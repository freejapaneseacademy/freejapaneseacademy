## Chapter Can-do: read and write voiced and p-series hiragana

The marks matter. **が・ざ・だ・ば** use dakuten (゛); **ぱ** uses handakuten (゜). The voiced counterparts of **か・さ・た・は** are **が・ざ・だ・ば**, while **は → ぱ** produces a p sound. The full series also includes **ぎ・ぐ・げ・ご**, **じ・ず・ぜ・ぞ**, **ぢ・づ・で・ど**, **び・ぶ・べ・ぼ**, and **ぴ・ぷ・ぺ・ぽ**. In standard modern spelling, **じ** and **ず** are common, while **ぢ** and **づ** occur in specific established words. Do not confuse **ば** with **ぱ**.

### Reading A: bakery menu and request

> **パンはありますか。** — Do you have bread?  
> **はい。たまごパンとぶどうパンがあります。** — Yes. We have egg bread and grape bread.  
> **ぶどうパンをひとつください。** — One grape bread, please.  
> **みずをください。** — Water, please.

In **たまご**, **ご** carries dakuten. In **ぶどう**, both **ぶ** and **ど** are voiced; **う** remains a separate written mora. **パン** is written in katakana and begins with the p-series **パ**. The written particle **を** is normally pronounced *o* in the request. These lines reproduce the chapter's original teaching dialogue.

### Reading B: at the zoo

> **あそこに、パンダがいますよ。** — There's a panda over there.  
> **ぞうもいます。** — There's an elephant too.  
> **ぞうはおおきいですね。** — The elephant is big, isn't it?  
> **ぶたをみにいきましょう。** — Let's go see the pigs.

**パ** bears handakuten; **ぞ** and **ぶ** bear dakuten. The particle **も** means *also*. Base answers on what the text actually says, not guesses about animals.

### Writing self-check

With a Japanese IME or paper, write **が・ざ・だ・ば・ぱ**, then **たまご・ぶどう・ぞう・ぶた**. Compare mark placement, kana order and legibility against the examples. This writing check is ungraded; no handwriting recognition is claimed.
