## Optional speaking: hear, pause, repeat

The small **っ** marks a brief consonant hold, not a separately spoken **つ**. A long vowel takes extra time. These features matter when you say **いっさつ** (“one bound item”) or **がっこう** (“school”). This is supported speaking practice, not a graded pronunciation test. You may complete the lesson without recording your voice.

The two original synthetic AivisSpeech tracks are reused unchanged from Chapter 03 Lesson 06. Listen to each scene, play one turn, pause and repeat it. The Japanese and English lines are visible to support shadowing; this is **not** an unseen listening assessment.

## Scene A — at the bookstore

The customer asks for a small notebook: **ちいさいノートをさがしています。** (“I'm looking for a small notebook.”) After the clerk shows a blue one, he says **いいですね。いっさつください。** (“That looks good. One, please.”) The word **いっさつ** has a held consonant before **さ**. The clerk replies **はい。ちょっとおまちください。** (“Certainly. Please wait a moment.”) In **ちょっと**, keep **ちょ** together and hold briefly before **と**.

Try repeating **いっさつください** and **ちょっとおまちください** separately, then as part of their complete turns. Do not insert a full **つ** between the sounds.

## Scene B — meeting near the park

A man asks **きょう、がっこうであいますか。** (“Shall we meet at school today?”) The woman suggests **さんじにあいましょう。** (“Let's meet at three.”) When asked about the location, she corrects it: **いいえ。こうえんのまえであいましょう。** (“No. Let's meet in front of the park.”)

Hear the consonant hold in **がっこう** and the longer vowel in **こうえん**. In writing, **こう** contains **おう** after こ, but do not turn the spelling into a separate pronounced *u* syllable in this common word. Repeat the complete meeting invitation at normal speed; use 0.85× only if helpful.
