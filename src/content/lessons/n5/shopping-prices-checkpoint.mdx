## Mission: finish a stationery-shop purchase

You are helping a fictional customer choose supplies. First read the price signs, then answer the **12 written checkpoint questions**. This is practice, not an official JLPT examination. The prices and dialogue below are original fictional teaching material, not real store offers.

### 1. Retrieve the language

| Japanese | Reading / English | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">これはいくらですか。</span> | kore wa ikura desu ka — How much is this? | **は** marks the item being priced. |
| <span lang="ja">ノートを一冊ください。</span> | nōto o issatsu kudasai — One notebook, please. | **を** marks the requested item; **冊（さつ）** counts bound books. |
| <span lang="ja">えんぴつを二本お願いします。</span> | enpitsu o nihon onegai shimasu — Two pencils, please. | **本（ほん）** counts long, thin objects; 二本 is read **にほん**. |
| <span lang="ja">赤いかばんをお願いします。</span> | akai kaban o onegai shimasu — The red bag, please. | **赤い** modifies the noun directly. |
| <span lang="ja">黒いのをお願いします。</span> | kuroi no o onegai shimasu — The black one, please. | **の** stands for a previously understood object. |

**Do not confuse** <span lang="ja">いくらですか</span> (how much money?) with <span lang="ja">いくつですか</span> (how many / how old in some contexts?). When choosing among sizes, <span lang="ja">大きいの</span> means “the large one” if the item is already understood.

### 2. Read four original price signs

| Sign | Japanese | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | <span lang="ja">青いノート　一冊300円</span> | Blue notebook: ¥300 each |
| B | <span lang="ja">えんぴつ　一本100円</span> | Pencil: ¥100 each |
| C | <span lang="ja">小さいかばん　900円／大きいかばん　1200円</span> | Small bag: ¥900 / large bag: ¥1,200 |
| D | <span lang="ja">白い紙　十枚200円</span> | Ten sheets of white paper: ¥200 |

These are fictional signs. Read the Japanese first. Find **円** for price, **冊** for notebooks, **本** for pencils, and **枚（まい）** for flat sheets. A quantity attached to a price can change the unit: **一本100円** means *each pencil* costs ¥100, while **十枚200円** gives a price for ten sheets.

**Evidence example:** If the question asks how much three pencils cost, quote <span lang="ja">一本100円</span> and calculate 100 × 3 = 300. Do not confuse the unit price with the total.

### 3. Read the customer's choices

<span lang="ja">店員（てんいん）：いらっしゃいませ。</span> — Clerk: Welcome.

<span lang="ja">客（きゃく）：この青いノートはいくらですか。</span> — Customer: How much is this blue notebook?

<span lang="ja">店員：一冊300円です。</span> — Clerk: It is ¥300 per notebook.

<span lang="ja">客：では、二冊ください。えんぴつも三本お願いします。</span> — Customer: Then two notebooks, please. Three pencils too, please.

<span lang="ja">店員：はい。全部（ぜんぶ）で900円です。</span> — Clerk: Certainly. The total is ¥900.

Check the arithmetic: 2 × ¥300 + 3 × ¥100 = **¥900**. The **も** in <span lang="ja">えんぴつも</span> adds pencils to the purchase; it does not mean the pencils cost the same as the notebooks.

### 4. Controlled practice, then guided practice

Complete these aloud or on paper before opening the scored questions.

1. Point to Sign C and ask the price of the small bag: <span lang="ja">この小さいかばんは＿＿＿＿ですか。</span> (Answer: <span lang="ja">いくら</span>.)
2. Request two pencils: <span lang="ja">えんぴつを＿＿＿＿お願いします。</span> (Answer: <span lang="ja">二本（にほん）</span>.)
3. Explain why <span lang="ja">ノートを二枚ください</span> is inappropriate when requesting two bound notebooks: use <span lang="ja">二冊（にさつ）</span>, not the flat-sheet counter **枚**.

Now change the dialogue: request one notebook and one pencil. Write the new total and cite both unit prices. Model: <span lang="ja">ノートを一冊と、えんぴつを一本お願いします。</span> The total is ¥400. **と** joins the two requested items.

### 6. Independent writing and optional speaking

Write **two to five connected Japanese sentences** as a customer. Include a price question, a color or size choice, a quantity, and a polite request. A model:

<span lang="ja">この青いノートはいくらですか。二冊ください。黒いペンも一本お願いします。</span>

“How much is this blue notebook? Two, please. One black pen as well, please.”

**Self-review (ungraded):** Did you (1) use **いくら** for price, (2) choose a suitable counter, (3) attach **い** to color/size adjectives, and (4) use **ください** or **お願いします** for the request? Your own sentences can differ from the model. No automatic writing or pronunciation grade is claimed.

Optionally play customer and clerk roles with a partner. No microphone or recording is activated by this checkpoint. Review incorrect items after a day and again later; focus on the evidence, not answer positions.
