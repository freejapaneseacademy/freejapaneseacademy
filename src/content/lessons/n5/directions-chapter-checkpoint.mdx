## Mission: help two visitors find their way

You are at a neighborhood information desk. Two visitors have different destinations. Read their original fictional route notes, choose the correct turns and explain where each destination is. This chapter checkpoint reviews navigation, landmarks, reading and writing; it is **not** an official JLPT test or a substitute for listening to the chapter's real recordings.

### 1. Three ways to describe movement

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning and use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">右</span> | みぎ | right |
| <span lang="ja">左</span> | ひだり | left |
| <span lang="ja">まっすぐ</span> | まっすぐ | straight ahead |
| <span lang="ja">次の角</span> | つぎのかど | next corner |
| <span lang="ja">曲がってください</span> | まがってください | please turn |
| <span lang="ja">行ってください</span> | いってください | please go |

To give a polite instruction, use the verb's **て-form + ください**. For example, <span lang="ja">この道をまっすぐ行ってください。</span> means “Please go straight along this road.” <span lang="ja">次の角を右に曲がってください。</span> means “Please turn right at the next corner.” The phrase <span lang="ja">右に</span> gives the direction of the turn. Do not confuse the destination of a movement with the position of an existing building.

### 2. Finding a landmark: 〜にあります

A building or other inanimate place can be located with <span lang="ja">〜にあります</span>. Compare:

- <span lang="ja">図書館（としょかん）は銀行（ぎんこう）のとなりにあります。</span> — The library is next to the bank.
- <span lang="ja">郵便局（ゆうびんきょく）は公園（こうえん）のとなりにあります。</span> — The post office is next to the park.
- <span lang="ja">駅（えき）の前（まえ）にあります。</span> — It is in front of the station.

**Common mistake:** <span lang="ja">右に曲がってください</span> asks someone to turn right; <span lang="ja">右にあります</span> states that something is on the right. The same <span lang="ja">右に</span> can appear with different verbs, but the function of the sentence changes.

### 3. Original route A — station to library

Read this invented exchange first without looking at the English meaning.

> <span lang="ja">みか：すみません。駅（えき）の前（まえ）から図書館（としょかん）まで、どう行きますか。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">案内（あんない）の人：この道（みち）をまっすぐ行（い）ってください。次（つぎ）の角（かど）を右（みぎ）に曲（ま）がってください。図書館は銀行（ぎんこう）のとなりにあります。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">みか：ありがとうございます。</span>

**Meaning:** Mika asks how to get from in front of the station to the library. The guide says to go straight along this road, turn right at the next corner, and find the library next to the bank.

**Evidence trail:** <span lang="ja">駅の前から</span> tells us the starting point; <span lang="ja">まっすぐ</span> gives the first movement; <span lang="ja">右に曲がってください</span> gives the turn; <span lang="ja">銀行のとなり</span> identifies the library's location.

### 4. Original route B — park to post office

> <span lang="ja">たく：郵便局（ゆうびんきょく）はどこですか。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">案内の人：公園（こうえん）までまっすぐ行ってください。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">たく：公園で右に曲がりますか。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">案内の人：いいえ、左（ひだり）に曲がってください。郵便局は公園のとなりにあります。</span>

**Meaning:** Taku asks where the post office is. The guide says to go straight to the park. Taku suggests turning right, but the guide corrects him: turn **left**. The post office is next to the park.

**Evidence trail:** A question is not a confirmed direction. <span lang="ja">右に曲がりますか</span> is Taku's proposed right turn; <span lang="ja">いいえ、左に曲がってください</span> is the guide's actual instruction. Always follow the correction, not the suggestion.

### 5. Guided practice

Before using the scored checkpoint, try these without looking back:

1. Complete the polite instruction: <span lang="ja">次の角を＿＿に曲がってください。</span> Choose <span lang="ja">右</span> or <span lang="ja">左</span> according to your intended turn.
2. Say where the library is: <span lang="ja">図書館は銀行のとなりに＿＿。</span> The missing word is <span lang="ja">あります</span>.
3. Identify the action in <span lang="ja">この道をまっすぐ行ってください。</span> It asks the listener to **go straight**, not turn.

### 7. Write directions and review your own work

Write **two to five connected Japanese sentences** directing a visitor from a station to a library or a post office. Include a straight movement, a turn, and a landmark location. You may change the fictional landmark layout if you keep your instructions internally consistent.

**Model:** <span lang="ja">駅の前からまっすぐ行ってください。次の角を左に曲がってください。郵便局は公園のとなりにあります。</span>

**Meaning:** Please go straight from in front of the station. Please turn left at the next corner. The post office is next to the park.

Check that (1) the start and destination are clear, (2) <span lang="ja">まっすぐ</span> describes a straight movement, (3) <span lang="ja">右</span> or <span lang="ja">左</span> matches the intended turn, (4) location uses <span lang="ja">にあります</span>, and (5) the sentences are polite and connected. Your free writing is not machine-graded.

Optional speaking: read the model aloud and substitute the other turn. You control any recording outside this exercise; no microphone is activated here and no pronunciation grade is given.
