## Mission: decode the descriptions in a small shop

**Can-do:** Describe food, places and objects with adjectives. Today you will *recognize and read* six useful kanji inside whole adjective words. These examples are original and the shops and people are fictional. You do not need to memorize every possible reading of each kanji.

## 1. Learn the whole word, not just the character

| Kanji | Word | Kana | Meaning in this lesson |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 大 | <span lang="ja">大きい</span> | おおきい | big |
| 小 | <span lang="ja">小さい</span> | ちいさい | small |
| 高 | <span lang="ja">高い</span> | たかい | expensive; also tall/high |
| 安 | <span lang="ja">安い</span> | やすい | inexpensive |
| 新 | <span lang="ja">新しい</span> | あたらしい | new |
| 古 | <span lang="ja">古い</span> | ふるい | old (of objects, buildings, etc.) |

**Reading trap:** <span lang="ja">大きい</span> is **おおきい**, not *だいきい*. <span lang="ja">小さい</span> is **ちいさい**, not *しょうさい*. Kanji have multiple readings; the complete word determines the reading. In <span lang="ja">新しい</span>, the reading is **あたらしい**.

These are all **い-adjectives**: the final **い** is part of the word. They can appear before a noun or at the end of a polite sentence with **です**.

## 2. See the adjective do a job

| Japanese | Reading | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">大きいケーキ</span> | おおきいケーキ | a big cake |
| <span lang="ja">小さいカップ</span> | ちいさいカップ | a small cup |
| <span lang="ja">このかばんは高いです。</span> | このかばんは たかいです。 | This bag is expensive. |
| <span lang="ja">このパンは安いです。</span> | このパンは やすいです。 | This bread is inexpensive. |
| <span lang="ja">新しい店です。</span> | あたらしい みせです。 | It is a new shop. |
| <span lang="ja">あの建物は古いです。</span> | あの たてものは ふるいです。 | That building is old. |

The topic marker **は** is pronounced **わ**. The written character <span lang="ja">高</span> also appears in words about height, but on a **price comparison** sign, <span lang="ja">高い</span> usually means *expensive*. Context decides.

## 3. Decode an original set of shop cards

Imagine a small café beside a stationery store. Read the four cards; all information is fictional.

| Card | Japanese sign | Reading and meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | <span lang="ja">小さいケーキ　300円</span> | ちいさいケーキ — small cake, ¥300 |
| B | <span lang="ja">大きいケーキ　600円</span> | おおきいケーキ — big cake, ¥600 |
| C | <span lang="ja">新しいノート　200円</span> | あたらしいノート — new notebook, ¥200 |
| D | <span lang="ja">古い本　100円</span> | ふるい ほん — old book, ¥100 |

A customer comments:

> <span lang="ja">このケーキは大きいです。でも、高いです。</span>  
> このケーキは おおきいです。でも、たかいです。  
> This cake is big. But it is expensive.

**Evidence:** The large cake costs 600円, compared with 300円 for the small cake. **高い** is a price judgment in this conversation, not a statement about the cake's height. The comment is a speaker's evaluation, not an objective universal threshold for "expensive."

## 4. Form and meaning: a useful contrast

- Before a noun: <span lang="ja">新しいノート</span> — a new notebook. Do **not** add な: *新しいなノート* is incorrect.
- Polite statement: <span lang="ja">このノートは新しいです。</span> — This notebook is new.
- Negative: <span lang="ja">このノートは新しくないです。</span> — This notebook is not new. Change **い** to **くない**; do not say *新しいじゃないです* for the ordinary beginner negative.
- Another negative: <span lang="ja">この本は古くないです。</span> — This book is not old.

**Important distinction:** <span lang="ja">きれい</span> (pretty/clean) ends in the sound い but is a **な-adjective**: <span lang="ja">きれいな店</span>. In contrast, <span lang="ja">新しい店</span> is an い-adjective construction. Not every word ending in い behaves alike.

## 5. Guided decoding

Cover the kana column in section 1. Read <span lang="ja">大きい・小さい・高い・安い・新しい・古い</span> aloud or silently, then reveal the readings. Next, answer from the shop cards:

1. Which card has <span lang="ja">新しい</span>? **C**, the notebook.
2. Which item is <span lang="ja">古い</span>? **D**, the book.
3. Which cake is <span lang="ja">大きい</span>? **B**, the 600円 cake.

For each answer, point to the **Japanese word** that supports it. This is reading evidence, not guessing from the English.

## 6. Independent use: your own shop description

Write **three connected short sentences** about a fictional item or place. Use at least two of today's adjective words. A model:

> <span lang="ja">これは新しいかばんです。大きいです。でも、高いです。</span>  
> これは あたらしいかばんです。おおきいです。でも、たかいです。  
> This is a new bag. It is big. But it is expensive.

**Self-review:** Check the correct reading of each word, the final い, the adjective's relation to its noun, and the negative form if you use one. Your free writing is **not automatically graded**. Optional speaking: read your three sentences aloud with the kana support. No audio recording or pronunciation evaluation is required.
