## An outing with different travel plans

**Can-do for this chapter:** Explain transport choices and who accompanies you. Imagine two classmates, **ゆい (Yui)** and **たくみ (Takumi)**, making plans for Saturday. Read the fictional exchange once for the overall meaning, then again to find each traveller's transport and companion.

> **ゆい:** <span lang="ja">土曜日、だれと公園に行きますか。</span>
>
> **たくみ:** <span lang="ja">妹と行きます。バスで行きます。ゆいさんは？</span>
>
> **ゆい:** <span lang="ja">私は一人で行きます。自転車で行きます。</span>
>
> **たくみ:** <span lang="ja">そうですか。公園で会いましょう。</span>
>
> **ゆい:** <span lang="ja">はい。十時に会いましょう。</span>

**English translation**

- Yui: “Who are you going to the park with on Saturday?”
- Takumi: “I'm going with my younger sister. I'm going by bus. How about you, Yui?”
- Yui: “I'm going alone. I'm going by bicycle.”
- Takumi: “I see. Let's meet at the park.”
- Yui: “Yes. Let's meet at ten.”

The dialogue says they **plan to meet** at the park. It does not say that they travel there together.

## Read the key expressions

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">土曜日</span> | どようび | Saturday |
| <span lang="ja">公園</span> | こうえん | park |
| <span lang="ja">だれと</span> | だれと | with whom |
| <span lang="ja">妹</span> | いもうと | one's own younger sister |
| <span lang="ja">バス</span> | バス | bus |
| <span lang="ja">自転車</span> | じてんしゃ | bicycle |
| <span lang="ja">一人で</span> | ひとりで | alone, by oneself |
| <span lang="ja">会いましょう</span> | あいましょう | let's meet |
| <span lang="ja">十時</span> | じゅうじ | ten o'clock |

**Notice the two questions hidden inside a plan:** *How* does a person travel? *With whom* do they travel? These are different pieces of information.

## Three small expressions with different jobs

### 1. A vehicle + で: the means of travel

<span lang="ja">バスで行きます。</span> — “I go by bus.” The particle **で** after バス tells us *how*. Similarly, <span lang="ja">電車で行きます。</span> (でんしゃでいきます) means “I go by train.” The vehicle is not a person accompanying the traveller.

### 2. A person + と: a companion

<span lang="ja">妹と行きます。</span> — “I go with my younger sister.” The particle **と** identifies a companion. Compare <span lang="ja">友だちと行きます。</span> (ともだちといきます) — “I go with a friend.” **と** is not the usual particle for the means of transport.

### 3. 一人で: travelling alone

<span lang="ja">私は一人で行きます。</span> (わたしはひとりでいきます) — “I go alone.” Here **一人で** describes travelling by oneself; it does **not** name a vehicle. Yui can travel **一人で** *and* **自転車で**: she is alone and uses a bicycle.

Compare the two complete plans:

| Traveller | Companion | Means | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Takumi | <span lang="ja">妹と</span> — with his younger sister | <span lang="ja">バスで</span> — by bus | <span lang="ja">公園に</span> — to the park |
| Yui | <span lang="ja">一人で</span> — alone | <span lang="ja">自転車で</span> — by bicycle | <span lang="ja">公園に</span> — to the park |

The particle **に** in <span lang="ja">公園に行きます</span> marks the destination. The **で** in <span lang="ja">公園で会いましょう</span> instead marks the **place of the meeting**. A single particle can serve different functions depending on the expression.

## Common misunderstandings

- **“妹で行きます”** does not mean “I go with my sister.” Use <span lang="ja">妹と行きます。</span> The person is a companion, not a means of transport.
- **“バスと行きます”** is not the normal way to say “I go by bus.” Use <span lang="ja">バスで行きます。</span>
- **“一人で”** does not mean “on foot.” Walking is <span lang="ja">歩いて</span> (あるいて). Yui is alone **and** cycling.
- The dialogue does not establish the bus route, fare, or whether Yui owns the bicycle. Do not add facts not given.

## Controlled practice: find the evidence

1. In <span lang="ja">妹と行きます。バスで行きます。</span>, underline the **companion** (妹) and circle the **transport** (バス).
2. In <span lang="ja">私は一人で行きます。自転車で行きます。</span>, which phrase says “alone”? **一人で**. Which says “by bicycle”? **自転車で**.
3. In <span lang="ja">公園で会いましょう。</span>, **公園で** names the meeting place, not the vehicle.

## Guided practice: change one part

Start with <span lang="ja">友だちと電車で公園に行きます。</span> (ともだちとでんしゃでこうえんにいきます) — “I go to the park with a friend by train.”

Replace **友だち** with **母** (はは, my mother): <span lang="ja">母と電車で公園に行きます。</span> — “I go to the park with my mother by train.”

Replace **電車** with **バス**: <span lang="ja">母とバスで公園に行きます。</span> — “I go to the park with my mother by bus.”

To describe going alone, use <span lang="ja">一人でバスで公園に行きます。</span> — “I go to the park alone by bus.” Repeating で here is grammatical, although you can rearrange or simplify the sentence in conversation.

## Independent use: plan a fictional outing

Write **three short sentences**: a destination, a companion (or 一人で), and a means of transport. Model:

> <span lang="ja">日曜日、図書館に行きます。友だちと行きます。電車で行きます。</span>

“On Sunday I will go to the library. I'll go with a friend. I'll go by train.”

Check your own work: (1) Is the destination clear? (2) Is the companion marked with **と**, or is **一人で** used? (3) Is the means marked with **で**? (4) Does your English interpretation match what you actually wrote? This open response is **self-reviewed**, not automatically scored.

**Optional speaking:** Read the Yui–Takumi exchange aloud, then change the vehicle and companion. This is text-based rehearsal, **not** recorded pronunciation assessment. The chapter's L06 will provide genuine listening practice separately.
