## Mission: help visitors find their destinations

You are volunteering at the reception desk of a fictional community learning center. A visitor needs the library, another needs the restroom, and a student wants the office at Sakura School. By the end, you can **say where a place is** and explain the words that support your answer. This is original academy practice, not an official JLPT test.

### 1. Review location words and the speaker's viewpoint

| Japanese | Reading | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">ここ</span> | ここ | Here, at the speaker's location |
| <span lang="ja">そこ</span> | そこ | There, often near the listener or an indicated place |
| <span lang="ja">あそこ</span> | あそこ | Over there, away from both |
| <span lang="ja">どこ</span> | どこ | Where? |
| <span lang="ja">一階・二階</span> | いっかい・にかい | First floor; second floor |
| <span lang="ja">入口・階段</span> | いりぐち・かいだん | Entrance; stairs |
| <span lang="ja">右・左</span> | みぎ・ひだり | Right; left |

At the reception desk, a staff member can say <span lang="ja">受付はここです。</span> — “Reception is here.” A visitor standing across the room might instead say <span lang="ja">受付はあそこです。</span> — “Reception is over there.” Both can describe the same desk from **different viewpoints**. Do not choose ここ or あそこ solely from a printed map that does not specify the speaker's position.

### 2. Ask a location question; choose the right particle

- <span lang="ja">図書館はどこですか。</span> — Where is the library?
- <span lang="ja">図書館は二階にあります。</span> — The library is on the second floor.
- <span lang="ja">事務室は一階の東です。</span> — The office is on the east side of the first floor.
- <span lang="ja">トイレは階段の右です。</span> — The restroom is to the right of the stairs.

**に** marks a location with the existence verb **あります**: <span lang="ja">二階にあります</span>. A location followed by **です** does not require に: <span lang="ja">二階です</span>. **の** connects the floor to its side in <span lang="ja">一階の東</span>, or a landmark to a relative location in <span lang="ja">階段の右</span>. Do not say <span lang="ja">二階があります</span> to mean “it is on the second floor”; that would make the floor the thing that exists.

### 3. Read the original Sakura School building map

This is the same **fictional two-floor text map** used in Chapter L05. West and east are labels, not directions from a known viewpoint. The map does not show a cafeteria.

| Floor | West (西・にし) | Center (中央・ちゅうおう) | East (東・ひがし) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **二階 (にかい)** | **図書室 (としょしつ)** — library room | **階段 (かいだん)・廊下 (ろうか)** — stairs and corridor | **教室 (きょうしつ)** — classroom |
| **一階 (いっかい)** | **保健室 (ほけんしつ)** — health room | **入口 (いりぐち)・階段・廊下** — entrance, stairs and corridor | **事務室 (じむしつ)** — office |

Read the original written guide, then cover the English labels:

> <span lang="ja">入口は一階の中央です。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">事務室は一階の東です。保健室は一階の西です。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">図書室は二階の西です。教室は二階の東です。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">階段は一階と二階にあります。</span>

**Guided evidence:** “Where is the library room?” The exact evidence is <span lang="ja">図書室は二階の西です。</span>, so answer “second floor, west.” Do not confuse **図書室** (library room) with **教室** (classroom). “Where is the cafeteria?” is **not answered by this map**; the separate L06 community-center recording mentions a cafeteria, but it does not change the Sakura School map.

### 5. Write your own mini-direction exchange

Create **three or four connected Japanese sentences** for a fictional visitor. Ask where a room is, give a floor or landmark, and thank the helper. Use the model only after drafting:

> A: <span lang="ja">すみません。図書室はどこですか。</span>
>
> B: <span lang="ja">図書室は二階の西です。</span>
>
> A: <span lang="ja">ありがとうございます。</span>

Self-review your work against these criteria: (1) Does **どこですか** ask for a location? (2) Does your answer name a real location in the fictional map? (3) Do you use **の** between floor and side, or **に** with あります? (4) Does the room agree with the evidence? (5) Is the exchange polite and clear? This composition is **self-assessed**, not automatically graded. Try changing 図書室 to 事務室 and updating the answer.

**Optional speaking:** Read your dialogue aloud and switch roles. When genuine chapter audio is available, replay a short L06 turn and shadow it. No microphone activates automatically, and no pronunciation grade is claimed.
