## Listen for dates, times and quantities

Two separate fictional community announcements give practical information. **Track A** is an art class and **Track B** is library story time. The dates, times and materials are different. On your first play, listen for the *purpose*, not every syllable. These are fictional events, not real schedules.

### What to listen for

| Expression | Reading | Listening function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| お知らせ | おしらせ | announcement |
| 何時からですか | なんじからですか | when does it start? |
| 午前 / 午後 | ごぜん / ごご | a.m. / p.m. |
| 〜日 | 〜にち (some irregular) | day of the month |
| 〜枚 | 〜まい | flat objects |
| 〜人 | 〜にん (some irregular) | people |
| 〜つ | 〜つ | general items |

**十一月** (じゅういちがつ) means November. Day readings are not always predictable: **十四日** is じゅうよっか and **二十一日** is にじゅういちにち. Listen to the complete date. **午前** means morning, **午後** means afternoon. **十時から** means “starting at ten”; **から** marks the starting point.

Japanese counters depend on the thing being counted. Paper uses **枚**; people use **人**; small general items may use **つ**. Do not substitute one counter for another. When a visitor asks about books, listen carefully: a question does not establish that books are required.

### Track A: an art-class announcement

Listen once for the class topic. On the second play, note the date and start time separately. On the third, catch the material and quantity requested. A visitor repeats the quantity as confirmation.

### Track B: a library announcement

This is a *different event*. Listen for the date, the number of teachers, and the contrast between something not required and the item that *is* requested. Distinguish **午後** from **午前**.

### Three-pass practice

1. Listen without the transcript and describe the purpose of each announcement in English.
2. Replay, noting date, time, people and materials. Do not rely on written answers before the first attempt.
3. Answer six questions. Then reveal transcripts and English translations if needed. Opening a transcript labels the attempt **transcript-assisted**.

### Guided writing and optional speaking

Write two fictional event notices using **[event] は [month] [day] です。[time] からです。** Add one sentence with **枚**, **人**, or **つ**. Example of a *different* event: **料理の会は十二月三日です。午後一時からです。お皿を二枚持ってきてください。** (“The cooking meeting is December 3. It starts at 1 p.m. Please bring two plates.”) Self-check your counter, time marker, date reading and polite request. This is ungraded learner self-review.

After authentic audio becomes playable, optionally shadow a short announcement and practise **何時からですか。** with your own fictional answer. No microphone or pronunciation grade is required.

### Review and media status

Can you distinguish a date, a time and a quantity by ear? Review mistakes with a second listening pass before revealing the transcript. The candidate TTS job is not itself audio.
