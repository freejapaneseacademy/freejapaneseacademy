## Your mission: read a stationery shop's price tags

**Can-do:** Ask prices, amounts and sizes politely. This written kanji lesson builds recognition for prices, colors and sizes. It does not require handwriting mastery or recorded pronunciation. All labels and people below are fictional.

## 1. Three characters that help with prices

| Character | Meaning | Word and reading | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">円</span> | yen | <span lang="ja">百円（ひゃくえん）</span> | ¥100 |
| <span lang="ja">百</span> | hundred | <span lang="ja">三百円（さんびゃくえん）</span> | ¥300 |
| <span lang="ja">千</span> | thousand | <span lang="ja">千円（せんえん）</span> | ¥1,000 |

**Important pronunciation distinction:** <span lang="ja">百</span> by itself is <span lang="ja">ひゃく</span>, but **三百** is <span lang="ja">さんびゃく</span>, not *さんひゃく*. The number 300 can be printed **300円** or **三百円**. A shop may also use Arabic numerals, so practice recognizing both.

**Read the full label:** <span lang="ja">このノートは三百円です。</span> (このノートは さんびゃくえん です。) — This notebook costs 300 yen. The topic marker は is pronounced **わ** here.

## 2. Three color kanji in actual words

| Character | Useful word | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">赤</span> | <span lang="ja">赤い</span> | あかい | red |
| <span lang="ja">青</span> | <span lang="ja">青い</span> | あおい | blue |
| <span lang="ja">白</span> | <span lang="ja">白い</span> | しろい | white |

These words are **い-adjectives**. Put them before a noun: <span lang="ja">赤いペン</span> (あかいペン) — a red pen; <span lang="ja">青いかばん</span> (あおいかばん) — a blue bag; <span lang="ja">白いノート</span> (しろいノート) — a white notebook.

A color word without い may be a noun in other constructions, but for this chapter use the reliable beginner patterns **赤い・青い・白い + noun**. Do not write *赤ペン* as a direct replacement for every **赤いペン** context.

## 3. Size kanji: big and small

| Character | Word | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">大</span> | <span lang="ja">大きい</span> | おおきい | large |
| <span lang="ja">小</span> | <span lang="ja">小さい</span> | ちいさい | small |

In these words the readings are **おお** and **ちい**, not a simple one-kana reading for each character. Learn the whole word: <span lang="ja">大きいかばん</span> (おおきいかばん) — a big bag; <span lang="ja">小さいノート</span> (ちいさいノート) — a small notebook. The kana **きい** and **さい** are part of the spelling.

## 4. Read an original shop display

| Fictional item | Label | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pen | <span lang="ja">赤いペン　200円</span> | Red pen — ¥200 |
| Notebook | <span lang="ja">白いノート　300円</span> | White notebook — ¥300 |
| Bag | <span lang="ja">小さい青いかばん　千円</span> | Small blue bag — ¥1,000 |
| Bag | <span lang="ja">大きい赤いかばん　二千円</span> | Big red bag — ¥2,000 |

Notice that <span lang="ja">小さい青いかばん</span> contains **two modifiers** before the noun: *small* and *blue*. The order can vary, but both describe the same bag. The label **千円** is ¥1,000, not ¥100.

### Short shop exchange

> 店員：<span lang="ja">いらっしゃいませ。</span> — Welcome.
>
> 客：<span lang="ja">この小さいかばんはいくらですか。</span> (この ちいさい かばんは いくらですか。) — How much is this small bag?
>
> 店員：<span lang="ja">千円です。</span> (せんえんです。) — It is ¥1,000.
>
> 客：<span lang="ja">青いかばんをお願いします。</span> (あおい かばんを おねがいします。) — The blue bag, please.

**Evidence check:** The small blue bag is <span lang="ja">千円</span>. The customer requests <span lang="ja">青いかばん</span>. The polite request uses **をお願いします**, reviewed in the previous grammar lesson.

## 5. Guided decoding and common mistakes

First cover the readings above. Decode <span lang="ja">百円・千円・赤い・青い・白い・大きい・小さい</span>. Then read **赤いペン 200円** without translating one character at a time.

- **百** and **千** are easy to mix up: 百 is 100; 千 is 1,000.
- **大きい** is **おおきい**, not *だいきい*; **小さい** is **ちいさい**, not *しょうさい*.
- **白い** is **しろい**, not *はくい*. Kanji have multiple readings; use the reading of the complete word.
- In **300円**, the 円 is read **えん**. The Arabic numerals do not change the currency.

**Try it:** A fictional sign says <span lang="ja">白いノート　300円</span>. Identify the object (**ノート**), its color (**白い**), and its price (**300円**). Then compare with <span lang="ja">赤いペン　200円</span>: which costs more? The notebook costs ¥100 more.

## 6. Independent use and optional writing

Write two short labels for a fictional stationery display. Use one color word and one size word, and choose a sensible price. Example: <span lang="ja">小さい赤いノート　百円</span> — Small red notebook, ¥100. Then write a polite question: <span lang="ja">このノートはいくらですか。</span> — How much is this notebook?

**Self-check:** Are the adjectives written with their い endings? Does the price include 円? Is the question marked with か? Free writing is self-reviewed and not automatically graded. For optional speaking, read your labels aloud using the kana support; no pronunciation grade is claimed.
