## A visitor at a Japanese school

A fictional school has a printed floor guide. Your task is to read the **whole word** on each sign, rather than guess every individual character reading. By the end you can distinguish the library from the classroom and find the entrance, exit and floor.

## Nine practical written signs

| Sign | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">図書館</span> | としょかん | library |
| <span lang="ja">教室</span> | きょうしつ | classroom |
| <span lang="ja">学校</span> | がっこう | school |
| <span lang="ja">入口</span> | いりぐち | entrance |
| <span lang="ja">出口</span> | でぐち | exit |
| <span lang="ja">一階</span> | いっかい | first floor |
| <span lang="ja">二階</span> | にかい | second floor |
| <span lang="ja">右</span> | みぎ | right |
| <span lang="ja">左</span> | ひだり | left |

**Pronunciation and script notes:** 図書館 is としょかん, while 教室 is きょうしつ. 学校 is がっこう, with a small っ. 一階 is **いっかい** (not いちかい in normal usage); 二階 is **にかい**. 入口 is いりぐち and 出口 is でぐち. The character 口 has other readings in other words; do not assume a single universal reading. 右 is みぎ, left is 左 (ひだり).

## Original building guide

This building and its layout are invented for study.

| Floor | Facilities |
| --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">二階（にかい）</span> | <span lang="ja">図書館（としょかん）</span> and <span lang="ja">教室（きょうしつ）</span> |
| <span lang="ja">一階（いっかい）</span> | <span lang="ja">入口（いりぐち）</span> and <span lang="ja">出口（でぐち）</span> |

From the first-floor entrance, a sign points **右（みぎ）** toward the stairs. On the second floor, the library is **左（ひだり）** of the stairs. These clues are sufficient to find the library. The guide does *not* name a teacher or specify which classroom is in use; do not invent missing details.

## Read a short exchange

**Visitor:** <span lang="ja">図書館は どこですか。</span> (としょかんは どこですか。) — Where is the library?

**Staff:** <span lang="ja">図書館は 二階に あります。</span> (としょかんは にかいに あります。) — The library is on the second floor.

**Visitor:** <span lang="ja">ありがとうございます。</span> — Thank you.

The topic particle **は** is pronounced *wa*. **に** identifies the location, and **あります** states existence for a facility or inanimate thing. A short answer can also be <span lang="ja">二階です。</span> (“It's on the second floor”). This lesson assesses written recognition, not listening.

## Avoid these common mistakes

- **入口 vs 出口:** entrance versus exit. Both contain 口 but mean opposite movements through a doorway.
- **右 vs 左:** right versus left. Trace the directions with your hand as you read.
- **一階 vs 二階:** first floor versus second floor; pay attention to the small っ in いっかい.
- **学校 vs 教室:** a school is a whole institution or building; a classroom is one room.

## Controlled, guided and independent practice

**Controlled:** Cover the readings above. Read 図書館, 教室, 入口 and 出口; then uncover to check としょかん, きょうしつ, いりぐち and でぐち.

**Guided:** Find the exact sign proving the library is upstairs: **二階**. Find the sign for leaving the building: **出口**. Explain why 教室 does not mean the whole school.

**Independent:** Sketch your own two-floor building. Label floors 一階 and 二階, and place 図書館, 教室, 入口 and 出口. Write two sentences following <span lang="ja">図書館は 二階に あります。</span>. Check the placement of に against the model; this writing is self-reviewed, not automatically graded.

## Knowledge check

Answer ten written-recognition questions without referring to the table. After submission, review each explanation and retry as needed. The score does not certify pronunciation, handwriting or speaking.
