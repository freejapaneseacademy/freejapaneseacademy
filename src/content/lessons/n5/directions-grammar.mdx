## A short route to the library

You are outside a station. A visitor wants to find the library. The following conversation is fictional and written for this lesson.

**Visitor:** すみません。図書館（としょかん）はどこですか。  
*Excuse me. Where is the library?*

**Local:** この道（みち）をまっすぐ行（い）ってください。次（つぎ）の角（かど）を右（みぎ）に曲（ま）がってください。図書館は左（ひだり）にあります。  
*Please go straight along this road. Turn right at the next corner. The library is on the left.*

**Visitor:** ありがとうございます。  
*Thank you.*

Read the route twice. On your second reading, point to each action: straight, right turn, and finally the location on the left. This is written reading practice, not a recording.

## The three direction words

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 右 | みぎ | right | 右に曲がります。 — I turn right. |
| 左 | ひだり | left | 左に曲がります。 — I turn left. |
| まっすぐ | まっすぐ | straight ahead | まっすぐ行きます。 — I go straight. |
| 角 | かど | corner | 次の角 — the next corner |
| 交差点 | こうさてん | intersection | 交差点を右に曲がります。 — I turn right at the intersection. |
| 図書館 | としょかん | library | 図書館は左にあります。 — The library is on the left. |
| 駅 | えき | station | 駅はどこですか。 — Where is the station? |

A direction is relative to where the person is facing. **右** does not always mean the east side of a map.

## Grammar 1: movement versus direction

**右に曲がります** (みぎにまがります) means *turn right*. **左に曲がります** means *turn left*. Here **に** marks the direction of the turn.

**まっすぐ行きます** (まっすぐいきます) means *go straight*. Do not insert **に** after まっすぐ in this simple pattern: **まっすぐ行きます**, not *まっすぐに行きます* as the default teaching form (まっすぐに行きます is also grammatical in other contexts).

In **次の角を右に曲がります**, **を** marks the point or path at which the turn is made. It does not make 角 a person or an object being turned.

## Grammar 2: polite instructions

Use the **て-form + ください** for a polite request to take an action.

- 行きます → 行ってください (いってください) — Please go.
- 曲がります → 曲がってください (まがってください) — Please turn.
- まっすぐ行ってください。 — Please go straight.
- 次の角を左に曲がってください。 — Please turn left at the next corner.

**行ってください** is a request, whereas **行きます** is a statement. A person giving directions can use 〜てください without sounding like an angry command when the conversation is polite.

## Grammar 3: where something is

**図書館は左にあります。** (としょかんはひだりにあります) — *The library is on the left.*

The pattern **[landmark] は [place] にあります** states the location of a nonliving landmark or object. **銀行（ぎんこう）は駅のとなりにあります。** — *The bank is next to the station.*

Contrast the two meanings:

- **右に曲がってください。** — Please **turn** right. An action.
- **郵便局（ゆうびんきょく）は右にあります。** — The post office **is** on the right. A location.

A frequent mistake is **図書館は右に曲がります**, which suggests the library itself turns right. Use **あります** for a stationary library.

For people or animals, **います** is ordinarily used instead of **あります**. That distinction will receive more practice in later lessons.

## Controlled practice

Complete the missing phrase before checking the model:

1. *Please go straight.* → ＿＿＿＿行ってください。  
   **Model:** まっすぐ行ってください。
2. *Please turn left at the next corner.* → 次の角を＿＿＿＿曲がってください。  
   **Model:** 次の角を左に曲がってください。
3. *The station is on the right.* → 駅は＿＿＿＿あります。  
   **Model:** 駅は右にあります。

## Guided route building

Imagine a visitor at a bus stop. The park is reached by going straight and turning left at the next corner. The park is on the right after the turn.

**Model:** まっすぐ行ってください。次の角を左に曲がってください。公園（こうえん）は右にあります。  
*Please go straight. Turn left at the next corner. The park is on the right.*

Now change **左** and **右** to create a different route. Read it aloud only if you wish; no microphone or pronunciation grading is used.

## Independent use

Write three connected sentences giving directions from a station to a café, library, or park. Include one straight-ahead instruction, one turn, and one final location with **あります**. Check your writing against four criteria: (1) correct order of steps, (2) correct direction word, (3) correct **〜てください** request, and (4) **〜にあります** for the final landmark.
