## Your mission: plan a Saturday museum visit

Two friends are arranging a **fictional** Saturday outing. You will play both the person inviting and the person responding. Speaking is entirely optional: you may say the lines, read them silently or type your own version. This lesson **does not request microphone access or grade pronunciation**. For genuine recorded itinerary listening, revisit chapter lesson 06 when its licensed audio is available. The models on this page are printed Japanese, **not recordings**.

## 1. Build your travel toolkit

| Japanese | Reading | English and function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 土曜日に | どようびに | on Saturday; day of the outing |
| いっしょに行きませんか。 | いっしょに いきませんか | Would you like to go together? A polite invitation |
| いいですね。 | いいですね | Sounds good |
| 何時に会いますか。 | なんじに あいますか | What time shall we meet? |
| 九時半に駅で会いましょう。 | くじはんに えきで あいましょう | Let's meet at the station at 9:30 |
| 電車で行きます。 | でんしゃで いきます | We will go by train |
| 片道は三百円です。 | かたみちは さんびゃくえんです | One-way fare is 300 yen |
| すみません、十時でもいいですか。 | すみません、じゅうじでも いいですか | Sorry, would ten o'clock work instead? |

**Three different particles, three jobs.** In **九時半に駅で会いましょう**, **に** marks the meeting time and **で** marks where the meeting takes place. In **博物館に行きます**, **に** marks the destination. In **電車で行きます**, **で** marks the means of travel. A station can be a meeting place (駅で会う) or a destination (駅に行く), depending on the verb. Do not treat all place phrases as interchangeable.

**Fare caution:** 片道（かたみち）means *one way*. A 300-yen one-way fare is not automatically the full return-trip cost. In this fictional example, no discount or extra fees are assumed.

## 2. Model A: invitation and details

> **美咲（みさき）:** 土曜日に、いっしょに博物館（はくぶつかん）に行きませんか。  
> Would you like to go to the museum together on Saturday?
>
> **健（けん）:** いいですね。何時に会いますか。  
> Sounds good. What time shall we meet?
>
> **美咲:** 九時半に駅（えき）で会いましょう。  
> Let's meet at the station at 9:30.
>
> **健:** どうやって行きますか。  
> How will we get there?
>
> **美咲:** 電車（でんしゃ）で行きます。片道（かたみち）は三百円（さんびゃくえん）です。  
> We'll go by train. The one-way fare is 300 yen.
>
> **健:** わかりました。ありがとうございます。  
> Understood. Thank you.

Notice that **九時半** is the meeting time, not necessarily the train's departure time. The dialogue does not provide a train departure time. The fare is explicitly *one way*.

## 3. Model B: repair a meeting time

> **葵（あおい）:** 日曜日（にちようび）に公園（こうえん）に行きませんか。  
> Would you like to go to the park on Sunday?
>
> **春（はる）:** いいですね。九時に駅で会いますか。  
> Sounds good. Shall we meet at the station at nine?
>
> **葵:** すみません、十時でもいいですか。  
> Sorry, would ten o'clock work instead?
>
> **春:** はい、十時に駅で会いましょう。  
> Yes, let's meet at the station at ten.
>
> **葵:** バスで行きましょう。  
> Let's go by bus.

The **agreed** time is ten, not nine. The second speaker proposes nine; the first asks to change it. When you retell a plan, use the final agreed detail.

## 4. Controlled rehearsal

Read Model A once as 美咲 and once as 健. On the second pass, replace **博物館** with **図書館（としょかん）**, and replace **電車で** with **バスで**. Check that **に** still marks where you are going and **で** still marks the vehicle. You can practise without speaking aloud.

Then finish these three prompts before revealing the answer:

1. 九時半 ___ 駅 ___ 会いましょう。 → **に / で** (meeting time / meeting place)
2. 電車 ___ 博物館 ___ 行きます。 → **で / に** (transport / destination)
3. 片道は三百円です。 → **One way costs 300 yen**, not a confirmed round-trip price.

## 5. Guided partner exchange

Imagine you want to visit a library on Sunday. Invite a friend, agree to meet at the station at ten, and say you will go by bus. Use this frame, but supply your own details:

> A: 日曜日に、いっしょに図書館に行きませんか。  
> B: いいですね。何時に会いますか。  
> A: 十時に駅で会いましょう。  
> B: どうやって行きますか。  
> A: バスで行きます。  
> B: わかりました。

Change the time once: B asks **十一時でもいいですか** (Would eleven work instead?), and A agrees with **はい、十一時に会いましょう**. Rehearse the **final** plan, not the superseded one.

## 6. Independent role switch and self-review

Write a **six-to-eight-line exchange** between two people planning an outing. Include (1) an invitation with a destination, (2) a response, (3) an agreed meeting time and place, (4) a transport method, (5) a fare explicitly marked as one-way if you mention a fare, and (6) one clarification or polite closing. Swap roles and rehearse the exchange. Use the interactive draft box below; it stays in page memory only and is not uploaded.

Review these five criteria: **invitation**, **time and place particles**, **destination and transport particles**, **fare accuracy**, and **clear agreement or repair**. Your self-check is formative and is **not a verified oral assessment**. The written recognition questions below test the meanings of expressions, not your pronunciation.

## 7. Continue practising

Revisit the travel notice in L05 and the authentic itinerary listening in L06 when available. Compare your spoken or written plan with the notice: does it distinguish meeting time, departure time and one-way fare? For later recall, repeat the exchange with a different destination and vehicle.
