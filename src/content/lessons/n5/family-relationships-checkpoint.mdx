## Your mission: welcome a family at a school exhibition

You have met Mika's family. Now show that you can introduce a relative politely, read a small family chart, and recognize the difference between a relationship and an occupation. This checkpoint uses fictional original academy material. It is **practice**, not an official JLPT examination.

### 1. Retrieve the essential patterns

- <span lang="ja">こちらは、わたしの母（はは）です。</span> — This is my mother. **こちら** is suitable for a polite introduction of a person.
- <span lang="ja">ミカさんの妹（いもうと）</span> — Mika's younger sister. **AのB** links the person A to the relationship B.
- <span lang="ja">その人（ひと）は、だれですか。</span> — Who is that person? **その** modifies **人**, a person already in focus, such as someone in a photograph.
- <span lang="ja">先生（せんせい）じゃありません。会社員（かいしゃいん）です。</span> — (The person) is not a teacher. (They) are a company employee.

**Register matters:** When speaking about your own mother to someone outside the family, **母（はは）** is conventional; when politely referring to someone else's mother, **お母（かあ）さん** is natural. Similarly, **兄（あに）** is used for your own older brother, whereas **お兄（にい）さん** is respectful when referring to someone else's older brother. Actual household address terms vary.

### 2. Read the family chart

This is a written chart, not a recording. Read each card and locate the exact phrase that proves the answer.

| Card | Japanese evidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | <span lang="ja">ミカさんの妹は、ユイさんです。</span> | Mika's younger sister is Yui. |
| B | <span lang="ja">こちらは、わたしの母、さちこです。</span> | This is my mother, Sachiko. |
| C | <span lang="ja">その人は、ミカさんの兄、けんさんです。</span> | That person is Mika's older brother, Ken. |
| D | <span lang="ja">けんさんは、先生じゃありません。会社員です。</span> | Ken is not a teacher. He is a company employee. |

**Reading strategy:** Circle the name before **の**. Underline the relationship after **の**. In card D, do not overlook the negative **じゃありません**: a rejected guess is not the correct occupation.

### 3. Repair common mistakes

| Intended meaning | Problem | Better expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mika's younger sister | <span lang="ja">妹のミカさん</span> | <span lang="ja">ミカさんの妹</span> |
| This is my mother (formal introduction) | <span lang="ja">これは、わたしの母です。</span> | <span lang="ja">こちらは、わたしの母です。</span> |
| That person is my older brother | <span lang="ja">そのは、わたしの兄です。</span> | <span lang="ja">その人は、わたしの兄です。</span> |

**Why:** Reversing **の** reverses the relationship. **これ** normally refers to an object rather than politely presenting a person. **その** must modify a noun.

### 4. Listening transfer — two family conversations

The chapter's original L06 contains two distinct tracks. Track A introduces a younger sister and says she is a high school student. Listening questions are unlocked only when **both genuine recordings** report playable audio. ### 5. Guided writing (ungraded)

Imagine that you are introducing two fictional relatives at a school event. Write **three connected Japanese sentences**: introduce one person with **こちら**, state whose relative they are with **の**, and describe a person in a photograph using **その人**.

Model: <span lang="ja">こちらは、わたしの妹、ユイです。その人は、わたしの兄、けんです。けんは会社員です。</span>

Meaning: This is my younger sister Yui. That person is my older brother Ken. Ken is a company employee.

**Self-check rubric:** (1) Is the family relationship attached to the correct person? (2) Did you use **こちら** for an introduction and **その人** for the photographed person? (3) Does each sentence end naturally? (4) Are family terms appropriate to who is speaking? Other natural answers can be correct; this writing is not automatically graded.
