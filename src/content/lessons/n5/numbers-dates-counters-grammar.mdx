## Your task: understand the community-center schedule

A community center has classes and events on different dates, at different times, and with different quantities of materials or people. In this lesson, read a timetable and tell a classmate **when** an event starts and **how many** items or people are involved. This is written grammar practice; real spoken date announcements are taught in L06.

### 1. Dates: month + day

Japanese writes dates in the order **month → day**. The month is a number followed by 月（がつ）: 4月（しがつ, April）, 7月（しちがつ, July）, 9月（くがつ, September）. Many other months follow the familiar number reading, such as 3月（さんがつ）.

Days of the month often use special readings. Learn these as whole words rather than trying to attach にち to every number.

| Date | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1日 | ついたち | first day of the month |
| 2日 | ふつか | second |
| 3日 | みっか | third |
| 4日 | よっか | fourth |
| 5日 | いつか | fifth |
| 6日 | むいか | sixth |
| 7日 | なのか | seventh |
| 8日 | ようか | eighth |
| 9日 | ここのか | ninth |
| 10日 | とおか | tenth |
| 14日 | じゅうよっか | fourteenth |
| 20日 | はつか | twentieth |
| 24日 | にじゅうよっか | twenty-fourth |

For example, <span lang="ja">4月4日（しがつ よっか）</span> means April 4. **1日** is read **ついたち** when it means the first day of a month; **いちにち** can mean one day as a duration. Do not confuse these uses.

### 2. Time: 時 and 分

<span lang="ja">3時（さんじ）</span> means 3 o'clock, <span lang="ja">7時（しちじ）</span> means 7 o'clock, and <span lang="ja">9時（くじ）</span> means 9 o'clock. The readings of 4時（よじ） and 9時（くじ） are especially important. <span lang="ja">3時半（さんじはん）</span> is 3:30.

Minutes take **分**, whose sound varies: <span lang="ja">5分（ごふん）</span>, <span lang="ja">10分（じゅっぷん）</span>, <span lang="ja">15分（じゅうごふん）</span>. Thus <span lang="ja">2時10分（にじ じゅっぷん）</span> is 2:10. Write a time without に when simply labeling a schedule; use **に** when saying **at** a time:

- <span lang="ja">じゅぎょうは 3時に はじまります。</span> — The class starts at three.
- <span lang="ja">じゅぎょうは 3時半に はじまります。</span> — The class starts at half past three.
- <span lang="ja">なんじに はじまりますか。</span> — At what time does it start?

The verb はじまります means “starts” (an event begins). **に** marks the specified start time. In everyday Japanese, relative time words such as **きょう** (today) usually do not take this time-marker に.

### 3. Choose the counter that fits

| What you count | One | Two | Three |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General items with 〜つ | 一つ（ひとつ） | 二つ（ふたつ） | 三つ（みっつ） |
| People with 人 | 一人（ひとり） | 二人（ふたり） | 三人（さんにん） |
| Flat items with 枚 | 一枚（いちまい） | 二枚（にまい） | 三枚（さんまい） |

Use <span lang="ja">りんごが 二つ あります。</span> for two apples, <span lang="ja">せんせいが 二人 います。</span> for two teachers, and <span lang="ja">かみが 三枚 あります。</span> for three sheets of paper. **人** counts people; **枚** counts flat things such as paper and tickets. The general 〜つ series is useful for small quantities of many objects, but it is not the normal way to count people. Other counters exist; you only need these three families here.

The question words follow the counter: <span lang="ja">いくつ</span> (how many general items), <span lang="ja">なんにん</span> (how many people), <span lang="ja">なんまい</span> (how many flat items).

### 4. Original reading: a small event timetable

This is a fictional community center. Every fact in the questions below comes from this timetable.

| Date | Start | Event | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">4月4日</span> | <span lang="ja">9時</span> | <span lang="ja">えの じゅぎょう</span> (drawing class) | <span lang="ja">せんせいが 二人 います。かみが 三枚 あります。</span> |
| <span lang="ja">4月7日</span> | <span lang="ja">2時10分</span> | <span lang="ja">にほんごの じゅぎょう</span> (Japanese class) | <span lang="ja">せんせいが 一人 います。</span> |
| <span lang="ja">4月10日</span> | <span lang="ja">3時半</span> | <span lang="ja">おかしの じかん</span> (snack time) | <span lang="ja">りんごが 二つ あります。</span> |

Read the first row aloud with written pronunciation support: <span lang="ja">しがつ よっか、くじ、えの じゅぎょう。</span> The drawing class is on April 4 at nine. There are two teachers and three sheets of paper.

**Short exchange (written, not audio):**

**A:** <span lang="ja">にほんごの じゅぎょうは いつですか。</span> — When is the Japanese class?

**B:** <span lang="ja">4月7日です。</span> — April 7.

**A:** <span lang="ja">なんじに はじまりますか。</span> — At what time does it start?

**B:** <span lang="ja">2時10分に はじまります。</span> — It starts at 2:10.

**A:** <span lang="ja">せんせいは なんにん いますか。</span> — How many teachers are there?

**B:** <span lang="ja">一人 います。</span> — One.

### 5. Repair common errors

- **4月4日** is **しがつ よっか**, not *よんがつ よんにち*. Both the month and day have special readings.
- **4時** is **よじ**, not *よんじ*. The same numeral can sound different before different counters.
- **二人** is **ふたり**, not *ににん*. From 三人 onward, **さんにん** is regular for this level.
- **かみが 三人 あります** counts paper as people. Use <span lang="ja">かみが 三枚 あります。</span>
- **3時に はじまります** uses に for a specific time; **3時が はじまります** does not mean “starts at three.”

### 6. Controlled → guided → independent practice

**Controlled:** Choose the correct reading for <span lang="ja">4月7日</span>: **しがつ なのか**. Complete <span lang="ja">かみが 三___ あります。</span> with **枚**.

**Guided:** Use the timetable to answer <span lang="ja">おかしの じかんは なんじに はじまりますか。</span> A model answer is <span lang="ja">3時半に はじまります。</span> Then answer <span lang="ja">りんごは いくつ ありますか。</span> — <span lang="ja">二つ あります。</span>

**Independent writing:** Invent a *different fictional* community-center event. Write **three connected Japanese sentences**: one stating the date, one stating the start time with に, and one stating a quantity using the appropriate counter. Example: <span lang="ja">えの じゅぎょうは 4月8日です。10時に はじまります。かみが 二枚 あります。</span> Self-review your date reading, time particle, counter and the match between あります/います and what exists. Many different accurate answers are possible; free writing is **not automatically graded**.

**Optional speaking:** Read the model exchange aloud, then substitute your own event date and start time. This lesson neither records speech nor grades pronunciation. Listening without a visible transcript belongs to L06.

### 7. Formative practice

Complete twelve original questions on dates, times, counters and the timetable. Review the answer explanations, then retry missed items. This is practice, not an official proficiency score.
