## Your mission: choose supplies at a stationery shop

**Can-do:** Ask about prices, amounts and sizes politely. You are shopping for notebooks and paper at a fictional shop. The goal here is to learn the words you need before the next grammar lesson explains the request patterns in more depth. This is written word study; the chapter's L06 will teach listening with authentic recorded audio.

## 1. Twelve words and expressions

Read the kana as well as the written form. The price and stock details below are fictional.

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning | Shop example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">円</span> | えん | yen; currency counter | <span lang="ja">三百円です。</span> — It is 300 yen. |
| <span lang="ja">いくら</span> | いくら | how much (price) | <span lang="ja">これはいくらですか。</span> — How much is this? |
| <span lang="ja">値段</span> | ねだん | price | <span lang="ja">値段は百円です。</span> — The price is 100 yen. |
| <span lang="ja">赤</span> | あか | red | <span lang="ja">赤いノートです。</span> — It is a red notebook. |
| <span lang="ja">青</span> | あお | blue | <span lang="ja">青いノートです。</span> — It is a blue notebook. |
| <span lang="ja">白</span> | しろ | white | <span lang="ja">白い紙です。</span> — It is white paper. |
| <span lang="ja">大きい</span> | おおきい | big / large | <span lang="ja">大きいノートです。</span> — It is a large notebook. |
| <span lang="ja">小さい</span> | ちいさい | small | <span lang="ja">小さいノートです。</span> — It is a small notebook. |
| <span lang="ja">一つ</span> | ひとつ | one item (general counter) | <span lang="ja">消しゴムを一つください。</span> — One eraser, please. |
| <span lang="ja">二つ</span> | ふたつ | two items (general counter) | <span lang="ja">消しゴムを二つください。</span> — Two erasers, please. |
| <span lang="ja">一冊</span> | いっさつ | one bound book/notebook | <span lang="ja">ノートを一冊ください。</span> — One notebook, please. |
| <span lang="ja">一枚</span> | いちまい | one flat sheet | <span lang="ja">紙を一枚ください。</span> — One sheet of paper, please. |

**Reading note:** 円 is written after the number: **百円** (ひゃくえん), **二百円** (にひゃくえん), **三百円** (さんびゃくえん). In this lesson, prices are simple examples, not real store quotations. Japanese often uses a counter that matches the object. **冊** counts bound books and notebooks; **枚** counts flat sheets; **つ** is a useful general-purpose counter for many objects. The readings **ひとつ・ふたつ・いっさつ・いちまい** must be learned as whole words, not assembled from English number words.

## 2. A color word is not always an adjective

**赤** (あか), **青** (あお), **白** (しろ) name colors. Before a noun, the common descriptive forms are **赤い** (あかい), **青い** (あおい), and **白い** (しろい).

- <span lang="ja">赤いノート</span> (あかいノート) — a red notebook.
- <span lang="ja">青いペン</span> (あおいペン) — a blue pen.
- <span lang="ja">白い紙</span> (しろいかみ) — white paper.

**大きい** and **小さい** also go directly before nouns: <span lang="ja">大きいノート</span> (large notebook), <span lang="ja">小さいノート</span> (small notebook). Avoid **赤ノート** as your default beginner pattern; learn **赤いノート**. Not all Japanese color words work the same way, so do not assume every color noun simply adds い.

## 3. Original fictional price tags

| Tag | Item | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | <span lang="ja">赤いノート・小さい</span> (red, small notebook) | <span lang="ja">百円</span> — ¥100 |
| B | <span lang="ja">青いノート・大きい</span> (blue, large notebook) | <span lang="ja">三百円</span> — ¥300 |
| C | <span lang="ja">白い紙・一枚</span> (one sheet of white paper) | <span lang="ja">十円</span> — ¥10 |
| D | <span lang="ja">消しゴム・一つ</span> (one eraser) | <span lang="ja">五十円</span> — ¥50 |

The tags show **one unit's price** for each entry. Do not treat the ¥10 sheet as the price of an entire pack.

## 4. A short shop exchange

> **Customer:** <span lang="ja">すみません。これはいくらですか。</span> (Excuse me. How much is this?)
>
> **Clerk:** <span lang="ja">三百円です。</span> (It is 300 yen.)
>
> **Customer:** <span lang="ja">青いノートを一冊お願いします。</span> (One blue notebook, please.)
>
> **Clerk:** <span lang="ja">はい。</span> (Certainly.)

The customer asks the price before ordering. **お願いします** (おねがいします) is a polite request expression. **ください** is another useful polite request form: <span lang="ja">ノートを一冊ください。</span> (One notebook, please.) The next lesson examines the grammar of these expressions. **いくら** asks the amount of money; **何冊** (なんさつ) asks how many bound books, not the price.

## 5. Three easy mix-ups

**一つ versus 一冊:** <span lang="ja">ノートを一冊</span> specifies one bound notebook. <span lang="ja">消しゴムを一つ</span> uses the general counter for one eraser. The general counter is not a universal substitute for all specialist counters.

**一枚 versus 一冊:** A loose sheet of paper is <span lang="ja">紙を一枚</span>. A notebook made of bound pages is <span lang="ja">ノートを一冊</span>. Both are paper products, but their physical forms differ.

**いくら versus 何:** <span lang="ja">これはいくらですか。</span> asks the price. <span lang="ja">これは何ですか。</span> asks what the object is. The questions are not interchangeable.

## 6. Controlled → guided → independent practice

**Controlled:** Cover the English meanings and identify the words for *red*, *small*, *yen*, *two items*, and *one sheet*. Then read **百円・三百円・一冊・一枚** using their kana.

**Guided:** Using the fictional tags, answer: Which notebook is blue and large? **Tag B.** What is its price? **三百円**. Which item costs **十円**? **白い紙・一枚**, Tag C. Give the evidence phrase, not a guess based on the color.

**Repair the deliberate errors:** <span lang="ja">青ノート</span> → <span lang="ja">青いノート</span> (in this beginner description). <span lang="ja">紙を一冊ください</span> → <span lang="ja">紙を一枚ください</span> (for one loose sheet). <span lang="ja">ノートを一枚ください</span> → <span lang="ja">ノートを一冊ください</span> (for one bound notebook).

**Independent transfer:** Write two short lines as a fictional customer: ask a price and request an item with a quantity. Model: <span lang="ja">この青いノートはいくらですか。青いノートを一冊ください。</span> — How much is this blue notebook? One blue notebook, please. Check your color ending, counter and polite request. Other natural answers are possible; this writing is self-reviewed, not machine-graded.

**Optional speaking rehearsal:** Read the customer and clerk lines aloud, changing **青い** to **赤い** and **三百円** to **百円**. No audio or pronunciation grade is claimed here.
