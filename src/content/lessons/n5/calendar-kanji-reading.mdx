## A calendar pinned by the entrance

**Chapter can-do:** Understand dates, time and basic counters. This is an original **written** lesson. No recording or pronunciation assessment is provided.

At a neighborhood learning center, staff member 佐藤（さとう） writes a notice for visitors. Read the Japanese before using the English.

> **今月（こんげつ）のイベント** — This month's events  
> **火曜日（かようび）**：11月（じゅういちがつ）10日（とおか）、午後（ごご）2時（にじ）から。日本語（にほんご）クラス。4人（よにん）。  
> **木曜日（もくようび）**：11月12日（じゅうににち）、午前（ごぜん）9時（くじ）から。工作（こうさく）クラス。紙（かみ）3枚（さんまい）をつかいます。  
> **土曜日（どようび）**：11月14日（じゅうよっか）、午前10時（じゅうじ）から。書道（しょどう）クラス。2人（ふたり）。

The calendar is fictional; the weekday/date pairings describe an imagined November, not this year's calendar. The three events are Japanese class (Tuesday, four people), craft class (Thursday, three sheets of paper used), and calligraphy class (Saturday, two people).

## 1. Weekday kanji in actual words

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning | Character clue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 日曜日 | にちようび | Sunday | 日: sun/day |
| 月曜日 | げつようび | Monday | 月: moon/month |
| 火曜日 | かようび | Tuesday | 火: fire |
| 水曜日 | すいようび | Wednesday | 水: water |
| 木曜日 | もくようび | Thursday | 木: tree/wood |
| 金曜日 | きんようび | Friday | 金: gold/metal |
| 土曜日 | どようび | Saturday | 土: earth/soil |

**曜（よう）** appears in every weekday name. **日（び）** closes each weekday expression; its reading here is not the same as 日 in every other word. Learn whole words, not one universal pronunciation per character. In **日曜日（にちようび）**, 日 appears twice with different readings.

**月** in **11月（じゅういちがつ）** marks a *month*, whereas **月曜日（げつようび）** names *Monday*. The word determines the reading.

## 2. Read calendar and quantity signs

| Printed form | Reading | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11月 | じゅういちがつ | November |
| 10日 | とおか | the tenth day |
| 12日 | じゅうににち | the twelfth day |
| 14日 | じゅうよっか | the fourteenth day |
| 午前9時 | ごぜんくじ | 9 a.m. |
| 午後2時 | ごごにじ | 2 p.m. |
| 4人 | よにん | four people |
| 2人 | ふたり | two people |
| 3枚 | さんまい | three sheets |

**時（じ）** marks an hour on the clock; **人（にん）** counts people, with irregular forms including **ひとり** and **ふたり**. **枚（まい）** counts flat objects such as sheets. The general counter **〜つ** (ひとつ, ふたつ) is useful for many objects, but paper is more specifically counted with 枚. A written **2時** is *two o'clock*, not *two people*.

The number 4 in **4人** is normally **よにん**, not **しにん**. **9時** is **くじ**, not **きゅうじ**. Some date readings are irregular, especially **10日（とおか）** and **14日（じゅうよっか）**.

## 3. Read the short exchange

| Speaker | Japanese | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor | 日本語クラスは何曜日（なんようび）ですか。 | What day of the week is Japanese class? |
| Staff | 火曜日です。 | Tuesday. |
| Visitor | 何時（なんじ）からですか。 | What time does it start? |
| Staff | 午後2時からです。 | It starts at 2 p.m. |
| Visitor | 何人（なんにん）ですか。 | How many people? |
| Staff | 4人です。 | Four people. |

**から** marks the starting point, not an ending time. **何曜日** asks for a weekday; **何日（なんにち）** asks for a day of the month; **何時** asks for a clock time; **何人** asks for a number of people.

## 4. Look-alike meaning traps

**Common mistake 1:** reading **木曜日** as Tuesday because both are weekday names. Look at the *first* character: 火 = Tuesday, 木 = Thursday.

**Common mistake 2:** saying **3人** for three sheets of paper. The craft notice says **紙3枚**, not three participants. A number without its counter does not tell you what is counted.

**Common mistake 3:** treating **11月12日** as 11:12. **月** and **日** make a calendar date; **時** marks clock time.

## 5. Controlled practice

Without looking at the English translation, find the exact characters in the notice that answer these questions.

1. Which weekday is the craft class? **Evidence:** 木曜日.
2. What is the calligraphy class date? **Evidence:** 11月14日.
3. How many people are listed for Japanese class? **Evidence:** 4人.
4. What quantity of paper is mentioned? **Evidence:** 紙3枚.

## 6. Guided transformation

Change the weekday and quantity while keeping the correct counter.

- Model: 火曜日です。4人です。 (It is Tuesday. Four people.)
- New details: Friday; two people → **金曜日です。2人です。**
- New details: Monday; three sheets of paper → **月曜日です。紙は3枚です。**

The second line describes paper, not the number of people attending. Try reading each new sentence aloud only as optional rehearsal; there is no audio comparison or automatic speech grade.

## 7. Independent use

Write a two- or three-line notice for an imaginary event with (1) a weekday, (2) a month and date, (3) a start time and (4) either people or sheets. Example for a different event:

> 金曜日（きんようび）、12月（じゅうにがつ）4日（よっか）。  
> 午前（ごぜん）10時（じゅうじ）からです。  
> 参加（さんか）は2人（ふたり）です。

**Self-check:** Did you use 曜日 for the weekday? 月 and 日 for the date? 時 for the clock hour? 人 for people or 枚 for paper? Your writing is not automatically graded.

## 8. Check your kanji reading

Use the interactive practice to distinguish written forms, readings and quantities. These questions assess written recognition only. Listening to date announcements comes later in L06.
