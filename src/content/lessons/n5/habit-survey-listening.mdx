## Why this listening matters

In a short **アンケート** (survey), a question such as **よく本を読みますか** asks whether someone reads books often. A useful answer may include an adverb that tells you the frequency, even if you miss a few nouns. This lesson develops comprehension of **spoken** habits, not recognition of printed Japanese.

You will hear two original conversations with alternating male and female voices. Track A is a library survey about reading and studying; Track B is an exercise survey about walking, running and yoga. Listen for the **topic**, **habit**, **frequency**, and whether the answer is positive or negative.

### Listen for the contrast

| Expression | Approximate meaning | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| いつも | always | An affirmative habit here |
| よく | often | A frequent habit |
| ときどき | sometimes | An occasional habit |
| あまり〜ません | not much / not often | A negative ending is essential |
| ぜんぜん〜ません | not at all | A negative ending is essential |

**Important:** The frequency words are not exact numerical percentages. **あまり** and **ぜんぜん** in these beginner examples pair with a negative verb. **ぜんぜん走りません** means “I don't run at all,” not “I run very often.” **よく公園を歩きます** uses **を** with a path of movement; it does not mean the park is the object being carried.

### Track A — A library survey

A woman asks a man about reading and studying. Before playing, predict the kinds of answers that a library survey might elicit. On the first listen, notice whether he describes books or studying as a regular habit. On the second listen, distinguish **夜** (night) from **朝** (morning), and hear the negative ending **〜ません**.

### Track B — An exercise survey

A man asks a woman about exercise. Listen for the contrast between **歩きます** (walk), **走ります** (run) and **ヨガをします** (do yoga). These are different activities: hearing one does not establish the others.

## A three-pass listening method

1. **First pass:** Play each audio track without opening the transcript. Identify the main topic and the speakers' habits. You do not need to understand every word.
2. **Second pass:** Listen for frequency adverbs and the final verb ending. Answer all eight questions using what you heard.
3. **Third pass:** Submit, then reveal Japanese and English transcripts to check evidence. Replay and optionally shadow one short answer, without recording anything.

## Review after listening

Compare **いつも夜に本を読みます** (I always read at night) with **朝はあまり勉強しません** (I don't study much in the morning). These sentences describe **different actions and times**; the survey is not contradictory.

In Track B, **よく公園を歩きます** (I often walk in the park) and **ぜんぜん走りません** (I never run) are also compatible: walking and running are different activities. Finally, **ときどき家でヨガをします** identifies a third habit.

### Optional spoken rehearsal

After revealing the transcript, replay a single short answer and repeat it at a comfortable pace. You can also make your own two-sentence answer, for example: **よく本を読みます。でも、あまりテレビを見ません。** (“I often read books, but I don't watch much TV.”) This is optional rehearsal, **not** a pronunciation score.
