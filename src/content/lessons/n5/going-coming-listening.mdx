## Your listening mission

Two fictional conversations follow people travelling to a library and a park. **Listen for the route, the vehicle and the time**. Your first attempt should be audio-only: the questions never ask you to identify written kanji or spellings.

### Prepare your ears

Recall these familiar phrases before playback, without looking at the conversation transcript:

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 行きます | いきます | go |
| 来ます | きます | come (toward a reference point) |
| 帰ります | かえります | return home / to a familiar place |
| 駅 | えき | station |
| 図書館 | としょかん | library |
| 公園 | こうえん | park |
| 電車で | でんしゃで | by train |
| バスで | バスで | by bus |
| 何時 | なんじ | what time |

**へ / に** can indicate a destination. **で** after a vehicle tells you *how* someone travels. **五時に帰ります** means “I return at five”; it does not mean “I go to five.”

### Round one: listen for the main idea

Play **Track A: Meeting at the station** once. Ask yourself: What is the overall plan? Play **Track B: Returning from the park** once. Who is travelling, and what do they plan to do?

### Round two: find the details

Replay each track and answer the six questions below. Notice the difference between **十時 (10 o'clock)** and **五時 (5 o'clock)**. In Track B, the two speakers give *different* return times; listen for whose time the question asks about. Do not guess a travel method from a destination.

### After submitting: verify evidence

Reveal the transcript only after your blind attempt if you want an unaided listening score. Revealing a transcript beforehand switches the attempt to **assisted practice**, which is not stored as blind listening mastery. Find the exact phrase supporting each answer, then replay that part.

### Independent writing and optional speaking

Without the transcript, write two short Japanese sentences about your own fictional journey, such as **あした、電車で図書館へ行きます。五時に家へ帰ります。** (“Tomorrow I'll go to the library by train. I'll return home at five.”) Check that your destination takes **へ/に**, your vehicle takes **で**, and your return uses **帰ります**. Optionally shadow one line after listening. This is self-practice, not evaluated pronunciation; no microphone or recording is requested.

### Review

If you confuse a time, listen again for **時**. If you confuse **行きます** and **帰ります**, ask whether the person is departing for a destination or returning home. Record one weak expression to review later.
