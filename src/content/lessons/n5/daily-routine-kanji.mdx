## A note on the refrigerator

**Chapter can-do:** Describe a usual day using polite present verbs. This is a written kanji-recognition lesson, not a pronunciation recording. Read the Japanese first, then use the kana and English.

大学生（だいがくせい）のミナさんは、家族（かぞく）に一日の予定（よてい）を見せます。Mina, a university student, leaves a note for her family about her usual day.

> **毎日（まいにち）の生活（せいかつ）**  
> 朝（あさ）6時（ろくじ）に起（お）きます。  
> 7時（しちじ）にパンを食（た）べます。水（みず）を飲（の）みます。  
> 8時（はちじ）に学校（がっこう）へ行（い）きます。  
> 午後（ごご）5時（ごじ）に本（ほん）を読（よ）みます。  
> 夜（よる）9時（くじ）に日記（にっき）を書（か）きます。  
> 夜はテレビを見（み）ません。

**English:** Every day, I get up at six in the morning. At seven, I eat bread and drink water. At eight, I go to school. At five in the afternoon, I read a book. At nine at night, I write in my diary. I do not watch television at night.

The note describes a **habit**, not a past event. The final negative sentence is intentional: Mina does not watch TV at night.

## 1. Recognize action kanji in whole words

| Printed word | Reading | Meaning | Look for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 起きます | おきます | get up | 起, to rise |
| 食べます | たべます | eat | 食, eating |
| 飲みます | のみます | drink | 飲, drinking |
| 行きます | いきます | go | 行, movement |
| 見ます | みます | see / watch | 見, seeing |
| 読みます | よみます | read | 読, reading |
| 書きます | かきます | write | 書, writing |

Learn **the full written verb together with its kana ending**. The kanji does not encode the whole polite verb: 起きます contains 起 plus きます. The kanji **食** in 食べます is read た, while **食事（しょくじ）** uses a different reading. A character can have several readings depending on its word.

These are recognition targets in this lesson; copying the character slowly is optional. Accurate reading and meaning in context matter more than memorizing every stroke immediately.

## 2. Recognize time words

| Word | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 朝 | あさ | morning |
| 夜 | よる | night |
| 毎日 | まいにち | every day |
| 午後 | ごご | p.m. / afternoon |
| 6時 | ろくじ | six o'clock |
| 9時 | くじ | nine o'clock |

**毎日** is a recurring-time expression: 毎日、学校へ行きます。 means “I go to school every day.” **朝** and **夜** describe parts of the day. In this note, **朝6時に** specifies a clock time, while **夜は** introduces the topic “as for nighttime.”

## 3. How the kanji fit the sentence

**朝6時に起きます。** (あさ ろくじ に おきます。) — I get up at six in the morning. **に** marks the specific clock time.

**パンを食べます。** (パン を たべます。) — I eat bread. **を** marks the direct object of the action.

**学校へ行きます。** (がっこう へ いきます。) — I go to school. **へ** indicates direction. You may also encounter 学校に行きます, with に marking a destination.

**テレビを見ません。** (テレビ を みません。) — I do not watch television. **ません** makes the polite nonpast verb negative; it does **not** mean “did not watch.” That past-negative form is learned later.

## 4. Common reading traps

- **飲みます** means “drink,” not “eat.” In the note, 水を飲みます is drinking water, whereas パンを食べます is eating bread.
- **読みます** and **書きます** are different activities. Mina reads a book (**本を読みます**) and writes a diary (**日記を書きます**).
- **見ません** is negative. Do not overlook the kana **ません** when recognizing 見.
- **9時** is read **くじ**, not きゅうじ. Read the whole time expression, not each digit in isolation.

## 5. Controlled reading practice

Find the evidence in Mina's note before checking the English.

1. Which characters show “get up”? **起きます**.
2. Which written phrase shows “drink water”? **水を飲みます**.
3. Which action occurs at 午後5時? **本を読みます**.
4. Which activity does Mina *not* do at night? **テレビを見ません**.

## 6. Guided substitution

Keep the correct verb and particle when changing the object or time.

| Prompt | Japanese model | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| read a book at 7 | 7時に本を読みます。 | I read a book at seven. |
| drink water at 8 | 8時に水を飲みます。 | I drink water at eight. |
| do not watch TV at night | 夜はテレビを見ません。 | I do not watch TV at night. |

Now replace **本** with **日記** in the first sentence. **7時に日記を読みます。** means “I read a diary at seven.” Notice that 読みます remains “read”; 書きます would mean “write.”

## 7. Independent use and review

Write three short sentences about an **imagined** usual day: a time you get up, one thing you eat or drink, and one thing you read, watch or write. Use at least two action kanji from the table. You can write in the optional practice box below.

**Self-review:** Is each kanji paired with its correct kana ending? Did you use **に** with an exact time and **を** with an object? Did you choose **ます** for an affirmative habit or **ません** for a negative one? Compare your answer to the model in the practice panel. Writing is ungraded.
