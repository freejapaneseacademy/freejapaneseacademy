## A conversation about everyday actions

**Can-do:** Tell a partner which activities you do, and recognize the corresponding dictionary forms on an activity card. This is an **optional** speaking rehearsal: you can read aloud, whisper, or type instead. There is no microphone requirement and no automatic pronunciation grade. The chapter's L06 provides separate listening material; the dialogues below are **printed models, not new recorded audio**.

Imagine two learners planning activities at a community center. A posted list uses short dictionary-form labels, such as **本を読む** (read a book), while people speaking politely normally say **本を読みます** (I read a book / I will read a book, depending on context). The dictionary form is not inherently a command, and the ます form is not automatically future tense.

## Step 1 — Notice the verb pairs

| Dictionary form (plain nonpast) | Reading | Polite nonpast | Group | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 読む | よむ | 読みます（よみます） | 1 | read |
| 書く | かく | 書きます（かきます） | 1 | write |
| 飲む | のむ | 飲みます（のみます） | 1 | drink |
| 行く | いく | 行きます（いきます） | 1 | go |
| 買う | かう | 買います（かいます） | 1 | buy |
| 見る | みる | 見ます（みます） | 2 | see/watch |
| 食べる | たべる | 食べます（たべます） | 2 | eat |
| 起きる | おきる | 起きます（おきます） | 2 | wake up |
| する | する | します | 3 (irregular) | do |
| 来る | くる | 来ます（きます） | 3 (irregular) | come |

Group 1 verbs change their final **u-row** sound to an **i-row** sound before ます: 読**む** → 読**み**ます. Group 2 verbs here drop **る** before ます: 食べる → 食べます. Group 3 forms must be learned separately: する → します; 来る（くる）→ 来ます（きます）. **Warning:** not every verb ending in る is Group 2; 帰る（かえる）→ 帰ります（かえります） is Group 1.

## Step 2 — Read the first model (A and B)

**A:** 土曜日（どようび）に何（なに）をしますか。  
*What will you do on Saturday?*

**B:** 午前（ごぜん）は本（ほん）を読みます。午後（ごご）は絵（え）を見ます。  
*In the morning I'll read a book. In the afternoon I'll look at pictures.*

**A:** いいですね。私（わたし）は名前（なまえ）を書きます。  
*That sounds good. I'll write names.*

**B:** このカードの「読む」は「読みます」と同（おな）じ動詞（どうし）ですか。  
*Is “読む” on this card the same verb as “読みます”?*

**A:** はい。同じ動詞です。「読む」は辞書形（じしょけい）です。  
*Yes, it's the same verb. “読む” is the dictionary form.*

**Noticing:** 何をしますか is a polite question. 本を読みます is a polite statement. The quoted label 「読む」 is a dictionary form; it does not mean the speaker is being rude. 午前 and 午後 locate the activities in time.

## Step 3 — Read the second model: a correction

**A:** 日曜日（にちようび）は何（なに）をしますか。  
*What will you do on Sunday?*

**B:** 朝（あさ）は早（はや）く起きます。それから図書館（としょかん）に行きます。  
*I'll wake up early in the morning. Then I'll go to the library.*

**A:** 「起きます」の辞書形は「起きる」ですか。  
*Is the dictionary form of “起きます” “起きる”?*

**B:** はい。グループ２です。  
*Yes. It's Group 2.*

**A:** 「帰ります」も「帰る」ですね。グループ２ですか。  
*“帰ります” also comes from “帰る,” right? Is that Group 2?*

**B:** いいえ。「帰る」はグループ１です。  
*No. “帰る” is Group 1.*

The correction matters: **る** alone does not identify a verb group. Group labels are learning tools for conjugation, not something speakers must say in everyday conversation.

## Step 4 — Controlled rehearsal

Read each pair twice. First read the card label; then say the polite line. If you prefer, type it instead.

1. **本を読む** → 午前九時に本を読みます。 *(I read / will read a book at 9 a.m.)*
2. **絵を見る** → 午前十時に絵を見ます。 *(I look / will look at pictures at 10 a.m.)*
3. **名前を書く** → 午後一時に名前を書きます。 *(I write / will write names at 1 p.m.)*
4. **お茶を飲む** → 午後二時にお茶を飲みます。 *(I drink / will drink tea at 2 p.m.)*

Do not add ます directly to a dictionary form: **読むます** is not the polite form of 読む. Use **読みます**.

## Step 5 — Guided partner switch

Choose a partner role (or play both roles yourself). Substitute an activity from the table. Use this four-line frame:

**A:** 土曜日に何をしますか。 *(What will you do on Saturday?)*  
**B:** 午前は＿＿を＿＿ます。 *(In the morning I'll …)*  
**A:** 「＿＿ます」の辞書形は何ですか。 *(What's the dictionary form of …?)*  
**B:** 「＿＿」です。グループ＿＿です。 *(It's …, Group ….)*

For example, **午前はお茶を飲みます → 飲む → グループ１**. When you change the activity, also change the object: 本を読む, 絵を見る, 名前を書く, お茶を飲む.

## Step 6 — Independent optional rehearsal

Create a **six-to-eight-line** exchange: ask about a day, name two activities, ask for one dictionary form, identify its group, and include a correction or confirmation. Read the lines aloud if comfortable; otherwise type or silently rehearse them. You can switch roles and repeat without saving a recording. Use the interactive writing pad and self-review below.

**Self-review criteria:** (1) a natural polite question, (2) two meaningful activity statements, (3) an accurate dictionary/ます pair, (4) the correct group including an exception where relevant, and (5) a coherent reply or correction. Marking these criteria is your own reflection, **not a verified speaking score**.

## Review and next steps

Remember **読む → 読みます** (Group 1), **見る → 見ます** (Group 2), **する → します** (Group 3). Keep dictionary-form labels and polite spoken sentences distinct. Revisit the chapter L06 narration for real listening practice when available. The written quiz below checks **recognition only**; it cannot evaluate speech, accent, or fluency.
