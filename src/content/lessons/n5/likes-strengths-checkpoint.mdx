## Mission: welcome two people to a hobby club

A community center is making a hobby board. Your job is to distinguish **what someone likes**, **what they do well**, and **what they can do**. These are three different claims. A person can love an activity without being good at it.

### 1. Review the language

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning and function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 好きです | すきです | like; a preference |
| 好きではありません | すきではありません | do not like |
| 上手です | じょうずです | good at; a skill judgment |
| 下手です | へたです | not good at; a skill judgment, often blunt about others |
| できます | できます | can do; ability |
| できません | できません | cannot do |
| 水泳 | すいえい | swimming |
| 絵 | え | picture, drawing |
| 音楽 | おんがく | music |
| 弾く | ひく | play a stringed or keyboard instrument |

**Preference:** <span lang="ja">水泳が好きです。</span> (すいえいがすきです。) — I like swimming. **Skill:** <span lang="ja">水泳はあまり上手ではありません。</span> (すいえいはあまりじょうずではありません。) — I'm not very good at swimming. **Ability:** <span lang="ja">テニスができます。</span> — I can play tennis. In context, できます expresses ability; it does not say you are an expert.

With a verb, <span lang="ja">絵を描くのが好きです。</span> (えをかくのがすきです。) means “I like drawing pictures.” <span lang="ja">ピアノを弾くことができます。</span> (ピアノをひくことができます。) means “I can play the piano.” The verb is nominalized with の or こと in these models. Avoid *好きません*: 好き is a na-adjective, so the polite negative is **好きではありません**. Be careful describing someone else as 下手; it can sound harsh.

### 2. Read the original hobby-board profiles

Two fictional members have written these short profiles. Read each line before answering; the questions require evidence rather than guesses.

**春（はる）:** <span lang="ja">こんにちは。春です。水泳が好きです。でも、泳ぐのはあまり上手ではありません。テニスもできます。</span>

“Hello. I'm Haru. I like swimming. But I'm not very good at swimming. I can also play tennis.”

**芽衣（めい）:** <span lang="ja">こんにちは。芽衣です。絵を描くのが好きです。絵を描くのはあまり上手ではありません。でも、ピアノを弾くことができます。</span>

“Hello. I'm Mei. I like drawing pictures. I'm not very good at drawing. But I can play the piano.”

**Evidence practice:** Does Haru say that they are an expert swimmer? No: <span lang="ja">泳ぐのはあまり上手ではありません。</span> explicitly says the opposite. Does Mei say she can play guitar? The profile does not mention guitar, so the answer is *not stated*, not “no.”

### 4. Write and optionally rehearse

Write **four connected sentences** introducing yourself to a hobby club: one hobby you like, one activity you are not very good at, one ability and one activity you cannot do. You may invent a fictional identity; do not reveal personal information.

Model (compare after writing): <span lang="ja">私は音楽が好きです。歌はあまり上手ではありません。でも、ピアノができます。ギターはできません。</span> — “I like music. I'm not very good at singing. But I can play piano. I can't play guitar.”

Self-review your work against five criteria: (1) preference uses 好き, (2) skill uses 上手/下手 with an appropriate softening when needed, (3) ability uses できます or a suitable potential form, (4) particles and polite endings are consistent, (5) the four statements make sense together. This writing is ungraded.

Optional speaking: ask <span lang="ja">何が好きですか。</span> (“What do you like?”) and answer <span lang="ja">音楽が好きです。</span> (“I like music.”). Rehearse aloud only if you wish; no recording starts and no pronunciation evaluation is claimed.
