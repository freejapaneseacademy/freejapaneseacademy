## A schedule at the community center

**Chapter Can-do:** Say what happened yesterday and what is planned. In this first lesson, you will *understand* a short conversation and distinguish past events from future plans. You do not yet need to conjugate every verb independently.

Two fictional classmates, **ミカ** and **レン**, are looking at a community-center activity calendar. Mika missed yesterday's reading group. Ren is arranging a visit for tomorrow.

### Original conversation

**ミカ:** レンさん、きのう、図書室（としょしつ）に行（い）きましたか。  
**レン:** はい、行きました。午後（ごご）三時（さんじ）に行きました。  
**ミカ:** 私（わたし）は行きませんでした。きのうはアルバイトでした。  
**レン:** そうですか。あしたは何（なに）をしますか。  
**ミカ:** あしたの午前（ごぜん）は日本語（にほんご）を勉強（べんきょう）します。  
**レン:** 私はあしたの午後（ごご）、図書室で本（ほん）を読（よ）みます。  
**ミカ:** いいですね。私も午後四時（よじ）に行きます。

**Meaning:** Mika asks whether Ren went to the library room yesterday. Ren did, at 3 p.m. Mika did not go because she had a part-time job. Tomorrow morning Mika will study Japanese. Tomorrow afternoon Ren plans to read in the library room, and Mika says she will go at 4 p.m. The dialogue expresses intentions, not a guarantee that future events will happen.

### First, find the time markers

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning in this conversation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| きのう | きのう | yesterday |
| あした | あした | tomorrow |
| 午前 | ごぜん | a.m.; morning |
| 午後 | ごご | p.m.; afternoon |
| 三時 | さんじ | three o'clock |
| 四時 | よじ | four o'clock (not よんじ) |
| 図書室 | としょしつ | library room |
| アルバイト | アルバイト | part-time job |
| 勉強します | べんきょうします | study / will study |
| 読みます | よみます | read / will read |

**Reading aid:** In Japanese, the time expression often tells you whether an otherwise nonpast sentence describes a habit or a future plan. **あした** anchors the nonpast **します／読みます／行きます** to tomorrow.

## A first look at polite past and nonpast

Compare the same verb in different contexts:

- **きのう、図書室に行きました。** — I went to the library room yesterday.
- **きのう、図書室に行きませんでした。** — I did not go to the library room yesterday.
- **あした、図書室に行きます。** — I will go to the library room tomorrow.

**行きました** is polite affirmative past; **行きませんでした** is polite negative past; **行きます** is polite nonpast. Japanese does not have a separate simple future tense here. **あした** makes the intended future meaning clear. You will practice how to form these endings systematically in L03.

**Important contrast:** **行きません** is polite nonpast negative (“do not go / will not go”), not “did not go.” For a completed past absence, use **行きませんでした**. In this conversation, **アルバイトでした** means “I had a part-time job / it was a work shift”; **でした** is the polite past of **です**, not a verb ending to attach to **行きます**.

## Who did what? Read for evidence

| Person | Yesterday | Tomorrow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| レン | Went to the library room at 3 p.m. | Plans to read a book there in the afternoon |
| ミカ | Did not go; had a part-time job | Studies Japanese in the morning; plans to go at 4 p.m. |

The **に** in **三時に行きました** marks the time of going. The **で** in **図書室で本を読みます** marks the place where reading takes place. By contrast, **図書室に行きます** uses **に** to mark the destination. These familiar particles help you understand the schedule; they are not today's new conjugation target.

## Controlled practice

1. Locate **きのう** and **あした**. Which one introduces past events?
2. Compare **行きました** and **行きませんでした**. Which says someone *did not* go?
3. Find **午後三時に行きました**. Who said it? What time does it indicate?
4. Find **あしたの午前は日本語を勉強します**. Is this a statement about yesterday or a plan for tomorrow?

Check each answer against the Japanese dialogue, not just the English summary.

## Guided transfer: change the time, change the meaning

Start with **きのう、本を読みました。** (“Yesterday I read a book.”) Now imagine the same person has a plan: **あした、本を読みます。** (“Tomorrow I will read a book.”) The time word and verb ending change together.

Next, take **きのう、行きませんでした。** (“Yesterday I did not go.”) Contrast **あした、行きません。** (“Tomorrow I will not go.”) The latter is a future interpretation because of **あした**; without context, the nonpast form can also describe a habit.

## Independent mini-task

Imagine a fictional learner who went to a library yesterday and plans to study tomorrow. Write two short sentences using **きのう…行きました** and **あした…勉強します**. Compare with:

**きのう、図書室に行きました。あした、日本語を勉強します。**  
“Yesterday I went to the library room. Tomorrow I will study Japanese.”

Check: (1) Is each time word appropriate? (2) Does the verb ending match past or nonpast? (3) Does the particle fit the destination or object? (4) Is your meaning clear? This is self-review, not an automatically graded writing assessment.

**Optional speaking rehearsal:** Read the two model sentences aloud if you wish; no microphone or pronunciation scoring is used here. Actual recorded schedule listening is reserved for L06 after verified audio production.
