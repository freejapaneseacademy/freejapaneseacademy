## Plan a simple outing

**Can-do:** Plan a simple outing and read short itinerary details. A class is visiting a museum. To follow the plan, you need to tell *when* people meet, *where* they go, *what* transport costs and *when* they arrive. This lesson focuses on **written vocabulary**, not audio comprehension.

## 1. Your itinerary word bank

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">出発</span> | しゅっぱつ | departure |
| <span lang="ja">到着</span> | とうちゃく | arrival |
| <span lang="ja">片道</span> | かたみち | one way |
| <span lang="ja">往復</span> | おうふく | round trip |
| <span lang="ja">運賃</span> | うんちん | fare |
| <span lang="ja">切符</span> | きっぷ | ticket |
| <span lang="ja">乗り場</span> | のりば | boarding area |
| <span lang="ja">時刻表</span> | じこくひょう | timetable |
| <span lang="ja">午前</span> | ごぜん | a.m. |
| <span lang="ja">午後</span> | ごご | p.m. |
| <span lang="ja">目的地</span> | もくてきち | destination |
| <span lang="ja">集合</span> | しゅうごう | meeting up |

Learn the complete word readings. **出発** and **到着** describe opposite ends of a journey. **片道** is a single journey in one direction, whereas **往復** covers going and returning. **運賃** is the price charged for transport; it is not the same as **切符**, the ticket itself. **乗り場** is where you board, not necessarily where you buy a ticket.

## 2. Time expressions that change the plan

**午前** (ごぜん) means before noon, usually a.m.; **午後** (ごご) means after noon, usually p.m. Compare <span lang="ja">午前九時</span> (ごぜん くじ, 9:00 a.m.) and <span lang="ja">午後九時</span> (ごご くじ, 9:00 p.m.). The numeral stays the same but the time changes by twelve hours.

In a notice, <span lang="ja">九時に出発します。</span> (くじに しゅっぱつします) means “We depart at nine.” The particle **に** marks a specific time. <span lang="ja">バスで行きます。</span> (ばすで いきます) means “We go by bus”: **で** marks the means of transport. Compare <span lang="ja">博物館に行きます。</span> (はくぶつかんに いきます), “We go to the museum”: **に** marks the destination. Do not confuse the two uses of に (time and destination) with transport で.

## 3. Original fictional travel notice

> **土曜日の博物館見学**
>
> <span lang="ja">集合：午前八時半、駅の前</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">目的地：市立博物館</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">出発：午前九時、駅前のバス乗り場</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">到着：午前九時二十分</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">運賃：片道二百円、往復四百円</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">切符はバスに乗る前に買ってください。</span>

Reading guide: **土曜日** (どようび) Saturday; **博物館見学** (はくぶつかんけんがく) museum visit; **駅の前** (えきのまえ) in front of the station; **市立博物館** (しりつはくぶつかん) municipal museum; **八時半** (はちじはん) 8:30; **九時二十分** (くじにじゅっぷん) 9:20; **二百円** (にひゃくえん) ¥200; **四百円** (よんひゃくえん) ¥400.

English: “Saturday museum visit. Meet at 8:30 a.m. in front of the station. Destination: municipal museum. Depart at 9:00 a.m. from the bus boarding area in front of the station. Arrive at 9:20 a.m. Fare: ¥200 one way, ¥400 round trip. Please buy a ticket before boarding the bus.”

This is an invented teaching notice, **not** a real bus timetable or fare. Notice that meeting at 8:30 is not the same as departing at 9:00. The **destination** is the museum; the **meeting point** is the station entrance area.

## 4. Guided retrieval

Cover the meanings in the table and explain **運賃**, **乗り場**, **到着** and **往復**. Then cover the Japanese and retrieve the words for “timetable,” “departure,” “ticket” and “destination.”

Check three contrasts:

1. <span lang="ja">午前九時に出発</span> means **depart at 9:00 a.m.**, not arrive then.
2. <span lang="ja">片道二百円</span> is **¥200 for one way**, not the whole return trip.
3. <span lang="ja">駅の前に集合</span> is **meet in front of the station**, not at the museum.

## 5. Independent practice

Write **three connected Japanese sentences** for a different fictional outing. Include a meeting time, a destination and a transport method. You can use this model:

> <span lang="ja">日曜日の午前十時に駅の前で集合します。電車で動物園に行きます。午前十一時に到着します。</span>
>
> (にちようびの ごぜんじゅうじに えきのまえで しゅうごうします。でんしゃで どうぶつえんに いきます。ごぜんじゅういちじに とうちゃくします。)
>
> “We meet in front of the station at 10 a.m. Sunday. We go to the zoo by train. We arrive at 11 a.m.”

Self-review five criteria: (1) time includes 午前 or 午後 when needed; (2) destination is clear; (3) で marks the means of transport; (4) に marks a specific time or destination appropriately; (5) the order of meeting, departure and arrival is plausible. The writing is **not automatically scored**. Optionally read your sentences aloud without recording or pronunciation grading.
