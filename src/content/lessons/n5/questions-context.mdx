## A question at the classroom welcome desk

**Can-do:** Ask and answer who, what, which and where questions. In this first lesson, you will **understand** questions in a short exchange before producing your own in later lessons.

At a language-school welcome desk, a new learner named ミナ is looking for her classroom. A staff member named 田中（たなか）さん is helping. There are two paper room cards on the desk. These are fictional people and a fictional setting.

### Original exchange

**ミナ:** すみません。田中さんはどなたですか。  
**スタッフ:** 私が田中です。こんにちは。  
**ミナ:** 田中さんは先生ですか。  
**田中:** はい、先生です。ミナさんも学生ですか。  
**ミナ:** はい、学生です。教室（きょうしつ）はどこですか。  
**田中:** 二階（にかい）です。  
**ミナ:** このカードは何（なん）ですか。  
**田中:** 教室のカードです。  
**ミナ:** 私のカードはどれですか。  
**田中:** こちらです。「A」と書いてあります。

**English:** Mina asks who Tanaka is (using polite どなた), whether Tanaka is a teacher, and where the classroom is. Tanaka confirms being a teacher and asks whether Mina is a student too. Mina asks what a card is and which card is hers; Tanaka points out the one marked A. The phrase 「A」と書いてあります (“It says A”) is only contextual support; you do not need to produce it today.

**Note on roles:** The first スタッフ line and subsequent 田中 lines are spoken by the same staff member. The scene uses **どなた** (a more polite form of “who”) in its opening. Today's target **だれ** is the everyday question word for “who”; choose どなた when you want extra politeness.

## Notice what the question is asking

| Question cue | Reading | Information requested | Answer in this scene |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| だれ / どなた | だれ / どなた | a person’s identity | 田中さん |
| 何 | なに / なん | an object or kind of thing | 教室のカード |
| どこ | どこ | a place | 二階 |
| どれ | どれ | one item among several | the card marked A |
| も | も | “also / too,” not a question word | ミナさんも学生ですか |

**Reading caution:** 何 has several readings. Before **ですか** in **何ですか**, it is normally **なんですか**. In **何を**, it is normally **なにを**. Do not apply one reading mechanically to every context.

**Which versus where:** **どれ** selects one item from multiple candidates; **どこ** asks for a location. In the scene, the classroom is on the second floor, but the card is the one marked A. Swapping the two answers would not meet the speaker's information need.

**Also:** In **ミナさんも学生ですか**, **も** marks Mina as an additional person being considered (“Are you also a student?”). It replaces topic **は** in that phrase; do not write **ミナさんはも**. The context does not establish that Tanaka is a student, so the sentence is an illustrative question, not proof that someone else is a student. A more natural alternative in this specific exchange is **ミナさんは学生ですか**; the original line is included to notice the form of も.

## Vocabulary to recognize, not memorize all at once

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 先生 | せんせい | teacher |
| 学生 | がくせい | student |
| 教室 | きょうしつ | classroom |
| 二階 | にかい | second floor |
| カード | カード | card |
| こちら | こちら | this way / this one (polite, context-dependent) |
| すみません | すみません | excuse me |
| どなた | どなた | who (polite) |

The written kanji **教室** and **二階** are reading supports here, not an L01 kanji-writing requirement.

## From supported noticing to independent choice

**Controlled check:** Match each English information need to its Japanese cue: “person” → **だれ**, “location” → **どこ**, “which of several cards” → **どれ**, “what kind of object” → **何**.

**Guided reading:** Read **教室はどこですか。** and underline the word asking for location. Then read **カードはどれですか。** and underline the word asking for a selection. Explain why the same answer cannot automatically answer both.

**Independent mini-task:** Imagine two notebooks on a table and a classmate standing beside you. Write or say one question to ask **which notebook** is yours and one question to ask **who** the classmate is. A supported model is **私のノートはどれですか。** (“Which notebook is mine?”) and **あの人はだれですか。** (“Who is that person?”). **あの人** (“that person”) is a context phrase, not a new grammar target.

## Reading, writing, listening and optional speaking

Read the exchange once without English, then return to the table only if necessary. Write the four question cues and one answer type beside each. For listening preparation, cover the written exchange and ask a partner to read it slowly; **no recorded audio is provided or scored in L01**. Listening with verified recordings is taught in L06. Optionally rehearse **すみません。教室はどこですか。** aloud; this is ungraded self-practice, not pronunciation verification.
