## A wish list for the coming days

**Can-do:** Express what you want to do or obtain. Read a fictional student's private wish list and identify specific intentions. Wishes are not confirmed reservations or promises.

### 1. Words and sentence patterns

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 土曜日 | どようび | Saturday |
| 日曜日 | にちようび | Sunday |
| 図書館 | としょかん | library |
| 勉強する | べんきょうする | to study |
| 映画 | えいが | film |
| 友だち | ともだち | friend |
| 新しい | あたらしい | new |
| 来月 | らいげつ | next month |
| 写真を撮る | しゃしんをとる | to take a photograph |
| 休む | やすむ | to rest |

**〜たいです** follows the verb's ます-stem: 勉強します → 勉強したいです (want to study). **〜がほしいです** names an object you want: ノートがほしいです (want a notebook). **〜に行く** expresses going somewhere for a purpose: 映画を見に行く (go to see a film). Do not treat a wish as something already completed.

> **C.** <span lang="ja">新しいノートがほしいです。</span>

> **D.** <span lang="ja">来月、京都へ行きたいです。</span>

> **F.** <span lang="ja">赤いかばんはほしくないです。</span>

**English support (check after reading):**

- **A:** On Saturday I want to study Japanese at the library.
- **B:** On Sunday I want to go to see a film with a friend.
- **C:** I want a new notebook.
- **D:** Next month I want to go to Kyoto.
- **E:** I want to take photos in Kyoto.
- **F:** I do not want a red bag.
- **G:** I do not have a movie ticket yet.
- **H:** On Sunday morning I want to rest at home.

### 3. Read the relationships

**Worked example:** Where does the student want to study? **A: 土曜日は図書館で日本語を勉強したいです。** **図書館で** means at the library; **で** marks the location of the study activity, while **を** marks what is studied.

**Second example:** **C: 新しいノートがほしいです** means the student wants a new notebook. It does not say they will buy one. Contrast **新しいノートを買いたいです** (want to buy a new notebook), which explicitly describes the action.

**Third example:** **B: 映画を見に行きたいです** means want to go to see a film. **に** after the verb stem **見** indicates purpose. **D: 京都へ行きたいです** uses **へ** to mark the destination.

### 4. Negatives and missing details

**F: 赤いかばんはほしくないです** explicitly rejects a *red* bag, not necessarily every bag. **G: 映画のチケットはまだありません** says there is no film ticket *yet*, not that the student will never obtain one. The note does not specify the notebook's color or which movie will be shown.

A common mistake is to equate **ほしい** and **〜たい**. The first names a desired object; the second follows a verb stem to describe a desired action. Also, a stated wish is not evidence that a ticket was purchased.

### 5. Guided evidence hunt

Cover the English translations. Find the line for (1) an activity with a friend, (2) a travel destination, (3) something the writer does not have. Check: **B — 友だちと映画を見に行きたいです**; **D — 来月、京都へ行きたいです**; **G — 映画のチケットはまだありません**.

**日曜日の朝** in H is more specific than **日曜日** in B. The film has no stated time; do not assume the two wishes conflict.

### 7. Optional writing and speaking

Write two or three sentences about an imaginary weekend: one desired action (**〜たいです**), one desired object (**〜がほしいです**), and optionally a destination (**〜へ行きたいです**).

Model: <span lang="ja">土曜日は本を読みたいです。新しいノートがほしいです。日曜日は公園へ行きたいです。</span> (On Saturday I want to read a book. I want a new notebook. On Sunday I want to go to the park.)

Self-check: Did you use a verb stem before **たい**? Did you use **がほしい** for the object? Are these wishes, not confirmed events? You may read your sentences aloud, but this lesson does not record or assess pronunciation.

### 8. Review

To understand a wish list, track **when**, **what action or object**, and **where**. Cite the exact Japanese phrase. Lesson 06 introduces recorded listening; this lesson is written reading only.
