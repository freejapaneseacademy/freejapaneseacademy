## Listen to a stranger asking for help

In two original fictional street encounters, one person asks for a place and another gives a short route. The dialogues use **まっすぐ** (straight), **右** (right), **左** (left), and **〜にあります** (is located at). They are intended for slow, clear Japanese listening, not kanji recognition.

**First listening — main idea.** Play Track A without revealing the transcript. Ask yourself: what kind of place does the visitor need? Do not try to translate every word. Listen a second time for the order of instructions: straight first, then a turn, then a location.

**Second listening — change of plan.** Play Track B without text. Notice that the visitor suggests a turn and the guide corrects it. A spoken **いいえ** changes the route; the answer is the correction, not the visitor's guess.

## Listening anchors (review after the first attempt)

| Sound to recognize | Meaning in context |
| --- | --- |
| まっすぐ行ってください | Please go straight |
| 右に曲がってください | Please turn right |
| 左に曲がってください | Please turn left |
| 〜は左にあります | … is on the left |
| 〜のとなりにあります | … is next to … |

These are written study cues, **not** audio. For a blind listening score, listen before using the table or transcript as a hint. The player lets you reveal the complete Japanese and English transcripts at any time; that switches the attempt to assisted practice, so it is not stored as an unaided score.

## Track A: from the station to a bakery

A visitor asks a local person for a bakery. Listen for the destination, the first instruction, and the direction of the turn. The three questions for Track A are based only on audible statements. The final left-side location is an extra detail you can check after answering.

## Track B: finding the post office

A different visitor asks for a post office near a park. Listen carefully to the exchange about turning right: the guide says **いいえ** and gives a different instruction. Identify the landmark to reach first, the corrected turn, and the post office's location.

## A three-pass method

1. **Listen once:** identify the destination and speaker purpose.
2. **Listen again:** track straight, turn, and final location. Replay at normal speed if possible.
3. **Answer before revealing:** choose the six English answers from what you heard, then check feedback. After submitting, reveal the speaker-labelled Japanese transcript and English translation to verify exact evidence.

You may use the speed control (0.8×, 1×, 1.2×) to adjust the recording. Speed changes do not guarantee perfect intelligibility. If the media cannot play, use the transcript-assisted option for accessible study; do not interpret it as proof of listening mastery.

## Transfer: give your own directions

Write or say two or three short Japanese instructions for a fictional visitor: **この道をまっすぐ行ってください。次の角を左に曲がってください。公園は右にあります。** (*Go straight along this road. Turn left at the next corner. The park is on the right.*)

Compare your result against four criteria: (1) understandable destination, (2) ordered steps, (3) correct left/right direction, and (4) a location statement using **あります**. This open response is self-reviewed, not automatically graded or recorded.
