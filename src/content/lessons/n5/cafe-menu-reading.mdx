## Your café reading mission

**Can-do:** Name common foods and express simple preferences. Read the fictional menu and customer note below, then find exact Japanese evidence for each answer. This is a written reading lesson, not an audio or pronunciation assessment.

## Menu reading signals

**メニュー** means menu; **食べ物（たべもの）** means food; **飲み物（のみもの）** means beverage; **円（えん）** is yen. **セット** means a named combination. **と** joins nouns: **パンと卵** means bread and egg. **ありません** means “there is not”; **ついていません** means “is not included.”

## Original menu — ひかりカフェ

| Item | Contents | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">パンセット</span> | <span lang="ja">パンと卵</span> — bread and egg | 500円 |
| <span lang="ja">魚セット（さかなセット）</span> | <span lang="ja">魚とごはん</span> — fish and rice | 600円 |
| <span lang="ja">お茶（おちゃ）</span> | tea | 200円 |
| <span lang="ja">水（みず）</span> | water | 100円 |
| <span lang="ja">牛乳（ぎゅうにゅう）</span> | milk | 300円 |

**お知らせ:** <span lang="ja">今日はコーヒーがありません。</span> — There is no coffee today.

**セットの飲み物:** <span lang="ja">セットに飲み物はついていません。</span> — A drink is not included with a set.

The café, menu and prices are fictional. Do not assume that a set includes a drink: the notice explicitly says otherwise.

## Customer note

> <span lang="ja">ゆいさんのメモ</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">私は卵が好きです。今日はパンセットを食べます。飲み物はお茶にします。魚も好きですが、今日は魚セットを食べません。</span>

**Reading support:** わたしは たまごが すきです。きょうは パンセットを たべます。のみものは おちゃに します。さかなも すきですが、きょうは さかなセットを たべません。

**Translation:** I like eggs. Today I'll eat the bread set. I'll choose tea for my drink. I like fish too, but I will not eat the fish set today. **〜にします** means “I'll choose ~” in this context.

## Evidence-based reading

**What is in the bread set?** Quote **パンと卵**. Do not include tea: **セットに飲み物はついていません**.

**What is the fish set's price?** Quote **魚セット 600円**. This is not the price of milk.

**Can you order coffee today?** Quote **今日はコーヒーがありません**. The negative **ありません** gives the answer.

**Does Yui like fish?** Yes: **魚も好きですが**. Yet **今日は魚セットを食べません** says she will not eat the fish set today. Liking something (**が好きです**) is different from eating it (**を食べます**).

## Reading strategy

Scan the menu headings, food names and numbers first. Next locate the precise row or sentence that answers the question. For negative questions, look for **ありません** or **ついていません**. Do not infer that a customer orders everything they like.

**Guided practice:** Find the ¥200 drink (**お茶 200円**). Find the set with rice (**魚とごはん**). Find the unavailable drink (**コーヒーがありません**).

## Independent practice and transfer

Answer the twelve original questions below, then compare the evidence. Write two or three short Japanese sentences about a fictional customer: one preference, one menu fact and one action.

Model: <span lang="ja">ゆいさんは卵が好きです。パンセットは500円です。今日はパンセットを食べます。</span>

Self-check: use **が** for **好きです**, **を** for **食べます**, and copy the price correctly. This writing is self-reviewed, not graded. Optional speaking: read your sentences aloud using kana support. No recording or verified pronunciation score is provided.
