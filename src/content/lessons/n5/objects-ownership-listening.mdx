## The listening mission

You are helping at a school lost-and-found counter. Two short **fictional** conversations contain an object, its owner, and sometimes its location. Your goal is to hear who owns what, rather than identify printed characters. The recordings use synthetic AivisSpeech voices; independent Japanese pronunciation review is pending.

### Before you play

Remember the difference between **これ** (“this one” near me), **それ** (“that one” near you) and **あれ** (“that one over there”). Before a noun, use **この・その・あの**. **だれの** asks “whose?” and a name followed by **の** identifies an owner. An answer such as **田中さんのです** leaves the repeated object understood.

For this activity, *青い* means blue, *赤い* red, *かさ* umbrella, *ノート* notebook, *かばん* bag, *教室* classroom, *机* desk and *上* on/top. These are listening supports, **not the answers**. Notice that **いいえ** often introduces a correction.

### Round 1 — Listen without the transcript

Play scene A and scene B. Focus first on the main situation, then replay and answer the three questions for each scene. The transcript reveal is optional. If you open either transcript, the attempt is marked **transcript-assisted**, not unaided. Never infer an owner from where an object happens to be.

### Round 2 — Verify what you heard

After answering, reveal the Japanese transcript and the turn-by-turn English meaning. Find the *exact spoken phrase* supporting each answer. In scene A, listen especially for the contrast between the blue and red umbrellas. In scene B, distinguish the distant bag from the notebook being discussed.

### Independent practice

Without looking at the transcript, write two English notes for each scene: **object → owner**, then one Japanese question using **だれのですか**. Compare your question to **このノートはだれのですか。** (“Whose notebook is this?”). Next, write a short Japanese response using a fictional name and **のです**. If you used the transcript, label your own practice as assisted.

### Optional speaking

At your own pace, shadow one short turn after playback, then say **このかさはだれのですか。** and answer **田中さんのです。** This is self-directed rehearsal, not a pronunciation assessment. No recording or microphone access is required.
