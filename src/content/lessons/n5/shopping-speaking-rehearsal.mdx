## Optional speaking mission

You are visiting a **fictional stationery shop** to buy a notebook and paper for class. Your goal is to ask about a price, clarify an item, and make a polite request. This speaking practice is **optional**: you may read silently, whisper, speak aloud, or type instead. No microphone is required and nothing is recorded or uploaded.

### First listen, then speak — if audio is available

Review the real checkout listening lesson **L06** at [the chapter listening page](/learn/n5/module-04/chapter-02/lesson-06/). Its recordings are separate checkout scenes, **not** a recording of the dialogue below. Use that lesson for real Japanese listening and rhythm. This page supplies a written rehearsal model; it does not pretend that a matching voice track exists. You can practise with a partner or read both roles yourself.

## Model dialogue: notebooks and paper

**客（きゃく）— customer:** すみません。青（あお）いノートは、いくらですか。  
*Excuse me. How much is the blue notebook?*

**店員（てんいん）— clerk:** 一冊（いっさつ）150円（ひゃくごじゅうえん）です。  
*It's 150 yen for one notebook.*

**客:** では、青いノートを一冊（いっさつ）ください。  
*Then, one blue notebook, please.*

**店員:** はい。ほかに何（なに）かありますか。  
*Certainly. Anything else?*

**客:** 白（しろ）い紙（かみ）を五枚（ごまい）お願いします。  
*Five sheets of white paper, please.*

**店員:** はい。紙は五枚で100円（ひゃくえん）です。  
*Certainly. The five sheets of paper cost 100 yen.*

The signs and prices are fictional learning examples, not live shop offers. The two purchases total 250 yen; you do not need to say the total to complete this speaking task.

## Three useful moves

### 1. Get attention and ask a price

**すみません。これはいくらですか。** — *Excuse me. How much is this?* Point to an item, or name it: **この赤（あか）いノートは、いくらですか。** — *How much is this red notebook?* The topic particle **は** is natural in a price question; do not use **を** in place of は here.

### 2. Confirm size or color

**小（ちい）さいかばんは、いくらですか。** — *How much is the small bag?*  
**大（おお）きいかばんをお願いします。** — *The large bag, please.*

The adjectives **小さい・大きい・青い・赤い・白い** appear directly before the noun they describe. Compare **青いノート** (blue notebook) with **青ノート**, which is not the default beginner adjective pattern taught here.

### 3. Request an item and a count

**ノートを一冊ください。** — *One notebook, please.*  
**紙を五枚お願いします。** — *Five sheets of paper, please.*  
**えんぴつを三本（さんぼん）ください。** — *Three pencils, please.*

**を** marks the requested object. The counter depends on the item: **冊（さつ）** for notebooks, **枚（まい）** for sheets, **本（ほん）** for pencils. Pronunciations change: **一冊（いっさつ）**, **一本（いっぽん）**, **三本（さんぼん）**. Both **ください** and **お願いします** can be polite in this shopping context; neither needs to be combined with the other in the same short request.

## Rehearsal ladder

**Round A — controlled:** Read the customer lines slowly. Replace 青い with 赤い and 一冊 with 二冊（にさつ）. Keep the item, adjective and counter together.

**Round B — guided:** Partner A is the customer, Partner B the clerk. Use these fictional prices: red notebook ¥180 each; small bag ¥900; pencil ¥80 each. Ask **いくらですか**, answer with a price, then request one item. Swap roles.

**Round C — independent:** Without reading the model, perform a three-to-five-turn exchange. Include a greeting or attention-getter, one price question, a clerk's answer, and one specific request with a counter. If alone, write both roles or speak them in turn. No automatic speaking grade is given.

## Self-review rubric

After rehearsing, check four criteria. (1) **Meaning:** Was the requested item identifiable? (2) **Price question:** Did you use **いくらですか** with an appropriate topic? (3) **Request:** Did you use **をください** or **をお願いします** and a suitable quantity counter? (4) **Clarity and politeness:** Could a partner follow the exchange, with a polite opening? Rate each yourself as *ready*, *needs another try*, or *not attempted*. This is reflection, not a verified pronunciation or proficiency score.

## Common repairs

- **✗ ノートが一冊ください。 → ✓ ノートを一冊ください。** Use を for the requested item.
- **✗ いくらをですか。 → ✓ いくらですか。** Do not insert を into the basic price question.
- **✗ 紙を五冊ください。 → ✓ 紙を五枚ください。** Sheets take 枚, not the book counter 冊.
- **✗ 大きいのかばん → ✓ 大きいかばん。** An い-adjective directly modifies its noun.

**Takeaway:** Ask **いくらですか**, listen for the amount, then request **item + を + counter + ください／お願いします** politely.
