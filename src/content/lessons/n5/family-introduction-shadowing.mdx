## At a school exhibition: introduce your family

**Can-do:** Describe family members and simple relationships in a short, polite conversation. You will rehearse two original fictional scenes from the chapter's listening lesson, switch speakers, and practise a new exchange of your own. No real family details or photographs are required.

This optional speaking studio uses the **L06 original AivisSpeech candidate recordings** as models *only if the real licensed WAVs have been generated and validated*. If playback is unavailable, use the printed scripts for ungraded reading and role-play; never count that as successful listening. Nothing here automatically grades pronunciation.

## 1. A useful four-part speaking pattern

| Function | Japanese | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduce | こちらは、わたしの妹です。 | This is my younger sister. |
| Ask politely | 妹さんは、学生ですか。 | Is your younger sister a student? |
| Refer back | その人は、わたしの兄です。 | That person is my older brother. |
| Correct | いいえ、先生じゃありません。 | No, (he) is not a teacher. |

**こちら** politely presents a person in front of you; **その人** points back to an already identified person, such as someone in a photograph. **の** connects a person to a family relationship: **わたしの兄** means “my older brother.” **兄（あに）** is a conventional reference to your own older brother when speaking to an outsider, while **お兄さん（おにいさん）** respectfully refers to another person's older brother. In family-internal speech, forms may differ.

## 2. Scene A — the school exhibition

**Woman:** こちらは、わたしの妹、ゆいです。  
**Man:** ゆいさんは、学生ですか。  
**Woman:** はい、高校生です。こちらは、わたしの母です。  
**Man:** はじめまして。よろしくお願いします。

The woman introduces **Yui as her younger sister**, then introduces her own mother. **高校生（こうこうせい）** means “high school student.” The man's question uses **ゆいさん** respectfully, not a possessive relation.

**Rehearsal:** Play the verified model once, if available. Pause after each woman's turn and repeat only her line. On the second pass, follow the man's lines. On a third pass, say the lines without reading, if comfortable. Keep pauses natural rather than rushing.

## 3. Scene B — the family photograph

**Man:** この写真の人は、だれですか。  
**Woman:** その人は、わたしの兄です。  
**Man:** お兄さんは、先生ですか。  
**Woman:** いいえ、先生じゃありません。会社員です。

The woman's **兄** is her older brother. The man uses **お兄さん** for *her* brother. **会社員（かいしゃいん）** is a company employee; the answer explicitly rejects **先生** (teacher). Do not infer any other job.

**Rehearsal:** Ask the photograph question, wait, then shadow the reply. Switch roles and practise the polite follow-up question. Compare the contrast **兄 / お兄さん** and notice the negation **じゃありません**.

## 4. Your own fictional role-play

Choose two invented people. Person A introduces a relative; person B asks one respectful question. For example:

> A: こちらは、わたしの姉、あきです。  
> B: あきさんは、学生ですか。  
> A: いいえ、学生じゃありません。先生です。  
> B: そうですか。よろしくお願いします。

This model is **new practice text, not a claimed audio recording**. It means: “This is my older sister Aki.” “Is Aki a student?” “No, she isn't a student. She's a teacher.” “I see. Nice to meet you.” Write or say a two-to-four-turn variation using only fictional facts. A name after a family noun is an appositive, not another possessive.

## 5. Self-check and next review

Use the optional private microphone only if you want to hear yourself. Recording starts **only** after your action and browser permission; it is not uploaded or stored as a grade. You can also rehearse silently. Review yourself against four criteria: (1) family relationship is correct; (2) の connects the right nouns; (3) こちら/その人 match the context; (4) you can switch roles and use a polite family form for the other person's relative.
