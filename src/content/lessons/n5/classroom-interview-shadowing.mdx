## At the classroom welcome desk

You are helping a new student find the right classroom. You need to ask **who** is teaching, **where** the room is, **what** a labeled object is, and **which** of three books belongs to the teacher. This optional speaking lesson turns the chapter's written questions into short conversational turns.

**Speaking is optional.** You can whisper, speak aloud, or silently rehearse. No microphone is required, no speech is uploaded, and this lesson does not award an automatic pronunciation score. Use the approved L06 interview recording for audio shadowing **only once its real audio, license, and QA manifest have been verified**. Until then, use the text-based rehearsal below; written text is not a substitute for verified audio.

## Model interview: read, pause, answer

The following is an original fictional role-play, not a transcript of the L06 recording. Read each question aloud slowly, pause for an answer, then read the model reply.

| Visitor | Welcome-desk helper | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| あの人はだれですか。 | 田中先生です。 | Who is that person? — That's Teacher Tanaka. |
| 教室はどこですか。 | あそこです。 | Where is the classroom? — Over there. |
| これは何ですか。 | 名札（なふだ）です。 | What is this? — It's a name tag. |
| どれが先生の本ですか。 | この青い本です。 | Which is the teacher's book? — This blue book. |
| 佐藤さんも先生ですか。 | いいえ、佐藤さんは学生です。 | Is Sato also a teacher? — No, Sato is a student. |

**What to listen for when approved audio becomes available:** the rising conversational contour of a polite question can help signal that the speaker expects an answer, but **か** is the explicit question marker here. Don't claim a specific accent pattern based on written text alone.

## A three-pass rehearsal

**Pass 1 — Meaning.** Read each Japanese question and point to the correct answer. Notice that **だれ** requests a person, **何（なん）** a thing in **何ですか**, **どこ** a place, and **どれ** one choice among several.

**Pass 2 — Turn-taking.** Cover the answer column. Ask a question, wait two beats, and answer from memory. If you forget a word, consult the model and retry. Say **もう一度お願いします。** (*Please say it once more*) when you need repetition.

**Pass 3 — Role reversal.** Be the helper first, then the visitor. Change just one detail, for example **田中先生** to **山田先生**, while keeping the question word and reply type consistent. Do not change the identity of someone in the model without explicitly making this a fictional variation.

## How to repair common mistakes

- **あの人はどこですか。** asks where that person is, not who they are. For identity ask **あの人はだれですか。**
- **どれ** is for selecting an item from a set. For an unknown object's identity use **これは何ですか。**
- **も** adds another matching case: **田中さんは学生です。佐藤さんも学生です。** It does not automatically mean *and* in every sentence.
- In **何ですか**, **何** is normally read **なん**. In other contexts its reading can be **なに**; do not assume one reading everywhere.

## Guided role-play

Imagine three books on the desk: a blue book belongs to the teacher, a red book belongs to Sato, and a green book belongs to Emi. Ask **どれが先生の本ですか。** Answer **この青い本です。** Then ask who the teacher is, where the classroom is, and whether Sato is also a teacher. Use the model above as your evidence.

## Independent speaking task

Create a **four-turn interview** with at least three different question words from **だれ・何・どこ・どれ** and one reply using **も** appropriately. Keep names and roles fictional. Practice once slowly and once at a comfortable pace. The interactive rehearsal below lets you reveal model answers and save a private self-review; it does **not** listen to or grade your voice.

### Self-review criteria

After rehearsal, check: (1) Did each question word match the information requested? (2) Did the answer provide the right kind of information? (3) Did you use **ですか** for polite questions? (4) Did **も** refer to a genuinely shared role? Record what you will improve on your next attempt.
