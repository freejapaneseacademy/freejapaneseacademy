## Mission: plan a visit to two community events

You are helping a visitor read notices and understand announcements. Dates and quantities can sound similar, so identify **what is being counted** before choosing an answer. These are fictional events, not real appointments.

### 1. Review the patterns

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 十一月 | じゅういちがつ | November |
| 十二月 | じゅうにがつ | December |
| 三日 | みっか | third day of the month |
| 十四日 | じゅうよっか | fourteenth |
| 二十一日 | にじゅういちにち | twenty-first |
| 午前十時 | ごぜんじゅうじ | 10 a.m. |
| 午後二時 | ごごにじ | 2 p.m. |
| 一つ | ひとつ | one general item |
| 二人 | ふたり | two people |
| 二枚 | にまい | two flat items |

A date such as <span lang="ja">十一月十四日</span> combines **month + day**. Day readings include irregular forms; **十四日** is not *じゅうよんにち*. The phrase <span lang="ja">午後二時から</span> means **starting at 2 p.m.** The particle **に** can mark a specific event time, as in <span lang="ja">午後二時に始まります。</span>; **から** marks the starting point. Do not confuse the time **二時** with the counter **二人**.

Counters depend on the noun: **枚** for paper and other flat items, **人** for people, and **つ** for general objects. <span lang="ja">二人</span> is pronounced ふたり, not ににん. <span lang="ja">一人</span> is ひとり. A notebook can be counted with **一つ** in this beginner scenario; **一冊** is the more specific counter for bound books.

### 2. Original reading: two event notices

The following notices are a **separate fictional schedule** from the audio announcements.

**Notice A — cooking group**

> <span lang="ja">料理の会は十二月三日です。午後一時からです。お皿を二枚持ってきてください。</span>

The cooking group is December 3, starting at 1 p.m. Bring two plates. The plate count uses **枚**, not **人**.

**Notice B — flower workshop**

> <span lang="ja">花の教室は十二月十日です。午前九時からです。先生が三人来ます。</span>

The flower workshop is December 10, starting at 9 a.m. Three teachers are coming. **三人** counts people, not objects. Read the notices carefully: neither notice states an admission fee.

### 3. Listening: chapter announcements

**Track A** announces an art class on November 14 at 10 a.m. and asks for two sheets of paper. **Track B** announces library story time on November 21 at 2 p.m., with two teachers and one small notebook requested. These are **different events** from the written notices.

Listen first without a transcript and identify the event. Replay to catch the date, time and quantity. The four listening questions only become scoreable when **both real chapter recordings can play**; printed transcripts alone are not evidence of listening ability. Showing a transcript labels the attempt *transcript-assisted*. Never interpret a visitor's question about bringing books as an instruction to bring them.

### 4. Write and self-check

Write **four connected sentences** about a new fictional event: its name, date, start time and one material or participant count. For example:

> <span lang="ja">音楽の会は十二月五日です。午後三時からです。先生が二人来ます。ノートを一つ持ってきてください。</span>

Self-check: (1) month and date readings are correct; (2) **午前/午後** matches your intended time; (3) **人/枚/つ** matches what is counted; (4) all four sentences refer to one consistent event. This is a self-assessed writing task, not an automatically graded composition.

**Optional speaking:** Practise <span lang="ja">何時からですか。</span> and answer <span lang="ja">午後三時からです。</span> You may shadow the approved recordings once available. No microphone starts automatically and no pronunciation score is claimed.
