## Mission: explain yesterday and tomorrow

A friend asks what you did yesterday and what you plan to do tomorrow. Your task is to separate **completed actions**, **things that did not happen**, and **plans**. This is original formative practice, not an official JLPT assessment.

### 1. Grammar and time clues

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 昨日、図書館で勉強しました。 | きのう、としょかんで べんきょうしました | Yesterday I studied at the library. |
| 午後は映画を見ませんでした。 | ごごは えいがを みませんでした | In the afternoon I did not watch a film. |
| 明日は公園へ行きます。 | あしたは こうえんへ いきます | Tomorrow I will go to the park. |
| 明日は買い物をしません。 | あしたは かいものを しません | Tomorrow I will not shop. |

**ました** is polite past affirmative. **ませんでした** is polite past negative. **ます** and **ません** are nonpast forms; with **明日**, they express future plans, not actions already completed. Japanese does not require a separate future verb ending.

**で** identifies an action location (図書館で勉強しました), **へ** indicates direction (公園へ行きます), **を** marks the object (映画を見ました), and **に** marks a specific clock time (九時に). **昨日** and **明日** generally do not take a time-marking に. Avoid saying **昨日、勉強しません** when you mean that you did not study yesterday: use **勉強しませんでした**.

### 2. Original reading: Haru's calendar note

This fictional note is separate from the chapter audio.

> <span lang="ja">昨日、はるさんは朝九時に図書館へ行きました。図書館で日本語を勉強しました。午後は映画を見ませんでした。家で本を読みました。今日は学校で友達と話します。明日は十時に公園へ行きます。午後は買い物をしません。</span>

**Translation:** Yesterday Haru went to the library at nine in the morning and studied Japanese there. In the afternoon Haru did not watch a movie, but read a book at home. Today Haru talks with friends at school. Tomorrow Haru will go to the park at ten and will not go shopping in the afternoon.

To answer reading questions, cite the shortest supporting phrase. **図書館で日本語を勉強しました** establishes the subject studied. **映画を見ませんでした** is a negative past fact. **明日は十時に公園へ行きます** is a plan, not a completed visit. Do not mix up Haru's written calendar with the two speakers in the audio.

### 3. Listening: chapter schedule conversations

**Track A** asks about yesterday: the speaker studied Japanese at a library in the morning, did not study in the afternoon, and watched a movie with a friend. **Track B** asks about tomorrow: a park visit at nine in the morning and reading at home in the afternoon rather than shopping.

First listen without a transcript, then listen for positive and negative verbs. You may reveal a transcript to check your understanding; that marks the attempt **transcript-assisted**. Four listening questions are scored only if both genuine chapter WAVs are playable. A written transcript is never a substitute for recorded listening evidence.

### 4. Write and self-review

Write **four connected sentences** about an imaginary yesterday and tomorrow: a past action, a past action you did not do, a destination tomorrow, and a plan you will not do.

Model:

> <span lang="ja">昨日、学校で日本語を勉強しました。午後はテレビを見ませんでした。明日は九時に図書館へ行きます。夜は買い物をしません。</span>

Self-review against four criteria: (1) **ました** describes a completed action; (2) **ませんでした** negates a past action; (3) **明日** is paired with a nonpast plan; (4) **で・へ・に・を** match their grammatical roles. Your original writing is self-assessed, not machine-scored.

Optional speaking: ask **昨日、何をしましたか。** and **明日は何をしますか。** Answer with two short sentences. Rehearse with chapter audio only when available. No recording starts automatically and no pronunciation grade is claimed.

### 5. Check, repair, revisit

Complete six grammar and four reading questions. When both recordings can play, four listening questions also unlock. Review Japanese evidence and feedback after submission. For any missed item, rewrite a correct contrast (for example, **見ました / 見ませんでした**) and revisit it tomorrow.
