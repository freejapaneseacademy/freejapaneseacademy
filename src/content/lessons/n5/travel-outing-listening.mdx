## Two journeys, two kinds of information

You will hear **two original fictional conversations**. In the first, friends plan a Saturday museum visit. In the second, a traveller asks about a Sunday bus to an aquarium. The fare is invented for language learning, not a current transit price.

**First listening:** Play each track once with the transcript hidden. Ask: *Where are they going?* Do not worry about every particle yet.

**Second listening:** Replay and note a specific time and the means of travel or price. Listen for the contrast between **午前九時** (9 a.m.) and **午前十時** (10 a.m.), and between **電車** (train) and **バス** (bus).

**Third listening:** Answer the six questions without revealing written support for an unaided listening score. You may slow playback to 0.8× or replay as often as necessary. Revealing a transcript before submission switches to ungraded assisted practice.

## Before listening: useful meanings, not the answers

| Expression | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 何時 | なんじ | What time? |
| 何で | なにで | By what means? |
| 会いましょう | あいましょう | Let's meet |
| 出ます | でます | Leaves / departs |
| 切符 | きっぷ | Ticket |
| 片道 | かたみち | One way |
| 二枚 | にまい | Two flat tickets/items |
| 二人分 | ふたりぶん | For two people |

These expressions help you parse a conversation. They do not tell you the answers to the two recordings.

## Hear the particles as clues

In **駅で会いましょう**, **で** marks where people meet. In **電車で行きます**, the same **で** marks transport. The noun and verb determine which meaning is intended.

A specific clock time often takes **に**: **午前九時に**. The destination takes **へ**: **博物館へ** (pronounced *e* as a particle). A point of departure takes **から**: **駅から**. These are different jobs; do not assume every place noun is the destination.

**片道** means *one way*. If a traveller buys two one-way tickets, that is not the same as buying a return ticket for one person. The listening questions ask only for information actually spoken.

## After your unaided attempt

Reveal both transcripts using the controls beside the players. Read each speaker's Japanese line alongside its English translation, then replay the corresponding audio. Find the exact spoken words that supported your answers. If you mixed up the meeting place and destination, listen again for **で会いましょう** versus **へ行きます**.

**Optional shadowing:** Replay a single short turn, pause, and repeat it at your own pace. This is voluntary rehearsal, not a scored pronunciation assessment. No microphone access is requested.

**Independent transfer:** Without copying a transcript, write two or three sentences about your own imaginary outing: a destination, a time and a means of travel. For example, **日曜日に公園へ行きます。午前十時に駅で会います。バスで行きます。** Compare your sentence roles with the particle guide above. This writing is ungraded.
