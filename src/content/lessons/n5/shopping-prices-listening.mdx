## Your mission: understand the checkout

**Can-do:** Ask prices, amounts and sizes politely. In this lesson, hear two different fictional shopping exchanges and identify what is bought and what is paid. Listen to the recordings **before revealing the Japanese transcripts**. Reading a script is useful review, but is not the same as hearing the answer.

### Before you press play

Imagine a stationery shop with a clerk and a customer. You know **いくらですか** (“How much?”), **ください** (“Please give me…”) and **お願いします** (“I'd like…, please”). Listen for numbers and the final choice, rather than assuming the first mentioned product is purchased.

The sound **えん** means yen. **さんびゃくえん** is 300 yen, **ろっぴゃくえん** is 600 yen, **せんえん** is 1,000 yen, and **せんにひゃくえん** is 1,200 yen. In connected speech, listen to the entire number before deciding. These are preparation words, not a transcript of either conversation.

## Track A — Two notebooks

A customer asks about a notebook, then buys more than one. Listen once for the item and color. On a second pass, catch the number of notebooks and the **total**. The counter **冊（さつ）** counts books and notebooks: **一冊（いっさつ）** one, **二冊（にさつ）** two. A unit price is not the same as a checkout total.

**Guided note-taking:** Without reading, write “color / number / total” on paper. Pause after the clerk's last line, and check whether the total makes sense.

## Track B — A small bag

A customer asks about one color but ultimately chooses another. Listen for **赤い（あかい, red）**, **黒い（くろい, black）**, and **小さい（ちいさい, small）**. The final request matters more than the first price question.

**Guided note-taking:** Make two columns, “price asked about” and “item bought.” A price quotation does not automatically mean a purchase.

## A useful contrast

| Japanese expression | Meaning | What to listen for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| この青いノートはいくらですか。 | How much is this blue notebook? | A price question, not a purchase |
| ノートを二冊ください。 | Two notebooks, please. | Quantity and request |
| 全部で六百円です。 | It's 600 yen altogether. | The checkout total |
| 黒いかばんをお願いします。 | The black bag, please. | The final selection |

The particle **を** marks the requested item in **〜をお願いします**. In **この青いノート**, **この** modifies the noun ノート; **これ青いノート** is not the same grammatical pattern. When the customer asks for a **small black bag**, both adjectives describe the bag.

## Independent listening and review

Play both tracks without reading, then answer the six questions. You may replay, pause or slow playback. **Reveal transcript** whenever needed for accessible assisted practice; that attempt will not be saved as an unaided listening score. After answering, compare each response with the Japanese line and English translation, and replay the specific spoken evidence.

For extra practice, say a fictional request such as **青いノートを一冊ください** (“One blue notebook, please”) or **小さいかばんをお願いします** (“A small bag, please”). This optional speaking rehearsal is not recorded or pronunciation-graded.

**Self-review:** Can you hear a unit price versus a total? Can you follow a change of choice? Can you hear the counter **二冊**? If not, retry a track before continuing. The quiz is formative, not an official JLPT grade.
