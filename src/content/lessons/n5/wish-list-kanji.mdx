## A wish list at the community club

At a fictional community club, three friends write down what they hope to do next weekend. Their notes are **original teaching examples**, not actual event announcements. Read the whole words with their kana first, then try covering the kana. **Can-do:** understand a simple wish list and tell whether someone wants a thing, wants to do something, or plans to go somewhere.

### Three short notes

> **A — あや**  
> 新（あたら）しい本（ほん）がほしいです。本屋（ほんや）に行（い）きたいです。  
> *Aya wants a new book. Aya wants to go to a bookstore.*

> **B — たく**  
> 土曜日（どようび）に山（やま）に行（い）きたいです。山（やま）で写真（しゃしん）を撮（と）りたいです。  
> *Taku wants to go to the mountains on Saturday. Taku wants to take photos in the mountains.*

> **C — みな**  
> 店（みせ）でかばんを見（み）たいです。でも、今（いま）は買（か）いたくないです。  
> *Mina wants to look at bags in a shop, but does not want to buy one right now.*

**Evidence hunt:** Aya's 本がほしいです describes a desired object. Taku's 行きたいです describes a desired action. Mina's 買いたくないです is a negative desire, not a prohibition.

## Six kanji, learned through words

| Character | Useful word (reading) | Meaning here | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 本 | 本（ほん） | book | A |
| 行 | 行く（いく）→ 行きたい（いきたい） | go → want to go | A, B |
| 山 | 山（やま） | mountain | B |
| 店 | 店（みせ） | shop | C |
| 見 | 見る（みる）→ 見たい（みたい） | see/look → want to see/look | C |
| 買 | 買う（かう）→ 買いたくない（かいたくない） | buy → do not want to buy | C |

Read **本屋（ほんや）** as a whole word meaning *bookstore*. It contains 本, but the second character 屋 is only recognition support here, not a seventh target to memorize. Likewise, 新しい, 土曜日, 写真, 撮る and 今 are provided with kana for comprehension, not assessed as new kanji.

### Form and reading cues

**本** has a short mark near the base of 木; in this lesson 本（ほん） means *book*. **山** resembles three peaks and is read やま here. **店** appears in 店（みせ）; the compound 本屋（ほんや） uses a different word for a bookstore. **見** is the character in 見る（みる）, *to look/see*. **買** is the character in 買う（かう）, *to buy*. **行** is read い in 行く（いく）. A character can have other readings in other words: learn these contextual readings rather than guessing one reading for every appearance.

## Reading the wishes accurately

**Noun + がほしいです** expresses wanting a thing: **本がほしいです** = “I want a book.” In the examples, the speaker is understood. Do not attach ほしい directly to the verb 行く to mean “I want to go.”

For your own desire to perform an action, take the **ます-stem** and add **たいです**:

| Dictionary form | ます-stem | Wish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 行く（いく） | 行き | 行きたいです — want to go |
| 見る（みる） | 見 | 見たいです — want to look |
| 買う（かう） | 買い | 買いたいです — want to buy |

For a negative desire, **買いたい** becomes **買いたくないです**. This does not mean “must not buy.” With a destination, **山に行きたいです** means “I want to go to the mountains”; **に** marks the destination. With an action location, **店で見たいです** means “I want to look in a shop”; **で** marks where the action takes place. In **かばんを見たいです**, **を** marks what is looked at. (In some たい constructions が is also possible, but を is clear and natural here.)

**Careful about other people's wishes:** these are fictional first-person notes. Do not automatically use ほしいです or たいです to assert someone else's private desires without evidence. When reporting a note in English, we know what the fictional writer stated.

## Guided practice

1. Underline **本** twice in Aya's note. Read **本** as ほん, then read **本屋** as ほんや.
2. Find **山** in Taku's two sentences. Which **に** marks the destination, and which **で** marks the location of photo-taking?
3. Find **見** and **買** in Mina's note. Is Mina refusing to enter a shop? No: she wants to look but does not want to buy now.
4. Cover the English translations. For each note, say which line contains **ほしい**, **たい**, or **たくない**.
5. Compare **本がほしいです** (want a book) with **本を買いたいです** (want to buy a book). The former focuses on the desired item; the latter on the action.

### Common corrections

- **× 本をほしいです → ○ 本がほしいです.** The beginner noun pattern takes が.
- **× 山で行きたいです → ○ 山に行きたいです.** Use に for the destination of 行く.
- **× 買うたいです → ○ 買いたいです.** Add たい to the ます-stem, not the dictionary form.
- **× 買いたいないです → ○ 買いたくないです.** The negative of たい changes たい to たくない.

## Independent writing and self-review

Write two or three sentences as a fictional club member. State one thing you want, one place you want to go, and optionally one action you want to do there. Try without the model first.

> 本（ほん）がほしいです。本屋（ほんや）に行（い）きたいです。店（みせ）で本（ほん）を見（み）たいです。  
> *I want a book. I want to go to a bookstore. I want to look at books in the shop.*

**Ungraded self-check:** (1) Is the desired object followed by がほしいです? (2) Does each verb use its ます-stem before たい? (3) Does に mark the destination? (4) Does で mark an action location? (5) Can a reader understand the short plan? Writing is not automatically graded.
