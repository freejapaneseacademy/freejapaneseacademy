## A journal about ordinary habits

**Chapter Can-do:** Explain how often you do familiar activities. Today you will read the *written shapes* of everyday activity words, not memorize isolated characters with every possible pronunciation. Some kanji below are review words from earlier daily-routine lessons; the new focus is reading them fluently together in a habit journal.

Hana (はな), a fictional student, keeps this short journal about her week. Read the lines first with the reading guide, then cover the guide and try again.

### はなの習慣（しゅうかん）— Hana's habits

> 毎朝、家で本を読みます。  
> 毎日、学校で日本語を勉強します。  
> 毎週土曜日、よく図書館へ行きます。  
> 図書館では、あまり動画を見ません。  
> 日曜日は、ときどき日記を書きます。  
> 夜はテレビをぜんぜん見ません。

**English:** Every morning I read a book at home. Every day I study Japanese at school. Every Saturday I often go to the library. At the library I do not watch videos very much. On Sundays I sometimes write in my diary. At night I do not watch television at all.

**Kana reading guide:**  
まいあさ、いえで ほんを よみます。  
まいにち、がっこうで にほんごを べんきょうします。  
まいしゅう どようび、よく としょかんへ いきます。  
としょかんでは、あまり どうがを みません。  
にちようびは、ときどき にっきを かきます。  
よるは テレビを ぜんぜん みません。

## Read kanji as words, not as a list of isolated symbols

| Word | Reading | Meaning | Context clue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 毎日 | まいにち | every day | 毎日、勉強します |
| 毎朝 | まいあさ | every morning | 毎朝、本を読みます |
| 毎週 | まいしゅう | every week | 毎週土曜日 |
| 本 | ほん | book | 本を読みます |
| 読みます | よみます | read (polite) | 本を読みます |
| 勉強します | べんきょうします | study (polite) | 日本語を勉強します |
| 学校 | がっこう | school | 学校で |
| 図書館 | としょかん | library | 図書館へ行きます |
| 行きます | いきます | go (polite) | 図書館へ行きます |
| 日曜日 | にちようび | Sunday | 日曜日は |
| 土曜日 | どようび | Saturday | 毎週土曜日 |
| 日記 | にっき | diary | 日記を書きます |
| 書きます | かきます | write (polite) | 日記を書きます |
| 見ません | みません | do not watch/see | 動画を見ません |
| 夜 | よる | night/evening | 夜は |

**Character connections:** 毎 appears in 毎日, 毎朝 and 毎週 and signals a repeating interval. 日 can be read にち in 毎日 and 日曜日; don't assume one fixed reading for every compound. 読 (read) and 書 (write) refer to different actions, even though both can involve a book or diary. The reading of 日記 is **にっき**, with a small っ; it is not にちき.

**Reading strategy:** Notice the full word and its particles. In **本を読みます**, を marks what is read. In **学校で勉強します**, で marks the place where studying happens. In **図書館へ行きます**, へ marks direction; it is pronounced え as a particle.

## Frequency words stay important

Kanji recognition is useful only when it preserves the sentence's meaning.

- **よく図書館へ行きます。** — I often go to the library.
- **ときどき日記を書きます。** — I sometimes write in my diary.
- **動画はあまり見ません。** — I do not watch videos very much.
- **テレビはぜんぜん見ません。** — I do not watch television at all.

In these beginner patterns, **あまり** and **ぜんぜん** accompany negative verbs. **あまり見ます** is not the intended standard beginner expression. **あまり見ません** does not mean never; **ぜんぜん見ません** expresses none at all. The journal says **毎週土曜日** (every Saturday), not every day. It also uses **よく** to describe a frequent habit, not a numerical count.

## Controlled recognition

1. Point to 毎 in **毎朝** and **毎週**. Say the two whole-word readings, **まいあさ** and **まいしゅう**.
2. Read **本を読みます** and **日記を書きます**. Which action is reading, and which is writing?
3. In **図書館では、あまり動画を見ません**, underline the location, frequency expression and negative verb separately.

**Check:** 読みます is read; 書きます is write. 図書館 is the location, あまり indicates “not much,” and 見ません is the negative verb.

## Guided reconstruction

Start with **毎日、日本語を勉強します。** (“I study Japanese every day.”) Replace 毎日 with **毎朝** to say “every morning.” Then try **ときどき日本語を勉強します。** (“I sometimes study Japanese.”) This last sentence uses a frequency adverb rather than a fixed interval.

For a contrasting negative sentence, begin with **テレビはぜんぜん見ません。** Change **ぜんぜん** to **あまり** and explain why the meaning becomes less absolute.

## Independent writing and optional speaking

Write three short lines for an *imaginary* character: one with 毎日 or 毎週, one with 読みます or 書きます, and one with あまり〜ません or ぜんぜん〜ません. Add kana above or beside any kanji you cannot yet retrieve from memory. Compare your sentences with the journal for particle placement and verb endings. This open writing is **not automatically graded**.

You may read your three sentences aloud at your own pace, but this written L04 contains no audio recording or pronunciation score. A separate L06 will provide real listening practice once its licensed media is ready.
