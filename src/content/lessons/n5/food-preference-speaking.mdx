## Optional speaking mission: choosing lunch together

**Can-do:** Name common foods and express simple preferences in a short, polite interaction. This speaking rehearsal is optional. It is **not** a JLPT speaking test, and you may complete the chapter without recording your voice.

Imagine two classmates, みお and たく, deciding what to eat at a café. First read their exchange for meaning. Then cover the Japanese and try saying the turns from the English prompts.

**みお：** 何（なに）が好きですか。  
**たく：** カレーが好きです。みおさんは？  
**みお：** わたしはパンとサラダが好きです。  
**たく：** 今日（きょう）は何を食（た）べますか。  
**みお：** サラダを食べます。水（みず）を飲（の）みます。  
**たく：** だれと食べますか。  
**みお：** 友（とも）だちと食べます。

**English meaning:** Mio asks what Taku likes. Taku likes curry and asks Mio in return. Mio likes bread and salad. Taku asks what she will eat today. Mio will eat salad and drink water. Asked who she will eat with, she says a friend.

This is an **original written model**, not a recording of these exact lines. To hear real Japanese café dialogue before rehearsing, visit [the chapter listening lesson](/learn/n5/module-04/chapter-01/lesson-06/) and use its playback and transcript reveal. Do not assume that its recording matches this script word for word. You can also ask a partner to read one role; no microphone access is needed.

## Hear the meaning of the particles

| Pattern | What it communicates | Speaking cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| カレー**が**好きです。 | I like curry. | Name the liked thing before が. |
| サラダ**を**食べます。 | I'll eat salad. | Name the food receiving the action before を. |
| 水**を**飲みます。 | I'll drink water. | Use を with the drink being consumed. |
| パン**と**サラダが好きです。 | I like bread and salad. | と joins the two named foods. |
| 友だち**と**食べます。 | I'll eat with a friend. | と marks the companion. |

**Common repair:** Avoid *カレーを好きです*; the introductory pattern is **カレーが好きです**. Avoid *サラダが食べます* when you mean “I eat salad”; say **サラダを食べます**. **嫌（きら）いです** expresses dislike and may sound stronger than **好きではありません** (“do not like”); choose a phrase that reflects your intended meaning.

## Controlled rehearsal: three substitutions

Say each sentence aloud or silently, replacing the bracketed word. Check against the complete model after each attempt.

1. **［カレー］が好きです。** → **パンが好きです。** “I like bread.”
2. **［サラダ］を食べます。** → **カレーを食べます。** “I'll eat curry.”
3. **［友だち］と食べます。** → **母（はは）と食べます。** “I'll eat with my mother.”

Try one more round using **お茶（ちゃ）を飲みます** (“I'll drink tea”). Keep **が**, **を** and **と** in their appropriate roles.

## Guided partner conversation

Partner A asks **何が好きですか。** (“What do you like?”). Partner B replies **りんごが好きです。** (“I like apples.”). Partner A asks **何を食べますか。** (“What will you eat?”). Partner B replies **パンを食べます。** (“I'll eat bread.”). Switch roles. For a companion question, add **だれと食べますか。** (“Who will you eat with?”) and answer **友だちと食べます。** (“I'll eat with a friend.”)

**Independent transfer:** Invent a new visitor. Give them a food preference, a food or drink choice and a companion. Speak three to five short connected turns. If you are studying alone, speak both roles or type the lines first. No speech is sent to the site.

## Self-review, not automatic pronunciation grading

Use the optional checklist in the practice section. Give yourself one point for each of four observable criteria: (1) you asked or answered a preference question, (2) が marked the liked food, (3) を marked an eaten or drunk item, and (4) と joined foods or marked a companion. A self-check is **not** a verified fluency or pronunciation grade. Repeat any uncertain turn while consulting the Japanese and English model.

**Reading bridge:** Re-read the dialogue and identify Mio's food and drink. **Listening bridge:** Compare the meaning of your rehearsal with the authentic chapter audio in L06; reveal its transcript only after listening. **Writing bridge:** Type a two- to five-sentence version of your invented visitor's lunch choice and compare it with the L07 writing rubric.
