## The noticeboard planner

**Chapter Can-do:** Say what happened yesterday and what is planned. This L04 focuses on **recognizing and using kanji in real words**, not memorizing every possible reading of a character.

At a fictional community center, two students keep a shared planner. Read the labels first; then read the complete notes. Kanji are shown with kana support at first.

| Planner label | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 昨日 | きのう | yesterday |
| 今日 | きょう | today |
| 明日 | あした | tomorrow |
| 朝 | あさ | morning |
| 昼 | ひる | daytime / noon |
| 夜 | よる | night / evening |
| 午前 | ごぜん | a.m. / before noon |
| 午後 | ごご | p.m. / after noon |
| 行きました | いきました | went (polite past) |
| 読みました | よみました | read (polite past) |
| 書きます | かきます | write / will write (polite nonpast) |
| 勉強します | べんきょうします | study / will study |

**Reading warning:** 昨日, 今日 and 明日 are whole-word readings: **きのう**, **きょう** and **あした**. Do not try to assemble these readings by pronouncing each kanji separately. 明日 also has the reading あす in other contexts; here the planner uses あした.

## Original planner: Mina and Sora

**昨日（きのう）・月曜日（げつようび）**  
ミナ：午後（ごご）三時（さんじ）に図書室（としょしつ）へ行（い）きました。日本語（にほんご）の本（ほん）を読（よ）みました。

**今日（きょう）・火曜日（かようび）**  
ソラ：朝（あさ）、漢字（かんじ）を勉強（べんきょう）します。昼（ひる）は休（やす）みます。

**明日（あした）・水曜日（すいようび）**  
ミナ：午前（ごぜん）十時（じゅうじ）に図書室へ行きます。午後（ごご）、短（みじか）い文（ぶん）を書（か）きます。

**English:** Yesterday, Monday, Mina went to the library room at 3 p.m. and read a Japanese book. Today, Tuesday, Sora studies kanji in the morning and rests at midday. Tomorrow, Wednesday, Mina plans to go to the library room at 10 a.m. and write short sentences in the afternoon. These dates are relative to the fictional planner, **not today's real calendar**.

## Read kanji as parts of words

**行** appears in **行きました（いきました）** and **行きます（いきます）**. Both describe going; the ending changes the time interpretation. **読** in **読みました（よみました）** marks reading; **書** in **書きます（かきます）** marks writing. **勉強（べんきょう）** is a two-kanji word meaning study; in **勉強します** it forms a polite verb expression.

Notice that **朝（あさ）** is a broad part of the day, while **午前十時（ごぜんじゅうじ）** identifies a specific a.m. clock time. **午後三時** is 3 p.m., not 3 a.m. **夜（よる）** can refer to evening or night; it is not the same as **午後**, which covers the entire p.m. period.

### The script changes, not the sentence logic

- **昨日、図書室へ行きました。** （きのう、としょしつへいきました。） — Yesterday I went to the library room.
- **明日、図書室へ行きます。** （あした、としょしつへいきます。） — Tomorrow I will go to the library room.
- **昨日、本を読みませんでした。** （きのう、ほんをよみませんでした。） — Yesterday I did not read a book.
- **明日、文を書きます。** （あした、ぶんをかきます。） — Tomorrow I will write sentences.

The written particle **へ** is pronounced **え** when it marks direction. The written particle **を** is normally pronounced **お**. The kanji spelling does not change these particle rules. **ました** marks polite past affirmative; **ませんでした** marks polite past negative. **ます** is nonpast, so a future reading depends on context such as 明日.

## Avoid these traps

**昨日（きのう）** is not pronounced *さくひ* in this ordinary planner. **今日（きょう）** is not the same word as **今朝（けさ）**, which means this morning. Do not treat **午後** as a synonym for **夜**. Also, **明日、行きました** combines tomorrow with a past verb and is not the intended ordinary future-plan sentence: use **明日、行きます**.

## Controlled recognition

Find the exact Japanese evidence in the planner.

1. Which heading contains **きのう**? Point to the kanji word.
2. Which action does Mina complete yesterday: **読みました** or **書きます**?
3. Which phrase names 10 a.m.? How is it different from **午後三時**?
4. What does Sora do **朝**? Find the two-kanji noun for the subject of study.

**Evidence check:** 昨日, 本を読みました, 午前十時, and 漢字を勉強します are the relevant phrases.

## Guided reconstruction

Read the kana-only note **あした、ごぜんじゅうじにとしょしつへいきます。** Rebuild it with the kanji you know: **明日、午前十時に図書室へ行きます。** (Tomorrow I will go to the library room at 10 a.m.)

Now change yesterday's action **きのう、ほんをよみました。** into **昨日、本を読みました。** (Yesterday I read a book.) You do not need to know every component kanji independently to read a familiar word.

## Independent writing and self-review

Write **two** short fictional planner sentences: one past action using **昨日** and **ました／ませんでした**, and one future plan using **明日** and **ます**. Include one action kanji from **行・読・書**, and a time-of-day word such as **朝・午後・夜**. A possible model:

**昨日の夜、本を読みました。明日の朝、短い文を書きます。**  
（きのうのよる、ほんをよみました。あしたのあさ、みじかいぶんをかきます。）  
“Last night I read a book. Tomorrow morning I will write short sentences.”

Self-review four criteria: (1) correct day word; (2) accurate familiar kanji and kana; (3) verb ending agrees with past or future; (4) clear activity and time. Other natural sentences are valid. This composition is **not automatically graded**. Optional speaking: read the model aloud without recording; verified audio listening is a separate L06 activity.

## Review

You can now find yesterday, today and tomorrow in a real-looking planner; distinguish morning, daytime, evening and a.m./p.m.; and recognize the activity kanji in complete words. Next, L05 uses a longer calendar note for evidence-based reading.
