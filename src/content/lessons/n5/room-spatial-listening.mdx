## Listen as a visitor, not as a reader

Imagine visiting two different rooms. In **Track A**, a guide shows a visitor a shared study room. In **Track B**, a host shows a guest another room where a cat is resting. The two rooms are **not** the same location. Your task is to follow spoken directions and identify where things are, without needing to recognize their written kanji.

### Listening toolkit

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 机 | つくえ | desk |
| 棚 | たな | shelf |
| 窓 | まど | window |
| 時計 | とけい | clock |
| 上 | うえ | above / on |
| 下 | した | below / under |
| 前 | まえ | in front of |
| 後ろ | うしろ | behind |
| 中 | なか | inside |
| 隣 | となり | next to |

Listen for **X の [position] に Y が あります** to locate an object relative to X. For an animal, **います** replaces **あります**. The topic form **本は棚の上にあります** is also natural when answering a question about a particular book. In speech, the particle **の** links the reference noun to the position, **に** marks the location, and **が** introduces what exists.

### Track A: the study room

Listen once for the purpose of the conversation. On the second play, imagine a desk and place the chair and bag around it. Finally listen for the visitor's observation about the window. Notice that the visitor asks **どこ** (“where”), and the guide answers with two different positions.

### Track B: the guest room

This tour introduces a shelf, a cat and a bed. Listen for the contrast between **中** (inside) and **上** (on top). The host answers the question about the cat using **います**. The last two turns contain a useful correction: the book is **not** on the bed; the host then states where it actually is.

### Three listening passes

1. Play both recordings without revealing the written transcripts. Name each room's main topic in English.
2. Replay and sketch a quick room plan: mark the desk, chair, bag and window for Track A; mark the shelf, chair, cat and bed for Track B.
3. Answer all six questions from what you heard. After checking, reveal the transcripts to compare your mental map with the actual spoken words. Opening any transcript labels the attempt **transcript-assisted**, even if it is hidden again.

### Guided writing and reflection

Write three original sentences about an imaginary room: one with **上** or **下**, one with **前** or **後ろ**, and one about a person or animal using **います**. For example, **机の上に本があります。いすの後ろにかばんがあります。部屋に猫がいます。** Check that the location has **に**, the item being located has **が** when introducing it, and that people or animals take **います**. This writing is self-reviewed and is not automatically graded.

**Optional speaking:** After authentic audio is available, replay a sentence and shadow its rhythm. Ask **本はどこにありますか。** and answer **棚の上にあります。** Record only if you personally choose to; no microphone access or pronunciation grade is required.

### Review

Can you distinguish **上** from **下**, and **前** from **後ろ**, without opening the transcript? Can you explain why **猫がいます** differs from **本があります**? Revisit the recordings and the feedback for any missed answers.
