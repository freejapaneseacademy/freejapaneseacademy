## A message about getting to the museum

**Chapter can-do:** Explain transport choices and who accompanies you. In this script lesson, recognize written travel words rather than memorizing isolated kanji readings. Read the fictional messages, using the kana guide whenever needed.

> **あき:** <span lang="ja">日曜日、だれと博物館に行きますか。</span>
>
> **けん:** <span lang="ja">父と電車で行きます。あきさんは？</span>
>
> **あき:** <span lang="ja">私は母とバスで行きます。</span>
>
> **けん:** <span lang="ja">友だちは？</span>
>
> **あき:** <span lang="ja">友だちは自転車で行きます。一人で行きます。</span>

**English:** Aki asks who Ken will go to the museum with on Sunday. Ken says he will go with his father by train. Aki will go with her mother by bus. Her friend will go by bicycle, alone. The conversation does **not** say the friend is travelling with Aki.

**Reading support:** 日曜日（にちようび） Sunday; 博物館（はくぶつかん） museum; 父（ちち） my father; 母（はは） my mother; 電車（でんしゃ） train; 友だち（ともだち） friend; 自転車（じてんしゃ） bicycle; 一人（ひとり） one person / alone in 一人で.

## Read whole words before studying individual characters

| Written word | Kana reading | Meaning in this lesson | Notice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">車</span> | くるま | car | A whole-word reading; not しゃ by itself here |
| <span lang="ja">電車</span> | でんしゃ | train | 車 is read しゃ in this compound |
| <span lang="ja">自転車</span> | じてんしゃ | bicycle | The last 車 is also しゃ |
| <span lang="ja">一人</span> | ひとり | one person; alone | Irregular whole-word reading |
| <span lang="ja">二人</span> | ふたり | two people | Not ににん in ordinary counting |
| <span lang="ja">友だち</span> | ともだち | friend | 友 is とも; だち remains kana here |
| <span lang="ja">母</span> | はは | my mother | Used for one's own mother in neutral reference |
| <span lang="ja">父</span> | ちち | my father | Used for one's own father in neutral reference |

**Kanji focus:** 車 can be read **くるま** as a standalone word and **しゃ** in 電車 and 自転車. Readings depend on the word. The character 人 is **ひと** in some words, but **一人（ひとり）** and **二人（ふたり）** must be learned as complete forms. Do not pronounce them by mechanically combining isolated character readings.

## Three sentence patterns in writing

**Means of travel:** <span lang="ja">電車で行きます。</span>（でんしゃでいきます） — “I go by train.” The vehicle takes **で**. Compare <span lang="ja">車で行きます。</span>（くるまでいきます） — “I go by car.”

**Companion:** <span lang="ja">父と行きます。</span>（ちちといきます） — “I go with my father.” The person takes **と**. Compare <span lang="ja">友だちと行きます。</span>（ともだちといきます） — “I go with a friend.”

**Alone:** <span lang="ja">一人で行きます。</span>（ひとりでいきます） — “I go alone.” This **で** does not specify a vehicle. Someone can go alone **by train**: <span lang="ja">一人で電車で行きます。</span> This is grammatical, though shorter separate sentences may be easier for beginners.

**A useful contrast:** <span lang="ja">二人で行きます。</span>（ふたりでいきます） means “We go as two people” or “Two people go,” depending on context. It gives a number of travellers, not the identity of the companion. <span lang="ja">母と行きます</span> identifies the companion.

## Find the kanji in context

Read the two original travel notes:

> **Note A:** <span lang="ja">月曜日、母と車で学校に行きます。</span>
>
> **Note B:** <span lang="ja">土曜日、友だちと二人で電車で公園に行きます。</span>

**Kana guides:** 月曜日（げつようび） Monday; 学校（がっこう） school; 土曜日（どようび） Saturday; 公園（こうえん） park.

**Meaning A:** “On Monday, I go to school with my mother by car.” **母と** gives the companion; **車で** gives the means.

**Meaning B:** “On Saturday, I go to the park by train with a friend; there are two of us.” **友だちと** identifies the companion, **二人で** gives the number of travellers, and **電車で** names the transport.

**Important distinction:** <span lang="ja">電車と行きます</span> is not the normal way to say “go by train”; use **電車で行きます**. <span lang="ja">母で行きます</span> does not mean “go with my mother”; use **母と行きます**. In this context, the で in **二人で** describes the group, not a vehicle.

## Controlled practice: notice, read, and retrieve

1. Cover the kana column. Read **車 → 電車 → 自転車** aloud, then uncover the readings: **くるま → でんしゃ → じてんしゃ**.
2. Compare **一人（ひとり）** and **二人（ふたり）**. Say what changes in meaning: one person versus two.
3. Find the written word for “father” in Ken's reply: **父**. Find the vehicle: **電車**.
4. Underline the companion in Note A (**母と**) and circle the means (**車で**). In Note B underline **友だちと** and circle **電車で**.

## Guided typing and optional handwriting

With a Japanese IME, type **でんしゃ** and select **電車**; type **じてんしゃ** and select **自転車**; type **ひとり** and select **一人**. Conversion candidates may differ by device, so check the final characters and reading rather than the number of keystrokes. If you cannot use an IME, copy the printed words by hand or type kana; kana is a valid support during practice.

Complete this model: <span lang="ja">私は＿＿と＿＿で公園に行きます。</span> — “I go to the park with ___ by ___.” One valid completion is <span lang="ja">私は父と電車で公園に行きます。</span>（わたしはちちとでんしゃでこうえんにいきます）. Another is **母と車で**. Avoid using **一人と** to mean “alone”; use **一人で** instead.

## Independent mini-task

Write **two or three short sentences** about a fictional trip. Include a destination, either a companion with **と** or **一人で**, and a means with **で**. For example:

> <span lang="ja">日曜日、博物館に行きます。父と行きます。電車で行きます。</span>

“On Sunday I will go to the museum. I will go with my father. I will go by train.”

**Self-review rubric (not automatically scored):** Can you (1) read each kanji word with its correct whole-word reading, (2) identify the companion or say you are alone, (3) identify the vehicle using **で**, and (4) match your English meaning to the Japanese? Try reading the model aloud as optional text-based rehearsal. There is no recording, audio playback or pronunciation evaluation in this L04.
