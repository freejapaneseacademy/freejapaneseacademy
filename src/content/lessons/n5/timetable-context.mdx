## A notice before Saturday's workshop

**Chapter can-do:** Understand dates, time and basic counters. At a community center, volunteer Aki shows new visitor Yuna a printed timetable for a beginner calligraphy session. This lesson uses an **original written notice**, not a recording. The people and schedule are fictional.

> **書道（しょどう）ワークショップ** — Calligraphy workshop  
> **日（ひ）にち：** 11月（じゅういちがつ）14日（じゅうよっか）、土曜日（どようび）  
> **時間（じかん）：** 午前（ごぜん）10時（じゅうじ）から11時（じゅういちじ）まで  
> **場所（ばしょ）：** 2階（にかい）の教室（きょうしつ）  
> **参加（さんか）：** 3人（さんにん）  
> **紙（かみ）：** 1人（ひとり）に2枚（にまい）  
> **参加費（さんかひ）：** ありません（free of charge）

The date is **November 14** in an unspecified year; the weekday is part of this *fictional* schedule, not a claim about the current calendar. The session runs from **10:00 to 11:00 a.m.** Three people are expected. Each participant receives two sheets of paper.

| Speaker | Japanese | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yuna | <span lang="ja">ワークショップは いつですか。</span> | When is the workshop? |
| Aki | <span lang="ja">11月14日です。</span> | It is November 14. |
| Yuna | <span lang="ja">何時（なんじ）からですか。</span> | What time does it start? |
| Aki | <span lang="ja">午前10時からです。</span> | It starts at 10 a.m. |
| Yuna | <span lang="ja">何人（なんにん）ですか。</span> | How many people? |
| Aki | <span lang="ja">3人です。</span> | Three people. |

## 1. Follow the information trail

Yuna wants to know **when** the workshop happens and **how many** people are participating. Aki points to the notice. Read the notice once for its main purpose, then again for four pieces of evidence: date, start time, number of people, and paper per person.

The written notice has two different quantities: **3人（さんにん）** counts people, while **2枚（にまい）** counts thin flat sheets. Do not read them as interchangeable "three" and "two" without the counters.

## 2. Reading dates and time

| Form | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">11月</span> | じゅういちがつ | November |
| <span lang="ja">14日</span> | じゅうよっか | the 14th day |
| <span lang="ja">土曜日</span> | どようび | Saturday |
| <span lang="ja">午前10時</span> | ごぜんじゅうじ | 10 a.m. |
| <span lang="ja">11時</span> | じゅういちじ | 11 o'clock |
| <span lang="ja">何時</span> | なんじ | what time? |

Dates require care: **14日** is **じゅうよっか**, not a mechanical reading of the number 14 followed by にち. **10時** is **じゅうじ**; **11時** is **じゅういちじ**. **午前** means a.m.; **午後（ごご）** means p.m.

In <span lang="ja">午前10時から11時まで</span>, **から** marks the starting point and **まで** the endpoint. The end time shares the morning context here. A session described as <span lang="ja">午前10時から</span> begins at 10 a.m.; it does not end then.

## 3. People and sheets: counters in context

| Written | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">1人</span> | ひとり | one person |
| <span lang="ja">2人</span> | ふたり | two people |
| <span lang="ja">3人</span> | さんにん | three people |
| <span lang="ja">1枚</span> | いちまい | one sheet |
| <span lang="ja">2枚</span> | にまい | two sheets |
| <span lang="ja">何人</span> | なんにん | how many people? |

**人** is a counter for people, with irregular readings **ひとり** and **ふたり**. **枚** counts flat objects such as sheets of paper. The broader **〜つ** counter (for example, **ひとつ**, one item; **ふたつ**, two items) is useful for general things, but this notice uses the more specific **枚** for paper. Counters are not simply added to an English plural.

**Common error:** <span lang="ja">3枚です</span> cannot answer the question <span lang="ja">何人ですか</span> about participants. Answer <span lang="ja">3人です</span>. Likewise, <span lang="ja">2人の紙</span> is not the notice's "two sheets"; use <span lang="ja">紙2枚</span> or <span lang="ja">紙が2枚</span> in a suitable sentence.

## 4. Controlled evidence retrieval

Cover the English gloss and find the exact printed evidence for each answer: When is the workshop? What time does it begin? How many participants? How many sheets does each participant receive?

**Evidence key:** <span lang="ja">11月14日</span>; <span lang="ja">午前10時から</span>; <span lang="ja">3人</span>; <span lang="ja">1人に2枚</span>.

## 5. Guided transfer

A different event has **two people**, begins at **1 p.m.**, and gives each person **one sheet**. Choose from <span lang="ja">ふたり・ごご・いちまい</span> to complete the three phrases:

1. <span lang="ja">参加は ______ です。</span>
2. <span lang="ja">______ 1時からです。</span>
3. <span lang="ja">1人に ______ です。</span>

**Check:** <span lang="ja">ふたり</span>; <span lang="ja">ごご</span>; <span lang="ja">いちまい</span>. The new event is hypothetical, not an amendment to Aki's notice.

## 6. Independent mini-notice

Write a three-line notice for an imagined study group: choose a month/day, a start time with **午前** or **午後**, and a participant count using **人**. Optionally add a number of sheets using **枚**. Model for a *different* event:

> <span lang="ja">日（ひ）にち：12月（じゅうにがつ）5日（いつか）</span>  
> <span lang="ja">時間（じかん）：午後（ごご）2時（にじ）から</span>  
> <span lang="ja">参加（さんか）：2人（ふたり）</span>

Self-check: (1) the month and day match your intention, (2) a.m./p.m. is explicit, (3) **人** counts people, and (4) **枚** counts paper if included. This free writing is **not automatically graded**. Reading and listening recur in later lessons; L06 will use actual date-announcement audio rather than pretending this notice can be heard.
