## Plan a club meeting

**Optional speaking / shadowing.** Imagine two students arranging a fictional club gathering. You can practise alone by reading both roles aloud, or with a partner. No microphone, recording, automatic speech recognition or pronunciation grade is used. The dialogues are original **written** models; no unverified audio is presented as a recording.

### 1. Your speaking toolkit

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning / use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| いつですか | いつですか | When is it? |
| 何日ですか | なんにちですか | What date is it? |
| 何時からですか | なんじからですか | What time does it start? |
| 午前 / 午後 | ごぜん / ごご | a.m. / p.m. |
| 何人 | なんにん | How many people? |
| 一人 / 二人 | ひとり / ふたり | One / two people |
| 何枚 | なんまい | How many flat items? |
| 何枚いりますか | なんまいいりますか | How many sheets are needed? |
| いくつ | いくつ | How many items? |
| 〜から | 〜から | From / starting at (a time) |

**Counter choice matters.** 四人（よにん） is four people; 三枚（さんまい） is three sheets; 二つ（ふたつ） is two general objects. Never use 枚 for people. **何時から** asks the start time; **何時ですか** can simply ask the time. Dates have special readings: 18日（じゅうはちにち）, 20日（はつか）. **午後3時** means 3 p.m., not 3 a.m.

### 2. Model dialogue A — make a plan

**A:** クラブの集まりはいつですか。  
*Kurabu no atsumari wa itsu desu ka?* — When is the club meeting?

**B:** 11月18日です。  
*Jūichi-gatsu jūhachi-nichi desu.* — It is November 18.

**A:** 何時からですか。  
*Nanji kara desu ka?* — What time does it start?

**B:** 午後3時からです。  
*Gogo san-ji kara desu.* — It starts at 3 p.m.

**A:** 何人来ますか。  
*Nannin kimasu ka?* — How many people are coming?

**B:** 四人来ます。  
*Yonin kimasu.* — Four people are coming.

**Why it works:** The date is a calendar date, not a duration. **から** attaches to the starting time; **四人** counts people. This exchange uses polite です / ます endings throughout.

### 3. Model dialogue B — prepare materials

**A:** 紙は何枚いりますか。  
*Kami wa nanmai irimasu ka?* — How many sheets of paper do we need?

**B:** 三枚いります。  
*Sanmai irimasu.* — We need three sheets.

**A:** りんごはいくつありますか。  
*Ringo wa ikutsu arimasu ka?* — How many apples are there?

**B:** 二つあります。  
*Futatsu arimasu.* — There are two.

**A:** 練習は何日ですか。  
*Renshū wa nannichi desu ka?* — On what date is the rehearsal?

**B:** 11月20日です。  
*Jūichi-gatsu hatsuka desu.* — It is November 20.

**Notice:** **あります** describes the existence of inanimate items; **いります** means “are needed.” They sound similar but express different ideas. **二つ** counts general objects; **二人** counts people.

### 4. Guided rehearsal: switch the roles

Read dialogue A slowly. First speak **A**, leaving a pause for the printed **B** line; then speak **B**, responding to each A question. Repeat dialogue B the same way. The interactive role switch below shows one role at a time so you can rehearse without looking at both sides.

Try these substitutions without changing the grammar:

| Original | Change | Model answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11月18日 | November 20 | 11月20日です。 |
| 午後3時 | 10 a.m. | 午前10時からです。 |
| 四人 | two people | 二人来ます。 |
| 三枚 | five sheets | 五枚いります。 |
| 二つ | one apple | 一つあります。 |

**Common mistake:** *二人あります* is not a natural way to say that two people are present. Say **二人います**. For the number expected to come, **二人来ます** is appropriate.

### 5. Independent speaking task (optional)

Imagine a different fictional meeting: **12月4日、午前10時、三人、紙を四枚**. Ask and answer at least three questions about the date, time and supplies. Then reverse roles. A possible exchange is:

**A:** 集まりは何日ですか。 **B:** 12月4日です。  
**A:** 何時からですか。 **B:** 午前10時からです。  
**A:** 何人来ますか。 **B:** 三人来ます。  
**A:** 紙は何枚いりますか。 **B:** 四枚いります。

Read 4日 as **よっか**, 10時 as **じゅうじ**, 三人 as **さんにん**, and 四枚 as **よんまい**. Other natural polite answers are possible.

### 6. Self-review, not a speech grade

Check four things: (1) your listener could identify the date, (2) your time includes the correct 午前/午後 and から when relevant, (3) your counter matches people, sheets or general objects, and (4) your questions and answers remain polite.
