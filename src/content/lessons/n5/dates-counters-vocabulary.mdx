## A calendar for the culture club

**Can-do:** Understand dates, time and basic counters. A fictional school culture club is preparing an event. You will read a calendar, count visitors and supplies, and choose natural Japanese expressions. This is written vocabulary study, not verified listening or pronunciation practice.

## 1. The first ten dates are special

Japanese dates are often written with a number followed by 日. The first ten dates have important special readings: learn each as a whole word rather than adding にち to a number.

| Written date | Reading | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">一日</span> | ついたち | first day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">二日</span> | ふつか | second day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">三日</span> | みっか | third day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">四日</span> | よっか | fourth day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">五日</span> | いつか | fifth day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">六日</span> | むいか | sixth day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">七日</span> | なのか | seventh day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">八日</span> | ようか | eighth day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">九日</span> | ここのか | ninth day of the month |
| <span lang="ja">十日</span> | とおか | tenth day of the month |

**一日** has two common readings: **ついたち** for the first of a month, but **いちにち** when it means a duration of one day. Context determines the reading. **五日（いつか）** means the fifth (or five days); it is not the same word as **いつか** meaning “someday,” despite the shared sound.

## 2. Months and times

The month name normally takes **月（がつ）**. Pay attention to **四月（しがつ）**, **七月（しちがつ）** and **九月（くがつ）**; **三月（さんがつ）** means March.

<span lang="ja">イベントは四月八日です。</span> (イベントは しがつ ようかです。) — The event is on April 8.

<span lang="ja">イベントは午後三時です。</span> (イベントは ごご さんじです。) — The event is at 3 p.m. **午前（ごぜん）** is a.m. and **午後（ごご）** is p.m. The particle **は** sets the topic; **です** completes the statement.

## 3. Count the right kind of thing

| Expression | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">一人</span> | ひとり | one person |
| <span lang="ja">二人</span> | ふたり | two people |
| <span lang="ja">三人</span> | さんにん | three people |
| <span lang="ja">一枚</span> | いちまい | one flat item, e.g. a ticket |
| <span lang="ja">二枚</span> | にまい | two flat items |
| <span lang="ja">五枚</span> | ごまい | five flat items |
| <span lang="ja">一つ</span> | ひとつ | one general object |
| <span lang="ja">二つ</span> | ふたつ | two general objects |
| <span lang="ja">三つ</span> | みっつ | three general objects |

**人** counts people, **枚** counts flat things such as paper and tickets, and **〜つ** counts many general objects. Some nouns permit more than one counter depending on context. These are practical beginner choices.

<span lang="ja">学生が二人います。</span> (がくせいが ふたり います。) — There are two students.

<span lang="ja">チケットが二枚あります。</span> (チケットが にまい あります。) — There are two tickets.

<span lang="ja">りんごが二つあります。</span> (りんごが ふたつ あります。) — There are two apples.

The number **二** alone does not determine the reading: **二日（ふつか）** is a date or duration, **二人（ふたり）** counts people, and **二つ（ふたつ）** counts general objects.

## 4. Read a club notice

> <span lang="ja">文化クラブのお知らせ</span> (ぶんかクラブの おしらせ)
>
> <span lang="ja">イベント：四月八日、午後三時</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">スタッフ：学生が二人います。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">チケット：五枚あります。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">りんご：三つあります。</span>

The fictional notice says April 8 at 3 p.m.; two student staff members; five tickets; and three apples. Find the Japanese evidence for each fact. **五枚（ごまい）** counts the tickets; **三つ（みっつ）** counts the apples.

## 5. Common traps and guided retrieval

**四月** is しがつ, not よんがつ. **一人** is ひとり, not いちにん. A ticket is not counted with **人**: use **枚**. **八日** is ようか, while **八つ** is やっつ.

Cover the table and retrieve these readings: <span lang="ja">三日、四日、八日、十日</span>. Check: **みっか、よっか、ようか、とおか**. Match two people → **二人**, two tickets → **二枚**, two apples → **二つ**.

## 6. Independent writing transfer

Write a three-line notice for a fictional gathering: one date, a number of attendees and a number of paper tickets.

Model: <span lang="ja">イベントは五月七日です。学生が三人います。チケットが四枚あります。</span> — The event is May 7; three students are present; there are four tickets. Read **五月七日** as **ごがつ なのか** and **四枚** as **よんまい**. Check your date reading and counter choice. This is ungraded writing; different natural notices are possible. You can optionally say your sentences aloud, but pronunciation is not evaluated.
