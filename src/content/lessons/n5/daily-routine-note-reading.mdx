## Mika's ordinary school day

**Can-do:** Describe a usual day using polite present verbs. Mika is a fictional learner. This is an original **written** note, not a recording.

### 1. Before reading

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 毎朝 | まいあさ | every morning |
| 朝ごはん | あさごはん | breakfast |
| 学校 | がっこう | school |
| 昼 | ひる | noon / daytime |
| 午後 | ごご | p.m. |
| 夜 | よる | evening / night |
| 家 | いえ | home |

**Action words:** 起きます（おきます） get up; 食べます（たべます） eat; 飲みます（のみます） drink; 行きます（いきます） go; 勉強します（べんきょうします） study; 話します（はなします） talk; 帰ります（かえります） return; 見ません（みません） do not watch; 読みます（よみます） read; 寝ます（ねます） go to bed.

### 2. Original reading: みかの一日

Read the Japanese first. A–G are evidence labels, not part of Mika's note.

| Line | みかのメモ |
| --- | --- |
| **A** | 毎朝七時に起きます。 |
| **B** | 七時半に朝ごはんを食べます。水を飲みます。 |
| **C** | 八時に学校へ行きます。バスで行きます。 |
| **D** | 学校で日本語を勉強します。昼に友だちと話します。 |
| **E** | 午後四時に家へ帰ります。 |
| **F** | 夜はテレビを見ません。本を読みます。 |
| **G** | 十一時に寝ます。 |

**English support:** A: I get up at seven every morning. B: I eat breakfast at 7:30 and drink water. C: I go to school at eight by bus. D: I study Japanese at school and talk with friends at noon. E: I return home at four p.m. F: I do not watch television at night; I read a book. G: I go to bed at eleven.

This is a usual day, not a dated appointment. The text never names Mika's school or bus route.

### 3. Read the particles and endings

**Time に:** 「七時に起きます。」(しちじに おきます) means “I get up at seven.” 「午後四時に家へ帰ります。」(ごごよじに いえへ かえります) means “I return home at four p.m.” **に** marks the clock time, while **へ** marks the destination.

**Object を:** 「水を飲みます。」(みずを のみます) means “I drink water.” 「本を読みます。」(ほんを よみます) means “I read a book.” **を** marks the object and is usually pronounced *o* as a particle.

**Negative ません:** 「夜はテレビを見ません。」(よるは テレビを みません) means “At night, I do not watch television.” **見ません** is negative, unlike **見ます**. The next sentence says what Mika does instead: 「本を読みます。」 (I read a book).

**Place / means で:** 「学校で日本語を勉強します。」 (I study Japanese at school) uses **で** for the location of an action. 「バスで行きます。」 (I go by bus) uses **で** for the means of travel.

### 4. Worked examples

**When does Mika eat breakfast?** In B, 「七時半に朝ごはんを食べます。」 gives **7:30**. **半** means half past.

**Does Mika watch TV at night?** In F, 「テレビを見ません。」 gives **no**. Missing **ません** reverses the meaning.

**What is the school called?** C and D mention **学校** but give no school name. The answer is **not stated**, not a guess.

### 5. Guided retrieval

Cover the English support and find the Japanese evidence.

1. How does Mika travel? **C — バスで行きます。** (by bus)
2. Who does Mika talk with? **D — 友だちと話します。** (with friends)
3. When does Mika return home? **E — 午後四時に家へ帰ります。** (4 p.m.)
4. What happens at eleven? **G — 十一時に寝ます。** (goes to bed)

### 6. Independent reading check

Answer the ten questions below using the Japanese note. Find an A–G phrase before choosing. A missing detail must not be invented. Scores are formative browser-local practice, not official JLPT scores.

### 7. Write and optionally speak

Write three connected sentences about your own day, or a fictional day. For example: 「わたしは七時に起きます。」 (I get up at seven.) 「朝ごはんを食べます。」 (I eat breakfast.) 「夜は本を読みます。」 (At night I read a book.)

**Self-review:** (1) Does the time match the verb? (2) Does **を** mark the object? (3) Does **ません** really mean an action that does not happen? (4) Are the events in a sensible order? Reading aloud is optional; no recording or pronunciation grade is provided.

### 8. Takeaway

Find the time, the action and the verb ending. Distinguish **見ます** from **見ません** and cite the exact Japanese phrase supporting each answer. Lesson 06 will practise comprehension of an original recorded conversation once its licensed audio is verified.
