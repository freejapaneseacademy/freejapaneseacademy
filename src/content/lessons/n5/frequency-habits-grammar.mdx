## Mission: tell a classmate about your habits

Your classmate asks **週末は何をしますか。** (What do you do on weekends?). You want to say not just *what*, but *how often*. Frequency words usually come before the verb phrase. This is a **written** grammar lesson; actual listening comprehension belongs to this chapter's L06.

### 1. Frequency expressions in a familiar routine

| Expression | Reading | Typical meaning | Original example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| いつも | いつも | always / regularly | **いつも朝ごはんを食べます。** I always eat breakfast. |
| よく | よく | often | **よく図書館で勉強します。** I often study at the library. |
| ときどき | ときどき | sometimes | **ときどき公園を散歩します。** I sometimes walk in the park. |
| あまり | あまり | not very often / not much (with a negative) | **あまりテレビを見ません。** I don't watch much TV. |
| ぜんぜん | ぜんぜん | not at all (with a negative) | **ぜんぜんコーヒーを飲みません。** I don't drink coffee at all. |

These are **qualitative** frequencies, not exact percentages. よく and ときどき can vary with context. いつも can be a generalization about a routine, not a mathematical guarantee of every occasion.

### 2. Positive and negative patterns

**Topic + frequency word + object/place + polite verb.**

- **私はよく本を読みます。** (わたしは よく ほんを よみます。) — I often read books.
- **弟はときどき学校でサッカーをします。** (おとうとは ときどき がっこうで サッカーを します。) — My younger brother sometimes plays soccer at school.
- **私はあまり映画を見ません。** (わたしは あまり えいがを みません。) — I don't watch movies very often.
- **私はぜんぜんお酒を飲みません。** (わたしは ぜんぜん おさけを のみません。) — I don't drink alcohol at all.

For the **beginner pattern taught here**, use **あまり + negative** and **ぜんぜん + negative**. A sentence such as **あまり本を読みます** is not the normal way to say “I don't read much.” Say **あまり本を読みません**. Colloquial Japanese sometimes uses ぜんぜん with positive evaluations (for example, ぜんぜん大丈夫), but that is a separate usage; do not apply it to this beginner frequency pattern.

**ません** expresses polite nonpast negative. With 毎日 (every day), it describes a present habit, not necessarily a future refusal. **昨日はぜんぜん勉強しませんでした** uses past negative **ませんでした** to report yesterday.

### 3. Particles and word order

- **私はよく学校で日本語を勉強します。** — I often study Japanese **at school**. で marks the activity location; を marks the object.
- **母はときどき七時に起きます。** — My mother sometimes gets up **at seven**. に marks the clock time.
- **日曜日はあまり外に行きません。** — On Sundays I don't go out very often. に marks a destination/direction here.

The frequency word can also appear after a topic: **日曜日は、よく本を読みます。** Keep the particle with the noun it marks. Do not put を after よく; よく is not a direct object.

### 4. Original reading: Sora's habit journal

This fictional journal describes ordinary recurring habits, not a real person's data.

> **そらの習慣（しゅうかん）メモ**
>
> 私はいつも七時に起きます。朝はよくパンを食べます。学校ではときどき友だちと日本語を勉強します。夜はあまりテレビを見ません。コーヒーはぜんぜん飲みません。日曜日はよく公園を散歩します。

**Meaning:** Sora always gets up at seven, often eats bread in the morning, sometimes studies Japanese with friends at school, rarely watches TV at night, never drinks coffee, and often walks in the park on Sundays.

**Evidence practice:** To answer “What does Sora never drink?”, cite **コーヒーはぜんぜん飲みません**. To answer “Where does Sora sometimes study Japanese?”, cite **学校ではときどき友だちと日本語を勉強します**.

### 5. Written mini-dialogue

**A:** 週末はよく何をしますか。 — What do you often do on weekends?

**B:** よく公園を散歩します。 — I often walk in the park.

**A:** テレビもよく見ますか。 — Do you often watch TV too?

**B:** いいえ、あまり見ません。 — No, not very often.

**A:** コーヒーを飲みますか。 — Do you drink coffee?

**B:** いいえ、ぜんぜん飲みません。 — No, not at all.

**あまり見ません** is less absolute than **ぜんぜん見ません**. Do not treat the two as synonyms in all contexts.

### 6. Repair common mistakes

- For “I rarely watch TV,” **あまりテレビを見ます** is mismatched. Use **あまりテレビを見ません**.
- For “I never drink coffee,” **ぜんぜんコーヒーを飲みます** is not the beginner negative-frequency pattern. Use **ぜんぜんコーヒーを飲みません**.
- **よくを勉強します** incorrectly gives an adverb the object particle. Use **よく勉強します**.
- **七時で起きます** incorrectly marks clock time with で. Use **七時に起きます**.

### 7. Practice ladder

**Controlled:** Change **テレビを見ます** to “I don't watch much TV”: **あまりテレビを見ません**. Change **コーヒーを飲みます** to “I don't drink coffee at all”: **ぜんぜんコーヒーを飲みません**.

**Guided:** Read Sora's journal. Complete: **そらは日曜日に＿＿公園を散歩します。** Answer: **よく**. Then answer **そらは夜、テレビをよく見ますか。** with **いいえ、あまり見ません** and cite the original journal.

**Independent writing:** Write four short connected sentences about a *fictional* student's habits. Include よく, ときどき, あまり and ぜんぜん; use negative verbs with the last two. Model: **私はよく本を読みます。ときどき公園を散歩します。あまりテレビを見ません。コーヒーはぜんぜん飲みません。** Self-review: Are the activities plausible? Are particles attached to nouns? Are both negative-frequency verbs negative? Your free writing is **not automatically graded**.

**Optional speaking:** Read your four sentences aloud, then ask **週末はよく何をしますか。** Speaking is optional and no recording or pronunciation score is claimed.
