Japanese uses three writing systems: **hiragana**, **katakana**, and **kanji**. Hiragana is a great place to begin because it represents basic Japanese sounds.

## Your first five hiragana

| Character | Sound | Simple pronunciation hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **あ** | a | like *a* in “father” |
| **い** | i | like *ee* in “see” |
| **う** | u | close to *oo*, with relaxed lips |
| **え** | e | like *e* in “met” |
| **お** | o | like *o* in “more”, without a long glide |

Try saying them aloud, slowly: **あ・い・う・え・お**.

## Furigana in action

Furigana are small reading hints shown above kanji. For example:

<p class="jp-line" lang="ja"><ruby>日本語<rt>にほんご</rt></ruby>を<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>します。</p>

*I study Japanese.*

Furigana lets beginners understand written Japanese while gradually learning kanji. Later courses will let you hide readings as your confidence increases.
