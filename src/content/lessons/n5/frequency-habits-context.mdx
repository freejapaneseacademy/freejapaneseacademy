## At the library after class

**Chapter Can-do:** Explain how often you do familiar activities. This first lesson is about *understanding a conversation*, not memorizing every frequency expression or producing a long survey.

Two fictional classmates, **みお (Mio)** and **たく (Taku)**, meet at their school library after class. Mio is preparing a small class survey about habits. Read the dialogue once for the situation, then again for the specific actions and frequency words.

### Original conversation

**みお：** たくさん、こんにちは。クラスのアンケートです。いつも本を読みますか。  
**たく：** いいえ、いつもは読みません。でも、よく図書館で勉強します。  
**みお：** そうですか。テレビは見ますか。  
**たく：** ときどき見ます。動画はあまり見ません。みおさんは？  
**みお：** わたしはよく本を読みます。でも、テレビはぜんぜん見ません。  
**たく：** ありがとうございます。

**English meaning:** Mio greets Taku and explains that she is conducting a class survey. She asks whether he always reads books. Taku says he does not always read, but he often studies at the library. He sometimes watches television, but does not watch videos very much. Mio says she often reads books and never watches television. Taku thanks her.

**Reading support:** 図書館（としょかん） library; 勉強（べんきょう） study; 本（ほん） book; 読みます（よみます） read; 見ます（みます） watch; 動画（どうが） video; アンケート survey.

### What can we prove from the text?

| Question | Japanese evidence | Supported conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why are they talking? | クラスのアンケートです | Mio is asking questions for a class survey |
| Does Taku always read? | いつもは読みません | No, he does not *always* read |
| Where does Taku often study? | よく図書館で勉強します | At the library |
| How often does Taku watch TV? | ときどき見ます | Sometimes |
| How much does Taku watch videos? | 動画はあまり見ません | Not very often |
| Does Mio watch TV? | テレビはぜんぜん見ません | She does not watch TV at all |

**Evidence boundary:** The dialogue does not tell us Taku's exact weekly study count or Mio's favorite book. “Often” is not the same as a fixed number of days.

## Five frequency clues

| Expression | Reading | Typical meaning | Model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| いつも | いつも | always / usually | いつも本を読みます。 |
| よく | よく | often | よく勉強します。 |
| ときどき | ときどき | sometimes | ときどきテレビを見ます。 |
| あまり | あまり | not very much / not often, with a negative | あまり見ません。 |
| ぜんぜん | ぜんぜん | not at all, with a negative in this beginner pattern | ぜんぜん見ません。 |

**Notice the negative forms:** In this lesson, **あまり** and **ぜんぜん** pair with **ません**, not affirmative **ます**. Compare **よく読みます** (“often read”) and **あまり読みません** (“do not read much”). **いつもは読みません** means “do not always read,” not “never read.” The contrastive **は** makes this distinction important.

**Particles in context:** **本を読みます** marks the book as the object of reading; **図書館で勉強します** uses **で** for the location of the studying action. The topic marker **は** in **テレビは見ますか** and **動画はあまり見ません** sets up the item being discussed.

**A common mistake:** Treating **ぜんぜん見ません** as “sometimes watches” reverses the meaning. Treating **あまり見ません** as “never watches” overstates it: it says “not much,” not necessarily zero.

## Guided comprehension

**Step 1 — Who and why?** Identify the questioner, the respondent and the purpose of the exchange. Underline the words **クラスのアンケート**.

**Step 2 — Find exact evidence.** Copy the short Japanese phrase showing that Taku sometimes watches television. Then copy the phrase showing that Mio does not watch television at all. Compare the frequency words and verb endings.

**Step 3 — Controlled substitution.** Keep the verb and change the frequency: **よく本を読みます。 → ときどき本を読みます。** (“I often read books” → “I sometimes read books”). For a negative pattern, try **動画はあまり見ません。 → 動画はぜんぜん見ません。** The second statement is stronger; use it only if it is true.

**Step 4 — Independent transfer.** Write two sentences about an *imagined* student's habits: one with **よく** or **ときどき** and one with **あまり〜ません** or **ぜんぜん〜ません**. Check that the negative expressions have negative verbs. This writing is not automatically graded.

## Reading, listening preparation and optional speaking

Read once without translating every word. On the second pass, circle each frequency expression and trace it to its speaker and action. For optional listening preparation, have a partner read the dialogue slowly while you hide the text, then reveal the written lines and check what you heard. **This L01 contains no recorded audio and does not award a listening score.** You may rehearse the two roles aloud; no microphone or pronunciation assessment is required.
