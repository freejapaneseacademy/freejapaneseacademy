## Mission: prepare a weekend wish list

A fictional student, 美香（みか, Mika）, is deciding how to spend the weekend and what to buy. Your task is to tell **a desired action**, **a desired object** and **the purpose of going somewhere** apart. These expressions do not prove that a wish has come true.

### 1. Recall three distinct meanings

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning | What it tells us |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 本を読みたいです。 | ほんをよみたいです | I want to read a book. | Desired action |
| 新しいかばんがほしいです。 | あたらしいかばんがほしいです | I want a new bag. | Desired object |
| 店にかばんを買いに行きます。 | みせにかばんをかいにいきます | I'm going to a shop to buy a bag. | Purpose of a trip |
| 本を読みたくないです。 | ほんをよみたくないです | I don't want to read a book. | Negative desire |
| かばんはほしくないです。 | かばんはほしくないです | I don't want a bag. | Negative object desire |

**Form check.** 読みます → 読みたいです: remove **ます** and add **たいです**. 買います → 買いに行きます: use the same verb stem, followed by **に行きます**. The particle **を** marks the object of 読む or 買う; **が** commonly marks the desired thing with ほしい. For a destination, **に** or **へ** can mark direction, as in 店に行きます.

**Common mistake:** *読みますたい* incorrectly keeps ます. *かばんをほしいです* is not the introductory pattern taught here; use **かばんがほしいです**. **ほしい** and **たい** behave like い-adjectives when negated: **ほしくない**, **読みたくない**. A plan to shop is not evidence of a completed purchase.

### 2. Read the original wish list

**美香の週末のメモ（みかのしゅうまつのメモ） — Mika's weekend note**

> 土曜日に図書館で日本語の本を読みたいです。
>
> 日曜日に新しいかばんを見たいです。
>
> 新しいかばんがほしいです。
>
> 駅の前の店にかばんを買いに行きます。

**English:** “On Saturday, I want to read a Japanese-language book at the library. On Sunday, I want to look at new bags. I want a new bag. I'm going to the shop in front of the station to buy a bag.”

**Useful readings:** 土曜日（どようび, Saturday）; 日曜日（にちようび, Sunday）; 図書館（としょかん, library）; 日本語（にほんご, Japanese language）; 駅（えき, station）; 前（まえ, front）; 店（みせ, shop）.

**Evidence challenge:** Which line states *where* Mika will read? The first line has **図書館で**, with **で** indicating where the reading activity takes place. Which line states *why* she is going to the shop? The fourth line contains **かばんを買いに行きます**. Does the note say she already bought the bag? No: it states a desire and an intended trip.

### 4. Guided writing and optional speaking

Write **four connected sentences** for an imaginary weekend: (1) something you want to do, (2) something you want, (3) a place you will go and the purpose, (4) something you do not want. You may use a fictional identity.

**Model:** 土曜日に映画を見たいです。新しい本がほしいです。日曜日に本屋へ本を買いに行きます。でも、日曜日に勉強したくないです。

*Doyōbi ni eiga o mitai desu. Atarashii hon ga hoshii desu. Nichiyōbi ni hon'ya e hon o kai ni ikimasu. Demo, nichiyōbi ni benkyō shitakunai desu.*

“On Saturday I want to see a film. I want a new book. On Sunday I will go to a bookstore to buy a book. But I don't want to study on Sunday.”

**Self-check rubric (ungraded):** Is the desired action expressed with a correct verb stem + たい? Is the desired object expressed with ほしい? Does the trip state a destination and a verb-stem purpose? Are negative forms and particles correct? Do the four statements form a coherent plan? Fix one issue and rewrite your sentences. Optionally read them aloud, without recording or automatic pronunciation scoring.

### 5. Check your understanding

Answer ten written questions about forms and the wish list. When authentic audio is playable, four additional questions test details heard in the two dialogues. Review the Japanese evidence after submitting, then retry missed items. Scores are local formative practice only, not official JLPT results.
