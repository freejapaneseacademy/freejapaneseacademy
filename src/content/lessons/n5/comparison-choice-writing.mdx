## Your writing mission

You are helping a fictional neighborhood center select **one** Saturday activity. Write **two to five short connected Japanese sentences** that compare activities and make a choice. You may choose any activity; there is no single correct personal preference. Use polite **です** style consistently. The figures and names below are invented for learning.

| Activity | Price | Start | Room |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 絵（え）のクラス — art | 1,000円（せんえん） | 9時（くじ） | 小（ちい）さい |
| 料理（りょうり）のクラス — cooking | 2,000円（にせんえん） | 10時（じゅうじ） | 大（おお）きい |
| 音楽（おんがく）のクラス — music | 1,500円（せんごひゃくえん） | 11時（じゅういちじ） | 大（おお）きい |

The **art class is cheapest**, cooking is most expensive, art starts earliest, and cooking and music have equally large rooms. Don't invent a size difference between those last two.

## Observe a model: two choices

> 料理（りょうり）のクラスより、絵（え）のクラスのほうが安（やす）いです。  
> 絵のクラスは九時（くじ）からです。料理のクラスは十時（じゅうじ）からです。  
> わたしは絵のクラスを選（えら）びます。

*Art is cheaper than cooking. Art starts at nine; cooking starts at ten. I choose art.*

**Notice:** In **A より B のほうが 安いです**, A is the comparison reference, and B is the cheaper option. **より** does not mean that A wins. **選びます（えらびます）** means “choose” and is provided as supported vocabulary.

## Observe a second model: three choices

> 三（みっ）つのクラスの中（なか）で、絵のクラスが一番（いちばん）安いです。  
> でも、音楽（おんがく）のクラスのほうが好（す）きです。  
> わたしは音楽のクラスを選びます。

*Of the three classes, art is the least expensive. But I prefer music. I choose music.*

Here **一番** has an explicit comparison set: **三つのクラスの中で** (“among the three classes”). The second sentence gives a personal preference, not a claim that music is cheaper. **でも** (“but”) links the contrast.

## Recombine the building blocks

| Purpose | Japanese frame | What to replace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask between two | AとBと、どちらが安いですか。 | A, B, and adjective |
| Compare two | Aより、Bのほうが安いです。 | reference A, described B |
| Choose among three | 三つのクラスの中で、Bが一番安いです。 | B and adjective |
| State your choice | わたしはBを選びます。 | B |
| Link a preference | でも、Bのほうが好きです。 | B |

**Accuracy check:** With the supplied prices, **料理のクラスより、絵のクラスのほうが安いです** is true; reversing the class names while keeping **安い** would be false. With room size, **絵のクラスより、料理のクラスのほうが大きい部屋です** is awkward because the compared nouns change; prefer **絵のクラスの部屋より、料理のクラスの部屋のほうが大きいです** (“the cooking classroom is bigger than the art classroom”).

**Time comparison:** **絵のクラスは料理のクラスより早く始まります** (“art starts earlier than cooking”) is a useful extension, but you can instead write the two start times as separate simple sentences. Do not confuse **早い** (early) with **安い** (inexpensive).

## Guided writing ladder

1. **Copy and notice:** Type **料理のクラスより、絵のクラスのほうが安いです。** Identify which class is the reference and which is cheaper.
2. **Substitute:** Compare music and cooking on price: **料理のクラスより、音楽のクラスのほうが安いです。** Check 1,500円 against 2,000円.
3. **Expand:** Add **三つのクラスの中で、絵のクラスが一番安いです。** Now the set contains three activities.
4. **Connect:** Add **でも** if your personal choice differs from the cheapest option, then write **わたしは〜を選びます。**

### Common corrections

- **× 料理のクラスより、絵のクラスのほうが高いです。** The art class costs less, not more. Use **安いです**.
- **× 三つのクラスの中で、料理のクラスが一番安いです。** Cooking is 2,000円; art at 1,000円 is cheapest.
- **× 料理のクラスより、絵のクラスが安いです。** This can be understood, but use **のほうが** in this lesson to make the contrast explicit.
- **× どちらが一番安いですか。** For exactly two options, ask **どちらが安いですか**. For all three, specify the group and use **一番**.
- **× 絵のクラスを好きです。** Use **絵のクラスが好きです** for liking, but **絵のクラスを選びます** for choosing.

## Independent writing task

Imagine a fictional friend asks you to recommend one class. Write **2–5 connected sentences** in the editor below. Include (a) a factually correct two-option comparison using **より／のほうが**, (b) a sentence using **一番** with the three-class set or a second relevant comparison, and (c) **わたしは〜を選びます**. Optional: use **でも** to contrast your choice with the cheapest activity.

**Alternative model (cooking choice):**

> 絵（え）のクラスより、料理（りょうり）のクラスのほうが高（たか）いです。三（みっ）つのクラスの中（なか）で、絵のクラスが一番（いちばん）安（やす）いです。でも、わたしは料理のクラスが好（す）きです。わたしは料理のクラスを選（えら）びます。

*Cooking is more expensive than art. Art is the cheapest of the three. But I like cooking. I choose cooking.*

Use the editor to compare your draft with this model and check the five criteria. **Your creative writing is not machine-graded**; the separate eight-question written recognition check is formative only. You can use a Japanese IME or paste Japanese text. No microphone, account, or personal information is required.

## Review and transfer

Try a new scenario using two fictional cafés: A costs 500円 and B costs 700円. Write **Bより、Aのほうが安いです** and then say which café you choose. A lower price is a fact; your preference can differ. Read your sentences silently or aloud if comfortable; pronunciation is not evaluated.

## Written recognition check

Answer eight questions about comparisons, factual evidence, and the choice patterns. After submitting, inspect each explanation and retry.
