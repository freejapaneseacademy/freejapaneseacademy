## Mission: Help a visitor find the post office

A visitor has left the station and wants to reach the post office. Read the **fictional written route** carefully. You will use the directions themselves—not a guess from a real map—to answer questions about turns, landmarks and location. This is reading practice; the street-instruction audio belongs to L06.

### 1. Read the route words first

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning and job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">駅</span> | えき | station; the starting point |
| <span lang="ja">まっすぐ</span> | まっすぐ | straight ahead; direction of movement |
| <span lang="ja">右</span> | みぎ | right |
| <span lang="ja">左</span> | ひだり | left |
| <span lang="ja">交差点</span> | こうさてん | intersection |
| <span lang="ja">信号</span> | しんごう | traffic signal |
| <span lang="ja">銀行</span> | ぎんこう | bank |
| <span lang="ja">郵便局</span> | ゆうびんきょく | post office |
| <span lang="ja">隣</span> | となり | next to; a neighboring place |
| <span lang="ja">前</span> | まえ | in front of |

A direction can contain an action: <span lang="ja">右に曲がってください。</span> (みぎに まがってください。 “Please turn right.”). A location sentence instead describes where something **is**: <span lang="ja">郵便局は銀行の隣にあります。</span> (ゆうびんきょくは ぎんこうの となりに あります。 “The post office is next to the bank.”) Here **に** marks the location, while **あります** describes the existence of a nonliving place or thing. It does not mean “go.”

### 2. Original reading: A route on the town noticeboard

The following map-like text describes a **made-up** neighborhood. Each step is in order. The signal is at the first intersection; there is no second turn after the bank.

> <span lang="ja">駅（えき）の前（まえ）から、まっすぐ行（い）ってください。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">一（ひと）つ目（め）の交差点（こうさてん）に信号（しんごう）があります。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">その信号で右（みぎ）に曲（ま）がってください。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">少（すこ）し歩（ある）くと、左側（ひだりがわ）に銀行（ぎんこう）があります。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">郵便局（ゆうびんきょく）は銀行の隣（となり）にあります。</span>
>
> <span lang="ja">郵便局の前に小（ちい）さい公園（こうえん）があります。</span>

**English meaning, line by line:** From in front of the station, please go straight. There is a traffic signal at the first intersection. Turn right at that signal. After walking a little, there is a bank on the left side. The post office is next to the bank. There is a small park in front of the post office.

### 3. Build a mental map from the words

1. **Start** at <span lang="ja">駅の前</span> (in front of the station).
2. **Continue straight** to <span lang="ja">一つ目の交差点</span> (the first intersection).
3. **Turn right** at <span lang="ja">その信号</span> (that signal).
4. **Look left** for <span lang="ja">銀行</span> (the bank). The text does **not** instruct you to turn left here.
5. **Locate the destination** next to the bank: <span lang="ja">郵便局</span>. A park is in front of it.

**Reference words:** <span lang="ja">その信号</span> points back to the signal just introduced at the first intersection. <span lang="ja">銀行の隣</span> means “next to the bank”; the bank is the reference landmark. Neither phrase names an additional intersection.

### 4. Common reading traps

- **Left-side landmark ≠ left turn.** <span lang="ja">左側に銀行があります</span> tells you where the bank is. The only turn is <span lang="ja">右に曲がってください</span>.
- **あります ≠ movement.** <span lang="ja">公園があります</span> says a park exists there; it does not instruct you to walk to the park.
- **First intersection matters.** <span lang="ja">一つ目の交差点</span> identifies the first one, not any later crossing.
- **隣 and 前 are different.** The post office is **next to** the bank, while the park is **in front of** the post office.

### 5. Guided reading: Find the evidence

Before using the quiz, cover the English translation and point to the Japanese phrase answering each question: Where do you begin? Which way do you turn? On which side is the bank? What is next to the bank? What is in front of the post office? Re-read only the relevant sentence if you are unsure. The scored questions below ask for the same kind of textual evidence, including distinctions between action and location.

### 6. Independent writing and optional speaking

Imagine a second visitor asking about this same fictional neighborhood. Write **two or three short Japanese sentences**: give the starting point, the right turn, and the post office's location. Model:

<span lang="ja">駅の前からまっすぐ行ってください。信号で右に曲がってください。郵便局は銀行の隣にあります。</span>

*From in front of the station, go straight. Turn right at the signal. The post office is next to the bank.*

Self-review your writing: (1) clear starting point, (2) correct movement instruction, (3) accurate landmark relation, (4) appropriate に and あります, (5) two or three connected sentences. Your draft is not machine-graded. Optional speaking: read your directions aloud slowly, pointing to the corresponding sentence as you speak. No recording or pronunciation score is required.

### 7. Check your reading

Answer all eight evidence-based questions. After submission, compare your answer with the quoted Japanese evidence and read the feedback. You can retry; scored attempts are saved locally when browser storage is available. Independent Japanese-language and assessment reviews are still pending.
