## Explain how often you do familiar activities

**Optional speaking lesson.** Imagine two classmates comparing their everyday routines. Both speakers are fictional. You may read aloud privately, whisper, or simply rehearse mentally. This page does **not** record you or evaluate pronunciation. No audio model is provided here; earlier listening work is separate.

### 1. Listen with your eyes: the question pattern

<span lang="ja">毎朝、何をしますか。</span> (まいあさ、なにをしますか。) — *What do you do every morning?*

<span lang="ja">いつも日本語を勉強します。</span> (いつも にほんごを べんきょうします。) — *I always study Japanese.*

A question with **何をしますか** asks *what action* someone does. A yes/no question like **テレビを見ますか** asks whether someone does a specific action. Answer with a frequency word plus a polite verb.

### 2. Original conversation cards

| Speaker A asks | Speaker B replies | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">毎朝、何をしますか。</span> | <span lang="ja">いつも日本語を勉強します。</span> | What do you do every morning? — I always study Japanese. |
| <span lang="ja">週末は、よく何をしますか。</span> | <span lang="ja">よく公園を散歩します。</span> | What do you often do on weekends? — I often take walks in the park. |
| <span lang="ja">テレビを見ますか。</span> | <span lang="ja">ときどき見ます。</span> | Do you watch television? — I sometimes watch it. |
| <span lang="ja">コーヒーを飲みますか。</span> | <span lang="ja">あまり飲みません。</span> | Do you drink coffee? — I don't drink it very much. |
| <span lang="ja">夜、ゲームをしますか。</span> | <span lang="ja">いいえ、ぜんぜんしません。</span> | Do you play games at night? — No, I don't play at all. |
| <span lang="ja">日曜日は、友だちと話しますか。</span> | <span lang="ja">はい、よく話します。</span> | Do you talk with friends on Sundays? — Yes, I often do. |

Read each line slowly, pause at punctuation, then exchange roles. These are written rehearsal models, **not verified pronunciation recordings**.

### 3. Frequency ladder and the negative trap

| Word | Reading | Approximate sense | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| いつも | いつも | always | <span lang="ja">いつも勉強します。</span> — I always study. |
| よく | よく | often | <span lang="ja">よく散歩します。</span> — I often walk. |
| ときどき | ときどき | sometimes | <span lang="ja">ときどき読みます。</span> — I sometimes read. |
| あまり | あまり | not much / not often | <span lang="ja">あまり見ません。</span> — I don't watch much. |
| ぜんぜん | ぜんぜん | not at all | <span lang="ja">ぜんぜん飲みません。</span> — I don't drink it at all. |

In these beginner examples, **あまり** and **ぜんぜん** go with **ません**. **あまり飲みます** does not mean “I don't drink much.” Informal Japanese sometimes uses ぜんぜん with affirmative expressions, but that is outside this beginner pattern. Frequency words do not specify exact counts per week.

### 4. Particle clues for natural replies

**日本語を勉強します** uses **を** for what is studied. **友だちと話します** uses **と** for the companion. **図書館で勉強します** uses **で** for the location of the action. **毎朝** usually needs no **に**, unlike an exact time such as **七時に**.

### 5. Guided substitutions

Try replacing one part at a time. **A:** <span lang="ja">毎朝、何をしますか。</span> **B:** <span lang="ja">いつもパンを食べます。</span> Replace **パンを食べます** with **日本語を勉強します**. Then replace **いつも** with **ときどき**. Finally, practise a negative: **A:** <span lang="ja">コーヒーを飲みますか。</span> **B:** <span lang="ja">あまり飲みません。</span>

### 6. Your own short roleplay

Invent a fictional student. In role A, ask about mornings, weekends and one specific activity. In role B, reply with **three different frequency words**, including one negative example. Swap roles and repeat without looking at the models. If you practise with another person, ask their permission before recording; this site never requests microphone access.

**Self-review (ungraded):** (1) Did you ask a clear question? (2) Did the answer describe the intended frequency? (3) Did **あまり／ぜんぜん** take a negative verb? (4) Could a listener follow the activities and any places? Your accent and fluency are **not** scored here.
