## Plan a day out in Japanese

You have arranged to visit a museum with a friend. You need to tell them **when to meet**, **where to meet**, **where you are going**, and **how you will get there**. Japanese particles help keep those details separate. By the end of this lesson, you can build a short plan from those pieces.

Read this original fictional plan aloud if you like. Reading and writing, not pronunciation, are assessed here.

> 土曜日（どようび）に友達（ともだち）と博物館（はくぶつかん）へ行きます。  
> 午前（ごぜん）九時（くじ）に駅（えき）で会（あ）います。  
> 駅から電車（でんしゃ）で行きます。  
> 切符（きっぷ）は片道（かたみち）二百円（にひゃくえん）です。

**Meaning:** On Saturday, I am going to the museum with a friend. We will meet at the station at 9 a.m. We will go by train from the station. The ticket is 200 yen one way. The price is invented for this practice, not an actual fare.

## 1. Attach each detail to the right particle

| Function | Japanese | Reading / meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specific clock time | 午前九時**に**会います | ごぜんくじにあいます — meet at 9 a.m. |
| Action location | 駅**で**会います | えきであいます — meet at the station |
| Destination | 博物館**へ**行きます | はくぶつかんへいきます — go to the museum |
| Starting point | 駅**から**行きます | えきからいきます — go from the station |
| Transport means | 電車**で**行きます | でんしゃでいきます — go by train |
| Companion | 友達**と**行きます | ともだちといきます — go with a friend |

The destination particle **へ** is written *へ* but pronounced **え**. **に** can also mark a destination (博物館に行きます), but here we practise **へ** for direction and **に** for a specific time. They are not interchangeable in every sentence.

**Notice the two で uses.** 駅で会います describes **where an action occurs**; 電車で行きます describes **the means of travelling**. You identify the role from the noun and verb, not from the particle alone.

## 2. Build a complete plan in small steps

Start with a destination:

> 公園（こうえん）へ行きます。 — I am going to the park.

Add a companion:

> 日曜日（にちようび）に妹（いもうと）と公園へ行きます。 — On Sunday I am going to the park with my younger sister.

For a **specific meeting time**, add **に**:

> 午前十時（ごぜんじゅうじ）に駅で会います。 — We will meet at the station at 10 a.m.

Add the transport and starting point:

> 駅からバスで行きます。 — We will go by bus from the station.

These are three short sentences with distinct functions. A learner can omit an already understood subject in Japanese. The time expression **日曜日に** is natural, but calendar days may also appear without に in some contexts; the fixed-clock-time pattern **九時に** is the main target here.

## 3. Order and contrast

A useful full sentence is:

> 午前九時に駅で友達と会います。 — I meet my friend at the station at 9 a.m.

Japanese often puts time and place before the verb. **友達と** identifies the person you meet; **駅で** identifies the location of the meeting. Other orders are possible if the relationships remain clear.

Compare these sentences:

| Japanese | What it actually says |
| --- | --- |
| 駅で会います。 | We meet at the station. |
| 駅へ行きます。 | We go to the station. |
| 電車で行きます。 | We go by train. |
| 駅から行きます。 | We leave/go from the station. |
| 九時に会います。 | We meet at nine o'clock. |

**Common error:** 「駅に会います」 does not normally express “meet at the station.” For a meeting *taking place at* the station, write **駅で会います**. **駅に行きます** is grammatical, but it says “go to the station,” not “meet there.”

**Another error:** 「電車へ行きます」 means going *toward/to the train*, not travelling *by train*. Use **電車で行きます** for the transport method.

## 4. Guided reconstruction

Here is a new fictional plan. All details are for practice, not real timetable or fare information.

| Detail | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Day | 日曜日（にちようび） — Sunday |
| Meeting time | 午前十時（ごぜんじゅうじ） — 10 a.m. |
| Meeting point | 学校（がっこう） — school |
| Destination | 公園（こうえん） — park |
| Means | バス — bus |
| Companion | 友達（ともだち） — friend |

Before revealing the model, write three Japanese sentences covering all six details. Check your particles yourself.

**Model:**

> 日曜日に友達と公園へ行きます。  
> 午前十時に学校で会います。  
> 学校からバスで行きます。

**Meaning:** On Sunday I will go to the park with a friend. We will meet at school at 10 a.m. We will go by bus from school.

The model uses **学校から** as the starting point. That is a reasonable extension of the meeting point in this fictional plan, not a claim that every real trip starts exactly where people meet.

## 5. Independent transfer and review

Write your own two to four sentence plan with a destination, a specific meeting time, a meeting location, and a means of transport. Choose from **図書館（としょかん）**, **公園**, **駅**, **バス**, **電車**, or another familiar word. Check that your time uses **に**, meeting location **で**, destination **へ**, and transport **で**. If you include a starting point, use **から**.

Try a second pass without looking at the table. The twelve recognition questions below check how particles function in sentences; they do **not** automatically grade your independent writing or speech. A listening-focused itinerary lesson comes later in this chapter.
