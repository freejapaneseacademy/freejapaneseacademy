## Tell someone how your morning goes

**Can-do:** Write a short, understandable account of actions in order. Imagine that you are sending a note to a classmate about a **fictional** Saturday morning. No real personal information is needed. You can type entirely in kana, or use familiar kanji with readings.

### 1. Notice the pattern

**朝（あさ）、七時（しちじ）に起（お）きます。** — “In the morning, I get up at seven.”

**顔（かお）を洗（あら）って、朝（あさ）ごはんを食（た）べます。** — “I wash my face and eat breakfast.”

**朝ごはんを食べてから、図書館（としょかん）に行（い）きます。** — “After eating breakfast, I go to the library.”

The connective **〜て** joins actions; **〜てから** explicitly marks that the first action finishes before the next begins. The final verb **食べます / 行きます** carries the sentence's polite ending. A chain like **洗って、食べて、行きます** is natural, but avoid ending a complete independent statement with a dangling **〜て** when you intend a finished sentence.

### 2. Form the connectors before composing

| Dictionary form | Linking form | Useful meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 起きる（おきる） | 起きて（おきて） | get up |
| 洗う（あらう） | 洗って（あらって） | wash |
| 食べる（たべる） | 食べて（たべて） | eat |
| 飲む（のむ） | 飲んで（のんで） | drink |
| 読む（よむ） | 読んで（よんで） | read |
| 書く（かく） | 書いて（かいて） | write |
| 行く（いく） | 行って（いって） | go |
| する | して | do |
| 来る（くる） | 来て（きて） | come |

Watch **行く → 行って**, not 行いて. **飲む / 読む → んで**, but **洗う → って**. The verb after **〜てから** can be in the polite **ます** form. For example, **本を読んでから、メモを書きます** means “After reading a book, I write a note.”

### 3. Controlled recombination

Turn each pair into one sentence, using the supplied final polite verb.

- **顔を洗う + 朝ごはんを食べます** → **顔を洗って、朝ごはんを食べます。** (“I wash my face and eat breakfast.”)
- **本を読む + メモを書きます** → **本を読んでから、メモを書きます。** (“After reading a book, I write a note.”)
- **図書館に行く + 本を読みます** → **図書館に行って、本を読みます。** (“I go to the library and read a book.”)

Try the three again with the right side hidden. **を** marks the object of reading, washing or eating; **に** marks the destination of **行く**. A common error is **図書館を行きます**; for a destination here, use **図書館に行きます**.

### 4. Guided writing

Use this frame and substitute one action in each bracket:

**朝、［時刻］に起きます。［行動Aのて形］、［行動Bのます形］。［行動Cのて形］から、［行動Dのます形］。**

One possible answer:

**朝、八時（はちじ）に起きます。水（みず）を飲（の）んで、本（ほん）を読（よ）みます。本を読んでから、メモを書（か）きます。**

“In the morning, I get up at eight. I drink water and read a book. After reading a book, I write a note.”

Notice that **本を読んでから** can repeat an action to clarify order. You can also choose different activities without repeating.

### 5. Independent writing challenge

Write **two to five short connected Japanese sentences** describing a fictional morning or an activity at a library. Include at least **two different actions**, one **〜て** connector, one **〜てから** connector, and at least one **polite final verb**. Include a time or place if helpful. Type with a Japanese IME or use kana; kanji handwriting is optional.

The practice panel below lets you write a private draft, compare a model, and revise. It **does not automatically grade free writing** or send it to a teacher. The twelve recognition checks only assess the building blocks used in your writing.

### 6. Model comparison and five-point self-review

A different valid example is:

**土曜日（どようび）、図書館（としょかん）に行（い）きます。本（ほん）を読（よ）んで、メモを書（か）きます。メモを書いてから、家（いえ）に帰（かえ）ります。**

“On Saturday, I go to the library. I read a book and write notes. After writing notes, I go home.”

Review your own draft against **five separate criteria**: (1) two to five complete sentences, (2) sensible action order, (3) correct て-forms including small っ or んで, (4) particles **を / に** appropriate to the action, and (5) a complete polite final verb. These are **self-review criteria**, not a teacher score. Different natural word orders and fictional activities are welcome.
