## Meeting a new classmate

**Can-do:** Write and understand a short self-introduction. At a community-language exchange, people fill out a brief profile and introduce themselves. This lesson teaches the **words** you need; later lessons examine the grammar and writing in more depth. All profiles and names below are fictional.

### 1. Read the profile words

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning / use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 名前 | なまえ | name |
| 何歳 | なんさい | how old; what age |
| ～歳 | ～さい | age counter, e.g. 十八歳（じゅうはっさい） |
| 二十歳 | はたち | twenty years old; special common reading |
| 出身 | しゅっしん | place of origin |
| ご出身 | ごしゅっしん | polite reference to another person's origin |
| 仕事 | しごと | work, job |
| お仕事 | おしごと | polite reference to another person's work |
| 会社員 | かいしゃいん | company employee |
| 学生 | がくせい | student |
| 大学生 | だいがくせい | university student |
| 高校生 | こうこうせい | high school student |
| 先生 | せんせい | teacher; also a respectful title |

The age suffix **歳** usually reads **さい** but has sound changes: 一歳（いっさい）, 八歳（はっさい）, 十歳（じゅっさい / じっさい）. The conventional age reading for twenty is **二十歳（はたち）**. Do not assume that a person who is a 学生 is necessarily a 大学生.

### 2. Three short profiles

**Profile A — 田中（たなか）あおい**

> 名前：田中あおい  
> 年齢（ねんれい）：十八歳（じゅうはっさい）  
> 出身：大阪（おおさか）  
> 学校：大学（だいがく）

Aoi is eighteen, from Osaka and attends a university. **出身** describes origin, **not necessarily where Aoi lives now**. A profile label such as 年齢 means “age,” while 何歳 is the question “how old?”

**Profile B — 森（もり）けん**

> 名前：森けん  
> 年齢：二十歳（はたち）  
> 出身：京都（きょうと）  
> 仕事：会社員（かいしゃいん）

Ken is twenty, from Kyoto and a company employee. Do not infer the name of his company or current address.

**Profile C — リナ**

> 名前：リナ  
> 出身：東京（とうきょう）  
> 学校：高校（こうこう）

Rina is from Tokyo and attends high school. Her age is **not stated**; do not infer it from her school.

### 3. Questions that fit the situation

| Polite question | Meaning | Typical response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| お名前は何ですか。 | What is your name? | 田中あおいです。 |
| 何歳ですか。 | How old are you? | 十八歳です。 |
| ご出身はどちらですか。 | Where are you from? | 大阪出身です。 |
| お仕事は何ですか。 | What do you do for work? | 会社員です。 |
| 学生ですか。 | Are you a student? | はい、大学生です。 |

**どちら** here is a polite way of asking “where.” **ご** and **お** in ご出身 and お仕事 make the reference to the listener more polite; do not automatically add them when describing your own origin or job. Asking someone's age or work can be intrusive in some situations: use the questions only when context makes them appropriate, and allow someone not to answer.

### 4. Notice the differences

- **大阪出身です。** — “I'm from Osaka.” This does *not* mean “I currently live in Osaka.”
- **大阪に住んでいます。** — “I live in Osaka.” This uses a different verb and describes present residence; it is here only for comparison.
- **大学生です。** — “I'm a university student.” A more specific school status than 学生です.
- **会社員です。** — “I'm a company employee.” This is not the same as 会社です (“It's a company”).

In polite nominal sentences, **です** closes a statement and **か** marks a question. The topic marker **は** is written は but pronounced *wa* in this role. You will study these structures explicitly in the next grammar lesson.

### 5. Controlled → guided → independent practice

**Controlled retrieval:** Cover the English column of the table and recall 出身, 会社員, 大学生, 何歳 and お仕事. Then uncover and check each meaning.

**Guided choice:** For Ken's profile, answer: (1) What phrase proves he is twenty? **二十歳**. (2) What phrase proves his occupation? **会社員**. (3) Can you say where he currently lives? **No**: the profile gives only 出身.

**Independent use:** Invent a fictional profile with a name, an age, an origin and a work/school status. Write two short statements, for example **リナです。東京出身です。** Add **高校生です。** if that is part of your invented profile. Compare your facts with the source profile and check that you have not mistaken origin for current residence.
