## A week of small habits

**Can-do:** Explain how often you do familiar activities. Yui (ゆい) is a fictional student keeping a short weekly journal. Read the original Japanese before using the English glosses. The questions below test what the journal *actually says*, not what you think Yui probably does.

## 1. Read Yui's journal

| Entry | Japanese journal sentence | English support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 月曜日 | <span lang="ja">月曜日は、朝七時に起きます。</span> | On Mondays I get up at seven in the morning. |
| 毎朝 | <span lang="ja">毎朝、パンを食べます。</span> | Every morning I eat bread. |
| 学校 | <span lang="ja">学校でよく日本語を勉強します。</span> | I often study Japanese at school. |
| 火曜日 | <span lang="ja">火曜日は、ときどき図書館で本を読みます。</span> | On Tuesdays I sometimes read books at the library. |
| 水曜日 | <span lang="ja">水曜日は、あまりテレビを見ません。</span> | On Wednesdays I do not watch much television. |
| 木曜日 | <span lang="ja">木曜日は、ぜんぜんコーヒーを飲みません。</span> | On Thursdays I never drink coffee. |
| 週末 | <span lang="ja">週末は、いつも公園を散歩します。</span> | On weekends I always walk in the park. |
| 日曜日 | <span lang="ja">日曜日は、友だちとよく話します。</span> | On Sundays I often talk with friends. |

This journal describes **habitual actions**, not a list of events that happened on one specific date. For example, **月曜日は** frames what Yui normally does on Mondays.

## 2. Reading toolkit

| Expression | Reading | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| いつも | いつも | always; consistently |
| よく | よく | often |
| ときどき | ときどき | sometimes |
| あまり | あまり | not very much / not often, typically with a negative verb |
| ぜんぜん | ぜんぜん | not at all, with a negative verb in these beginner examples |
| 毎朝 | まいあさ | every morning |
| 週末 | しゅうまつ | weekend(s) |
| 図書館 | としょかん | library |
| 散歩します | さんぽします | take a walk |
| 話します | はなします | talk |

## 3. Read frequency and verb endings together

The journal uses **よく日本語を勉強します** (often studies Japanese) and **ときどき図書館で本を読みます** (sometimes reads books at the library). These affirmative sentences use **ます**. Frequency words are not verb endings; they modify how the activity is described.

Contrast **あまりテレビを見ません** (doesn't watch much television) and **ぜんぜんコーヒーを飲みません** (doesn't drink coffee at all). In these beginner examples **あまり** and **ぜんぜん** are paired with **ません**. Do not translate **あまりテレビを見ません** as “watches television often.” It is a *negative* statement.

**Important nuance:** **あまり** here means “not much,” and **ぜんぜん** with a negative verb is stronger, “not at all.” Neither is a precise numerical count of times per week. In casual modern Japanese **ぜんぜん** may also appear with affirmative expressions, but this lesson teaches the standard beginner negative pattern.

## 4. Read particles and time expressions

**学校で** and **図書館で** identify the location of an action. **公園を散歩します** uses **を** to mark the area traversed while walking; do not automatically replace it with **で**. **友だちと** identifies the person Yui talks *with*. **朝七時に** gives a specific clock time, while **毎朝** means every morning without specifying an hour.

A journal sentence can tell you *how often* something happens without telling you *when* it happens. The Sunday line says Yui often talks with friends, but gives **no clock time**.

## 5. Find evidence, not guesses

**Worked example 1:** Where does Yui often study Japanese? The sentence <span lang="ja">学校でよく日本語を勉強します。</span> supports **学校** (school).

**Worked example 2:** Does Yui drink coffee on Thursdays? The sentence <span lang="ja">木曜日は、ぜんぜんコーヒーを飲みません。</span> says **no**, not merely “sometimes.”

**Worked example 3:** What time does Yui talk with friends on Sunday? The sentence <span lang="ja">日曜日は、友だちとよく話します。</span> gives no time. Answer **not stated** rather than inventing one.

## 6. Guided and independent practice

**First pass:** Circle **いつも**, **よく**, **ときどき**, **あまり** and **ぜんぜん**. Underline the verb ending in each sentence. Which two lines end in **ません**?

**Guided retrieval:** Complete the meaning: “On Tuesdays, Yui ___ reads at the library”; “On Wednesdays, Yui does not watch ___ television”; “On weekends, Yui ___ walks in the park.” Check: **ときどき**, **あまり**, **いつも**.

**Independent transfer:** Write three sentences about a *fictional* student's habits, using one affirmative frequency word and one negative pattern. Model: <span lang="ja">毎朝、パンを食べます。学校でよく勉強します。日曜日は、あまりテレビを見ません。</span> Check that each adverb fits the verb ending, and that locations and days are understandable. More than one natural response is possible.
