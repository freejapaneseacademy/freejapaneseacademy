## Your listening mission

You will hear **two original fictional planning conversations**. In Track A, two people discuss a Saturday museum visit. In Track B, two people discuss a Sunday library visit. Listen first for the overall plan, then replay to catch **who**, **how**, and **when**. These are listening questions, not kanji-reading questions.

### Before you play: recognize useful sounds

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| だれと | だれと | with whom |
| 兄と | あにと | with my older brother |
| 一人で | ひとりで | alone |
| 電車で | でんしゃで | by train |
| バスで | バスで | by bus |
| 自転車で | じてんしゃで | by bicycle |
| 午前十時 | ごぜんじゅうじ | ten in the morning |
| 日本語を勉強します | にほんごをべんきょうします | study Japanese |

A companion is marked by **と**: 兄と行きます, “I go with my older brother.” A vehicle is marked by **で**: バスで行きます, “I go by bus.” **一人で** is a fixed expression meaning “alone,” not a vehicle. **図書館で勉強します** uses で for the *location of an action*, not the means of transport.

### Round one: listen for the plan

Play Track A once without opening the transcript. Decide whether the man accepts the suggested train. Notice the correction pattern **いいえ、…** (“No, …”). Then play Track B: does the woman accept the suggestion that she is going with a friend?

### Round two: listen for details

Replay each track and answer the six questions below **before revealing the transcripts**. Listen for **午前** (morning), the distinction between **友だちと** (with a friend) and **一人で** (alone), and the difference between travelling **to** a library and studying **at** it. A written transcript is an aid for review, not evidence of blind listening ability.

### Verify after answering

Submit your responses, read the feedback, then reveal the transcript in the practice interface and locate the exact spoken phrase for each answer. If you reveal the transcript early, treat your attempt as assisted study rather than unaided listening.

### Guided production and reflection

Write two or three short sentences about a fictional outing. Model: **日曜日に図書館へ行きます。一人で自転車で行きます。図書館で日本語を勉強します。** (On Sunday I go to the library. I go alone by bicycle. I study Japanese at the library.) Check your destination **へ**, means **で**, and companion **と** or **一人で**. Your writing is not automatically graded.

Optionally shadow one short sentence *after* hearing the real generated audio. No microphone or pronunciation scoring is required. Independent qualified Japanese pronunciation and assessment reviews have **not** been claimed.

### Review your mistakes

If you miss a companion, replay the phrase immediately before **と**. If you miss a vehicle, listen for the noun immediately before **で**. If you confuse the speakers, ask which person answers the question. Return to L03 grammar when needed.
