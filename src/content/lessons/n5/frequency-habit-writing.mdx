## Your task: a short habit journal

**Can-do:** Explain how often you do familiar activities. Imagine you are joining a community-center reading club. Write **two to five connected Japanese sentences** for its habit notebook. Say what you do regularly, sometimes, and rarely or not at all. This is a **writing** lesson: no audio or pronunciation assessment is included.

### Model: ゆいの習慣（しゅうかん）

> わたしは毎朝、本を読みます。  
> 学校では、よく日本語を勉強します。  
> 土曜日は、ときどき図書館へ行きます。  
> テレビはあまり見ません。  
> 夜はゲームをぜんぜんしません。

**Reading support:** わたしは まいあさ、ほんを よみます。／がっこうでは、よく にほんごを べんきょうします。／どようびは、ときどき としょかんへ いきます。／テレビは あまり みません。／よるは ゲームを ぜんぜん しません。

**Meaning:** Every morning I read a book. At school I often study Japanese. On Saturdays I sometimes go to the library. I do not watch much television. At night I do not play games at all.

This is a fictional student's example, not a claim about a real learner.

## Build your sentences

| Expression | Meaning | Useful pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| いつも | always / habitually | いつも本を読みます。 — I always read books. |
| よく | often | よく日本語を勉強します。 — I often study Japanese. |
| ときどき | sometimes | ときどき図書館へ行きます。 — I sometimes go to the library. |
| あまり | not very much / not often | あまりテレビを見ません。 — I do not watch much television. |
| ぜんぜん | not at all | ぜんぜんゲームをしません。 — I do not play games at all. |

**Useful verbs:** 読みます（よみます, read）, 書きます（かきます, write）, 行きます（いきます, go）, 勉強します（べんきょうします, study）, 見ます（みます, watch/see）, しません (do not do). **Places and times:** 毎朝（まいあさ, every morning）, 毎日（まいにち, every day）, 学校（がっこう, school）, 図書館（としょかん, library）, 土曜日（どようび, Saturday）, 夜（よる, night).

**Word order:** A time or topic can begin the sentence: **土曜日は、ときどき図書館へ行きます。** Here **は** marks Saturday as the topic, and **へ** (pronounced *え* as a particle) indicates direction. **学校で勉強します** uses **で** for where an action happens. **本を読みます** uses **を** for the object being read.

**Negative agreement:** In these standard beginner patterns, **あまり** and **ぜんぜん** go with a negative predicate: **あまり見ません** (do not watch much), **ぜんぜん見ません** (do not watch at all). Do not write **ぜんぜん見ます** to mean “never watch.” In casual contemporary Japanese, ぜんぜん can sometimes occur with positive expressions, but that is not the pattern being practised here.

**Frequency is not a percentage:** いつも, よく and ときどき describe tendencies, not precise numerical schedules. **毎日** specifies every day, so avoid treating **ときどき** and **毎日** as interchangeable.

## Controlled practice: repair the meaning

1. “I do not read much.” Correct **あまり本を読みます。** → **あまり本を読みません。**
2. “I never watch TV.” Correct **ぜんぜんテレビを見ます。** → **ぜんぜんテレビを見ません。**
3. “I sometimes go to the library.” Select **ときどき図書館へ行きます。** rather than **毎日図書館へ行きます。**

## Guided composition

Complete this fictional journal using your own activities. Read the resulting sentences aloud only if you wish.

> わたしは毎朝、＿＿＿＿を＿＿＿＿ます。  
> ＿＿＿＿では、よく＿＿＿＿ます。  
> 土曜日は、ときどき＿＿＿＿へ行きます。  
> ＿＿＿＿はあまり＿＿＿＿ません。

**One possible completion:** わたしは毎朝、本を読みます。学校では、よく日本語を勉強します。土曜日は、ときどき図書館へ行きます。テレビはあまり見ません。 (I read a book every morning. I often study Japanese at school. I sometimes go to the library on Saturdays. I do not watch much television.)

## Independent writing workshop

Write **two to five complete sentences** in the text area below about an imaginary club member or your own ordinary routine. Include **at least two different frequency expressions**, **one negative expression** with あまり or ぜんぜん, and a meaningful action verb. You may use kana instead of unfamiliar kanji. Save your draft locally if you want to return to it. Then reveal the model and check your own writing. **The free-response text is not automatically graded.**

### Four-point self-review (one criterion each)

- **Meaning:** Are the activities and frequencies understandable?
- **Polarity:** Does あまり or ぜんぜん have the intended negative verb?
- **Particles:** Are を, で and へ used appropriately where needed?
- **Connection:** Do two to five sentences describe one coherent routine?

The multiple-choice check below tests recognition of patterns; it does not evaluate your original prose. Review your answers and revise your journal after the check.
