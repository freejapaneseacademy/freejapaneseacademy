## Write for an orientation desk

A fictional student needs a short introduction for an orientation card. Your task is to write **two to five connected Japanese sentences** that identify a person, age, place of origin and study or work. Do not use your real personal details. This is the chapter's writing and typing lesson, not an audio or pronunciation test.

## 1. Study a complete model

> はじめまして。わたしは田中あきです。二十歳です。大阪出身です。大学生です。よろしくお願いします。

*Nice to meet you. I'm Aki Tanaka. I'm twenty years old. I'm from Osaka. I'm a university student. I look forward to getting to know you.*

The four facts are **name**, **age**, **origin** and **student status**. The introduction does not say where Aki currently lives. 出身 describes origin, not necessarily current residence.

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning and use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| わたしは田中あきです。 | わたしは たなか あき です | I am Aki Tanaka; topic は is pronounced *wa*. |
| 二十歳です。 | はたち です | I am twenty; 二十歳 has the special reading はたち. |
| 大阪出身です。 | おおさか しゅっしん です | I am from Osaka; origin, not residence. |
| 大学生です。 | だいがくせい です | I am a university student. |
| 会社員です。 | かいしゃいん です | I am a company employee. |

Once the speaker is established, Japanese often omits repeated わたしは. The age and occupation sentences above are natural without an explicit subject.

## 2. Assemble polite sentences

**Topic:** わたし**は**田中あき**です**。 The topic marker is written は, even though it sounds *wa*. Do not write わたしわ in standard spelling.

**Name with の:** わたし**の**名前**は**あきです。 — “My name is Aki.” の links the person to the noun 名前. わたしのあきです is not the ordinary way to say “I am Aki”; use わたしはあきです instead.

**Age:** 十九歳（じゅうきゅうさい）, 二十歳（はたち）, 二十一歳（にじゅういっさい）. 歳 is the age counter; 人 counts people, not someone's age. Arabic numerals such as 20歳 are common, but the model practices 二十歳.

**Origin:** 京都出身です means “I'm from Kyoto.” It does **not** establish that the person lives or works there now. Current residence requires different wording, beyond this lesson's required grammar.

## 3. Compare another valid introduction

> はじめまして。わたしの名前は佐藤けんです。二十三歳です。福岡出身です。会社員です。

*Nice to meet you. My name is Ken Sato. I'm twenty-three. I'm from Fukuoka. I'm a company employee.*

Here わたしの名前は is a natural alternative to わたしは. The status 会社員 does not name a particular employer. Both models use polite です, not casual だ.

## 4. Guided reconstruction

Read this fictional card: **山本みお / 21 years old / from Nara / university student**. Write four sentences without looking at the answer.

One possible response:

> わたしは山本みおです。二十一歳です。奈良出身です。大学生です。

You may add はじめまして at the start. The point is to connect facts into a coherent introduction, not to reproduce the model's exact name or punctuation.

## 5. Independent writing

Invent a **different** fictional person. Choose a name, age, hometown and either 大学生 or 会社員. Write two to five connected Japanese sentences in the workshop below. The eight recognition questions check your understanding of written forms; they do not grade your own prose.

Before checking a model, ask yourself: does は mark the topic? If I use の, does it connect two nouns? Does each age have 歳? Am I describing origin without claiming a current address? Are all statements consistent?

## 6. Review and revise

Use four criteria: **(1)** the fictional speaker is clear, **(2)** age, origin and status facts are consistent, **(3)** は, の and です are used appropriately, **(4)** Japanese spelling and punctuation are readable. More than one natural introduction can satisfy these criteria. A self-check is not an independent Japanese teacher's grade.

Read your text quietly or aloud if you wish; no microphone or automated pronunciation scoring is involved. Return to L06 for genuine listening practice. Your draft can be saved explicitly on this device; completion and recognition attempts remain separate. Next in the blueprint is optional L08 speaking and shadowing.
