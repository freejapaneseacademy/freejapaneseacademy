## Can-do: choose a café with a friend

Two friends are deciding where to study. Read the original fictional conversation, then identify the *comparison* and the *choice*.

> ミカ：青（あお）カフェと緑（みどり）カフェと、どちらが静（しず）かですか。  
> タク：青カフェより緑カフェのほうが静かです。  
> ミカ：でも、青カフェのほうが安（やす）いですね。  
> タク：そうですね。三（みっ）つの店（みせ）の中（なか）では、白（しろ）カフェが一番（いちばん）安いです。  
> ミカ：じゃあ、今日（きょう）は白カフェに行（い）きましょう。

**English:** Mika: Which is quieter, Blue Café or Green Café? Taku: Green Café is quieter than Blue Café. Mika: But Blue Café is cheaper, isn't it? Taku: Yes. Of the three shops, White Café is the cheapest. Mika: Then let's go to White Café today.

**Notice:** **より** identifies the comparison baseline; **のほうが** identifies the side described as having more of the quality. **一番** identifies a highest degree within a group. The café names and situation are invented; no real prices or reviews are implied.

## 1 — Compare two things: A より B のほうが + adjective

**Pattern:** A より B のほうが + adjective + です.  
**Meaning:** B is more [adjective] than A.

| Japanese | Reading | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| バスより電車（でんしゃ）のほうが速（はや）いです。 | ばすよりでんしゃのほうがはやいです | The train is faster than the bus. |
| この本（ほん）よりあの本のほうが易（やさ）しいです。 | このほんよりあのほんのほうがやさしいです | That book is easier than this one. |
| 青カフェより緑カフェのほうが静（しず）かです。 | あおかふぇよりみどりかふぇのほうがしずかです | Green Café is quieter than Blue Café. |

**Form:** **静か** is a な-adjective; in a polite predicate say **静かです**, not **静かなです**. **速い** and **安い** are い-adjectives, so **速いです** and **安いです** are correct.

**Meaning changes when order changes:**  
**バスより電車のほうが速いです** = the train is faster.  
**電車よりバスのほうが速いです** = the bus is faster.  
Do not swap the two nouns unless you intend to reverse the claim.

You may also hear the shorter **B は A より速いです**. This expresses the same basic comparison but puts B first as the topic. In the target pattern, **B のほうが** highlights the side being compared.

## 2 — Ask about two choices: どちら

**A と B と、どちらが + adjective + ですか。** means “Which is more [adjective], A or B?” **どちら** is a polite two-way question word; **どっち** is its casual counterpart.

> コーヒーとお茶（ちゃ）と、どちらが好（す）きですか。  
> *Which do you like better, coffee or tea?*

> お茶のほうが好きです。  
> *I like tea better.*

With **好き**, the answer is about *preference*, not objective quality. **どちらも好きです** means “I like both.” **どちらも好きではありません** means “I don't like either.” A comparison question need not force a winner.

**Mini-dialogue:**  
A: 夏（なつ）と冬（ふゆ）と、どちらが好きですか。 (*Which do you like better, summer or winter?*)  
B: 冬のほうが好きです。 (*I prefer winter.*)

## 3 — Choose the top option in a group: 一番

**[Group] の中で、[item] が一番 + adjective + です** identifies the item with the highest degree of that quality in the stated group.

> 三つの店の中では、白カフェが一番安いです。  
> *Among the three shops, White Café is the cheapest.*

> このクラスでは、だれが一番背（せ）が高（たか）いですか。  
> *Who is the tallest in this class?*

> この三冊（さんさつ）の本の中で、どれが一番面白（おもしろ）いですか。  
> *Which of these three books is the most interesting?*

Use **どちら** for two alternatives and **どれ** for choosing an item from three or more. **一番** can also appear in preferences: **三つの中では、春（はる）が一番好きです** (*Of the three, I like spring best*). The comparison set must be clear from context.

**Important:** 一番 does not mean that something is the best in the whole world. **この三つの中で** limits the claim to those three options.

## 4 — Common confusion and repairs

| Intended meaning | Avoid | Use instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green is quieter than Blue | 緑カフェより青カフェのほうが静かです | 青カフェより緑カフェのほうが静かです |
| Which of two is cheaper? | 二つの店と、どれが安いですか | 二つの店では、どちらが安いですか |
| Which of three is cheapest? | 三つの店では、どちらが一番安いですか | 三つの店では、どれが一番安いですか |
| Green is quieter | 緑カフェのほうが静かなです | 緑カフェのほうが静かです |

The first incorrect example is **grammatical but reverses the meaning**. Always check which noun follows **より** and which follows **のほうが**.

## Controlled → guided → independent practice

**Controlled:** Fill the gaps, then check the answers below.

1. バス___電車のほうが速いです。 (*The train is faster than the bus.*)
2. お茶とコーヒーと、___が好きですか。 (*Which do you prefer?*)
3. 三つの中で、白カフェが___安いです。 (*White Café is the cheapest.*)

**Answers:** 1 **より**; 2 **どちら**; 3 **一番**.

**Guided:** Two fictional shops have these features: A is quiet but expensive; B is noisy but inexpensive. Say **A店（てん）よりB店のほうが安いです** (*B is cheaper than A*) and **B店よりA店のほうが静かです** (*A is quieter than B*). Then ask **どちらが好きですか** (*Which do you prefer?*).

**Independent writing (not automatically graded):** Invent three places to study. Write three or four polite sentences: one AよりBのほうが comparison, one どちら question, and one 一番 statement with a clear group. Avoid making factual claims about real businesses without evidence.

**Model:** 青カフェより緑カフェのほうが静かです。青カフェと緑カフェと、どちらが好きですか。三つの店の中では、白カフェが一番安いです。私（わたし）は緑カフェが好きです。  
**English:** Green Café is quieter than Blue Café. Which do you prefer, Blue Café or Green Café? Of the three shops, White Café is the cheapest. I like Green Café.

**Self-review:** Did you place the baseline after より? Did you use のほうが for the compared choice? Did you use どちら for two choices, and 一番 with an understood group? Is the adjective ending correct? Multiple natural answers are possible; this is an ungraded composition.
