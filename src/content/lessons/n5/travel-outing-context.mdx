## A real-life task: agree on a small outing

Two friends are planning a Saturday visit to a museum. Their dialogue is **fictional**, but the task is useful: understand *who is going*, *where they will meet*, *when they will leave*, and *how they will travel*. You do not need to calculate fares or book anything.

**Read once for the main idea**, then reread for the details. The destination, meeting point and transport are different pieces of information.

### Original conversation

> **ゆい:** 土曜日（どようび）、いっしょに博物館（はくぶつかん）へ行きませんか。  
> **あき:** いいですね。何時（なんじ）に行きますか。  
> **ゆい:** 午前（ごぜん）九時（くじ）に駅（えき）で会（あ）いましょう。  
> **あき:** わかりました。博物館へは何（なに）で行きますか。  
> **ゆい:** 駅から電車（でんしゃ）で行きます。  
> **あき:** 電車の切符（きっぷ）はいくらですか。  
> **ゆい:** 片道（かたみち）二百円（にひゃくえん）です。  
> **あき:** じゃあ、土曜日の午前九時に駅で会いましょう。

**English meaning**

- **Yui:** Would you like to go to the museum together on Saturday?
- **Aki:** Sounds good. What time shall we go?
- **Yui:** Let's meet at the station at 9 a.m.
- **Aki:** All right. How will we get to the museum?
- **Yui:** We'll go by train from the station.
- **Aki:** How much is a train ticket?
- **Yui:** It's 200 yen one way.
- **Aki:** Then let's meet at the station at 9 a.m. on Saturday.

This is a made-up ticket price for a language-learning scenario, **not** a current fare.

## 1. Find the plan's four anchors

| Question | Evidence from the dialogue | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who? | ゆい・あき / いっしょに | Yui and Aki, together |
| Where are they going? | 博物館へ | To the museum |
| When and where do they meet? | 午前九時に駅で会いましょう | At the station at 9 a.m. |
| How do they travel? | 駅から電車で行きます | By train from the station |

Notice that **駅** is the *meeting point* and departure point, while **博物館** is the *destination*. A question about where they meet is not asking where they will go sightseeing.

## 2. Useful expressions in context

| Japanese | Reading | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 土曜日 | どようび | Saturday |
| 博物館 | はくぶつかん | Museum |
| 午前九時 | ごぜんくじ | 9 a.m. |
| 駅 | えき | Station |
| 電車 | でんしゃ | Train |
| 切符 | きっぷ | Ticket |
| 片道 | かたみち | One way |
| 二百円 | にひゃくえん | 200 yen |

**Time + に:** 午前九時に会います。 — We meet at 9 a.m. The particle **に** marks the specific meeting time.

**Place of meeting + で:** 駅で会います。 — We meet *at* the station. This **で** marks the place where the action of meeting happens.

**Destination + へ:** 博物館へ行きます。 — We go *to* the museum. **へ** is pronounced **え** when used as a particle.

**Means + で:** 電車で行きます。 — We go *by* train. The same written particle **で** can mark the means of travel, rather than the meeting location.

**Starting point + から:** 駅から行きます。 — We go *from* the station.

**Invitation:** 行きませんか is a polite invitation, not a statement that someone refuses to go. **いいですね** accepts the suggestion in this conversation. **会いましょう** is a suggestion to meet.

## 3. Watch for common misunderstandings

**駅で会いましょう** does *not* mean “Let's go to the station by train.” It means “Let's meet at the station.” To express travelling by train, use **電車で行きます**.

**九時** is nine o'clock; **午前九時** explicitly means 9 a.m. **片道二百円** is a one-way price, not necessarily a round-trip total. Do not infer a return ticket price from this dialogue.

**博物館へは何で行きますか** asks about transport. Here **何で（なにで）** means “by what means?”; the answer is **電車で**.

## 4. Controlled and guided practice

**First pass:** Cover the English. Find **土曜日**, **博物館**, **午前九時**, and **電車**. Identify which expression answers *day*, *destination*, *time*, and *transport*.

**Second pass:** Explain the difference between **駅で会います** and **駅から電車で行きます**. The first gives the meeting place; the second gives the starting point and means of travel.

**Guided reconstruction:** Complete this summary before checking: “Yui invites Aki to the ____. They will meet on ____ at ____ at the ____. They will go by ____.” Answer: museum; Saturday; 9 a.m.; station; train.

## 5. Independent transfer

Imagine the friends change their plan: they will go to a park by bus and meet at 10 a.m. Write two or three short Japanese sentences, using **公園（こうえん）**, **バス**, and **午前十時（ごぜんじゅうじ）**. For example:

> 土曜日に公園へ行きます。午前十時に駅で会います。バスで行きます。

This is one valid model, not the only possible answer. You may rehearse the conversation aloud, but this written context lesson does not grade speaking or provide synthesized audio. The recognition check below assesses the original written dialogue; it does not score your independent composition.

## 6. Review

After answering, return to the dialogue and point to the **exact phrase** that supports each answer. If you confused the meeting point and destination, compare **駅で** with **博物館へ** again. The next lessons will expand travel vocabulary, particle combinations, reading, listening and writing.
