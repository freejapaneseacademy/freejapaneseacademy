## Mission: plan a trip with a friend—or alone

The Japanese club is planning a Saturday visit to a museum. You know **行きます** (いきます, go), **来ます** (きます, come) and **帰ります** (かえります, return). Now answer two new questions: **How do you travel?** and **Who accompanies you?** This is written grammar practice, not a listening test.

## 1. Vehicle + で: how you travel

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">電車で行きます。</span> | でんしゃで いきます | I go by train. |
| <span lang="ja">バスで学校へ行きます。</span> | バスで がっこうへ いきます | I go to school by bus. |
| <span lang="ja">自転車で公園に行きます。</span> | じてんしゃで こうえんに いきます | I go to the park by bicycle. |
| <span lang="ja">タクシーで帰ります。</span> | タクシーで かえります | I return by taxi. |

**で** marks the means of transport. A movement destination takes **に** or **へ** (pronounced え when it is the particle). In <span lang="ja">バスで駅へ行きます</span> (バスで えきへ いきます, “I go to the station by bus”), **バスで** answers *how?* and **駅へ** answers *where to?*

**On foot:** <span lang="ja">歩いて行きます</span> (あるいて いきます, “I go on foot”) uses a verb form. Do not add で after 歩いて. <span lang="ja">徒歩で行きます</span> (とほで いきます) is another natural, more notice-like expression.

## 2. Person + と: with whom

Use **person + と** for a companion:

- <span lang="ja">友だちと行きます。</span> (ともだちと いきます) — I go with a friend.
- <span lang="ja">母と電車で行きます。</span> (ははと でんしゃで いきます) — I go by train with my mother.
- <span lang="ja">田中さんと美術館へ行きます。</span> (たなかさんと びじゅつかんへ いきます) — I go to the art museum with Tanaka.

**と** here means *with*, not *by*. <span lang="ja">友だちで行きます</span> does not express “go with a friend.” Both <span lang="ja">友だちとバスで美術館に行きます</span> and <span lang="ja">バスで友だちと美術館に行きます</span> are possible; the information focus can change with word order.

## 3. 一人で: alone

<span lang="ja">一人で</span> (ひとりで) means *alone / by oneself*. It answers **with whom?**, not **which vehicle?**

- <span lang="ja">一人で行きます。</span> (ひとりで いきます) — I go alone.
- <span lang="ja">一人で電車に乗ります。</span> (ひとりで でんしゃに のります) — I board the train alone.
- <span lang="ja">日曜日は一人で図書館へ行きます。</span> (にちようびは ひとりで としょかんへ いきます) — On Sunday I go to the library alone.

Do not use <span lang="ja">一人と行きます</span> for “go alone”: **と** implies another participant.

## 4. Three functions involving で

| Expression | Function | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">電車で</span> | means of travel | <span lang="ja">電車で行きます。</span> — I go by train. |
| <span lang="ja">一人で</span> | acting alone | <span lang="ja">一人で行きます。</span> — I go alone. |
| <span lang="ja">図書館で</span> | place of an action | <span lang="ja">図書館で勉強します。</span> — I study at the library. |

Compare <span lang="ja">図書館に行きます</span> (“go **to** the library”) and <span lang="ja">図書館で勉強します</span> (“study **at** the library”). The verb and the noun before で tell you its role.

## 5. Original conversation: a club outing

Yui and Ken are at school discussing Saturday.

> <span lang="ja">ユイ：土曜日、美術館へ行きますか。</span>  
> ゆい：どようび、びじゅつかんへ いきますか。  
> Yui: “Are you going to the art museum on Saturday?”
>
> <span lang="ja">ケン：はい。友だちと電車で行きます。</span>  
> けん：はい。ともだちと でんしゃで いきます。  
> Ken: “Yes. I'll go by train with a friend.”
>
> <span lang="ja">ユイ：私は一人で行きます。バスで行きます。</span>  
> ゆい：わたしは ひとりで いきます。バスで いきます。  
> Yui: “I'll go alone. I'll go by bus.”
>
> <span lang="ja">ケン：美術館で絵を見ますか。</span>  
> けん：びじゅつかんで えを みますか。  
> Ken: “Will you look at paintings at the museum?”
>
> <span lang="ja">ユイ：はい。午後三時に家に帰ります。</span>  
> ゆい：はい。ごご さんじに いえに かえります。  
> Yui: “Yes. I'll return home at three in the afternoon.”

Ken's **友だちと** identifies his companion; **電車で** gives the transport. Yui's **一人で** means no companion; **バスで** still names transport. **美術館で** marks where they look at paintings, not where they are travelling. **午後三時に** marks a specific time.

## 6. Common errors

| Intended meaning | Avoid | Use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| with a friend | <span lang="ja">友だちで行きます。</span> | <span lang="ja">友だちと行きます。</span> | Companion と. |
| by bus | <span lang="ja">バスと行きます。</span> | <span lang="ja">バスで行きます。</span> | Means で. |
| alone | <span lang="ja">一人と行きます。</span> | <span lang="ja">一人で行きます。</span> | Fixed expression 一人で. |
| go to school | <span lang="ja">学校で行きます。</span> | <span lang="ja">学校に行きます。</span> | Destination に or へ. |

These corrections depend on the stated meanings. **学校で** is correct in <span lang="ja">学校で勉強します</span> (“study at school”).

## 7. Guided practice and independent use

Cover the answers and retrieve the particle: (a) <span lang="ja">母＿＿行きます</span> → **と**; (b) <span lang="ja">電車＿＿行きます</span> → **で**; (c) <span lang="ja">一人＿＿行きます</span> → **で**; (d) <span lang="ja">駅＿＿行きます</span> → **に** or **へ**. Explain each choice before taking the interactive quiz.

Write **three connected sentences** about a fictional outing: destination, transport and companion (or going alone). Model:

<span lang="ja">土曜日に美術館へ行きます。友だちと電車で行きます。午後四時に家に帰ります。</span>  
どようびに びじゅつかんへ いきます。ともだちと でんしゃで いきます。ごご よじに いえに かえります。  
“On Saturday I'll go to the art museum. I'll go by train with a friend. I'll return home at four in the afternoon.”

Compare your writing with the five self-review criteria below. Open writing is **not automatically graded**. Optionally read your plan aloud; this page does not record or evaluate pronunciation.
