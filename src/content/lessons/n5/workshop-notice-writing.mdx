## Your task: write a notice for a small workshop

**Chapter can-do:** Understand dates, time and basic counters. In this writing lesson, you will produce **two to five connected sentences** that a visitor could use to find an event. The setting is fictional. Your free writing is self-reviewed, not automatically graded. This lesson does not assess listening or pronunciation.

At the community center, 田中（たなか） is preparing a notice for a paper-craft class. The class is on **Friday, December 4**, starts at **10 a.m.**, has **three participants**, and each participant needs **two sheets of paper**.

### Read a model notice

> かみの工作（こうさく）クラスは12月4日（じゅうにがつよっか）、金曜日（きんようび）です。  
> 午前10時（ごぜんじゅうじ）からです。  
> 参加者（さんかしゃ）は3人（さんにん）です。  
> 紙（かみ）は一人（ひとり）2枚（にまい）です。

**Meaning:** The paper-craft class is Friday, December 4. It starts at 10 a.m. There are three participants. Each person needs two sheets of paper. The final sentence is a short notice-style expression; in fuller prose, **一人につき紙を2枚使います** means “We use two sheets of paper per person.”

The model does not state an ending time. Do not add one unless it is supplied.

## 1. Build each line from a purpose

| Purpose | Useful pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name and date | [event] は [month]月[day]日です | クラスは12月4日です。 |
| Day of week | [weekday]曜日です | 金曜日です。 |
| Start time | 午前/午後 [hour]時からです | 午前10時からです。 |
| People | [number]人です | 3人です。 |
| Flat sheets | 紙は[number]枚です | 紙は2枚です。 |
| General items | [number]つです | りんごは2つです。 |

**月（がつ）** marks the month; **日（にち／special date reading）** marks the day; **時（じ）** marks the clock hour. **から** indicates the start, whereas **まで** indicates the endpoint. **午前** means a.m. and **午後** means p.m.

## 2. Read irregular forms before typing

| Form | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12月4日 | じゅうにがつよっか | December 4 |
| 11月14日 | じゅういちがつじゅうよっか | November 14 |
| 9時 | くじ | nine o'clock |
| 1人 | ひとり | one person |
| 2人 | ふたり | two people |
| 4人 | よにん | four people |
| 2枚 | にまい | two flat sheets |
| 3つ | みっつ | three general items |

Do not write **2枚** for two people or **2人** for two sheets. **〜つ** is a general-purpose counter for many objects; for paper, **枚** is more specific. The date **4日** is **よっか**, not よんにち.

## 3. Controlled substitution

Start with **書道（しょどう）クラスは11月14日です。午前9時からです。2人です。** (“The calligraphy class is November 14. It starts at 9 a.m. There are two people.”)

Change the details to **December 4; 10 a.m.; four people**. A suitable answer is:

> 書道クラスは12月4日です。午前10時からです。4人です。

Notice that **4人** is read **よにん**. Do not change the counter to 枚 just because the class uses paper.

## 4. Guided reconstruction

A staff member gives you these details: **Tuesday, November 10; 2 p.m.; three sheets of paper**. Write a short notice without inventing a number of attendees.

One possible answer:

> 工作クラスは11月10日（とおか）、火曜日（かようび）です。  
> 午後2時からです。  
> 紙は3枚（さんまい）です。

The quantity is **paper**, not people. In the example, the three sheets are a stated supply quantity; it does not claim “per person.”

## 5. Independent writing challenge

Create an original notice of **two to five short sentences** for a fictional event. Include a month and day, a weekday, a start time, and at least one quantity with its correct counter. You may choose:

- **Option A:** Friday, December 4, 10 a.m., four participants.
- **Option B:** Monday, December 7, 1 p.m., two sheets of paper.
- **Option C:** your own *internally consistent* fictional date and details.

A sample for Option B:

> 工作クラスは12月7日、月曜日です。午後1時からです。紙は2枚です。

The weekday/date pairs above refer to an imagined 2026 December calendar; do not use them as a statement about a different year. You can type your notice in the workshop below. A model is available for comparison after you draft.

### Four-criterion self-review rubric

1. **Information:** Did you include a date, weekday, starting time and quantity?
2. **Counters:** Did you use 人 for people, 枚 for sheets or 〜つ for suitable general items?
3. **Japanese form:** Did you use 月, 日, 時 and から correctly, with clear sentence endings?
4. **Clarity:** Could a visitor identify the event details without guessing an unstated end time or quantity?

If one criterion is missing, revise that line and compare again. No automated Japanese-writing grade or native-speaker review is claimed.
