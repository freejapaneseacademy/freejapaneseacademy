## Listen for the plan, not just a word

In the first fictional conversation, two acquaintances discuss **Saturday and Sunday**. In the second, Tanaka explains a **birthday shopping trip**. Each recording has its own speakers and three questions. These are original, fictional scripts intended for licensed synthetic speech, **not** recordings of real people.

**Audio release gate:** The candidate synthesis job is not proof that WAV recordings exist. This lesson stays draft/noindex until actual audio, the QA manifest, hashes, licensing, playback and delivery checks pass. No independent native-speaker pronunciation approval is claimed.

## Before you listen: the three signals

- **読みたいです** (*yomitai desu*) — “want to read.” Attach **たい** to the polite verb stem: 読みます → 読みたいです. **〜たい** describes the speaker's desired action here.
- **かばんがほしいです** (*kaban ga hoshii desu*) — “I want a bag.” **ほしい** describes a desired **thing**, rather than an action.
- **買いに行きます** (*kai ni ikimasu*) — “go to buy.” The verb stem + **に行きます** expresses the **purpose** of going somewhere. Compare **映画を見に行きます** (*eiga o mi ni ikimasu*, “go to see a film”).

These short examples introduce meaning; they are **not the answers to the recording questions**. Do not confuse a wish with an already completed action. “Want to buy” is not the same as “bought.”

## Track A: Saturday and Sunday

A woman asks a man about his weekend. First listen for **which day** is being discussed. On a second play, note his **place**, **reading plan**, and **Sunday activity**. Listen for **土曜日** (*doyōbi*, Saturday), **日曜日** (*nichiyōbi*, Sunday), and the contrast between **読みたいです** and **見に行きます**.

## Track B: A birthday gift

Tanaka discusses buying a gift for her younger sister. Listen for **who the gift is for**, **where she plans to shop**, and **the color** she chooses. The phrase **駅の前** (*eki no mae*) means “in front of the station.” The connective **ですから** gives a reason, but this lesson tests the directly stated facts, not complex inference.

## Your listening cycle

1. Play Track A **without** opening its transcript. Listen for the overall plan.
2. Replay, answer its three questions, and submit your choices.
3. Do the same for Track B, then reveal the transcripts and English translations to locate the exact supporting phrases.
4. Replay a difficult sentence and optionally repeat it aloud. Speaking rehearsal is ungraded.

Transcripts are always available as accessibility alternatives. Revealing a transcript before submitting makes that attempt **transcript-assisted practice**, not an unaided listening score. If a recording is unavailable, do not claim a blind listening result.

## Vocabulary and meaning check

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 図書館 | としょかん | library |
| 日本語の本 | にほんごのほん | Japanese-language book |
| 映画 | えいが | film |
| 誕生日 | たんじょうび | birthday |
| 妹 | いもうと | younger sister (one's own) |
| 駅の前 | えきのまえ | in front of the station |
| 青い | あおい | blue |

**Nuance:** The person who says **妹** is referring to their own younger sister. **かばんがほしいです** is a desire; it does not prove that the bag was bought. **買いに行きます** tells us the purpose of the trip, not whether the purchase succeeds.

## After listening: guided production

Write two short sentences about an imaginary weekend or shopping plan. Use one **〜たいです** sentence and one **〜に行きます** sentence. For example: **土曜日に本を読みたいです。日曜日に本屋へ本を買いに行きます。** (*Doyōbi ni hon o yomitai desu. Nichiyōbi ni hon'ya e hon o kai ni ikimasu.* “I want to read a book on Saturday. On Sunday, I'll go to a bookstore to buy a book.”)

Check that the desired action uses a verb stem + **たい**, and the purpose of a trip uses a verb stem + **に行きます**. This writing is self-reviewed, not automatically graded. Lesson 07 develops connected written plans.
