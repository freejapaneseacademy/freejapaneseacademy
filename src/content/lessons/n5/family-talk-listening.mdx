## A family visit and a photograph

You are at a school exhibition. A visitor meets a family member; later, another pair discusses a photograph. You will listen for **who is related to whom**, rather than try to translate every word. Both scenes are fictional original academy dialogues.

### Before listening: three useful cues

- <span lang="ja">こちらは、わたしの妹です。</span> — This is my younger sister. **こちら** politely introduces a person; **の** links the speaker to the relative.
- <span lang="ja">その人は、わたしの兄です。</span> — That person is my older brother. **その人** points back to someone in focus, such as a person in a photograph.
- <span lang="ja">先生じゃありません。</span> — (He or she) is not a teacher. The negative corrects a guess; listen for the actual occupation afterward.

**Family term distinction:** <span lang="ja">母（はは）</span> and <span lang="ja">兄（あに）</span> are common when referring to your own mother or older brother to outsiders. <span lang="ja">お兄（にい）さん</span> is appropriate when politely referring to someone else's older brother. In the second scene the question uses お兄さん, while the sibling says 兄.

### Track A: meeting a family

Listen once without reading the transcript. Ask yourself: *Who is Yui? Is she a student? Who else is introduced?* Play a second time and answer the three questions. The family relationship and school role are separate facts: <span lang="ja">妹</span> means younger sister, whereas <span lang="ja">高校生（こうこうせい）</span> means high school student.

### Track B: the family photograph

A person in a photograph is discussed. Listen for the answer to <span lang="ja">だれですか</span>, then for the correction <span lang="ja">いいえ、先生じゃありません</span>. The final word <span lang="ja">会社員（かいしゃいん）</span> means company employee / office worker.

### How to practise

1. Play each track without opening its transcript. Focus on the named family relationship and one additional fact.
2. Answer the questions for that track. Submit the six answers for feedback only after both recordings are available.
3. Open the transcript **after** your first unaided attempt to compare each answer with the exact Japanese. Transcript-assisted practice is tracked separately, not treated as unaided listening.
4. Replay a short phrase, pause and optionally say it aloud. This is self-directed rehearsal, not pronunciation assessment.

### Listening versus reading

Do not guess from printed kanji forms: the test asks about audible facts. If the audio has not been generated or cannot play, the scored listening activity remains unavailable. The transcript is provided as an accessible learning alternative and for review, not as evidence of completed unaided listening.

### Follow-up writing (ungraded)

Imagine another fictional family photograph. Write two connected sentences using <span lang="ja">その人は…です</span> and <span lang="ja">…の…です</span>. Example: <span lang="ja">その人は、ミカさんの弟です。弟は学生です。</span> — That person is Mika's younger brother. Her younger brother is a student. Check whether the relative belongs to the correct person and whether **の** is placed between two nouns. Natural alternatives are welcome; this free response is not automatically scored.

### Review

**こちら** introduces someone politely; **その人** refers to an already identified person. **妹** and **兄** describe relationships; **高校生** and **会社員** describe roles. Listen for the explicit negative before deciding whether an occupation is true. No official JLPT score or verified pronunciation judgment is provided.
