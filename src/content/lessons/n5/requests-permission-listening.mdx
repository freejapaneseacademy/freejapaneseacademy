## Listen first, then confirm

**Can-do:** Understand requests, permission questions, and simple rules in a study room or community hall. Both scenes and all speakers' lines are original fictional teaching material, performed by synthetic voices if the approved audio-generation workflow succeeds.

**Important:** Listening scores are available only after authentic licensed audio files have been generated and verified. A transcript-only fallback is reading practice, not listening assessment. No pronunciation or native-speaker review is claimed.

## Listening strategy (3 minutes)

Listen once without looking at the text. On the second play, focus on the verb ending:

| Heard ending | Function | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 〜てください | Please do ... | ここで使ってください — Please use it here |
| 〜てもいいですか | May I ...? | 入ってもいいですか — May I enter? |
| 〜てもいいですよ | You may ... | 飲んでもいいですよ — You may drink |
| 〜てはいけません | Must not ... | 撮ってはいけません — Do not take photos |

A request asks another person to act; a permission question asks whether the speaker may act. The phrase **今は** means *for now*; it matters when interpreting a rule.

## Scene A — A dictionary at the study room (9 minutes)

A visitor asks to borrow a dictionary and later asks about photography. The attendant allows one action with a condition and prohibits another.

1. Play Scene A without opening the transcript. Identify the object requested.
2. Replay and listen for **ここで** (*here*) and **いけません** (*must not*).
3. Answer three audio-only questions below. After submission, reveal the original Japanese and English lines to check the exact evidence.

**Vocabulary to review after your first attempt:** 辞書（じしょ）dictionary; 使う（つかう）use; 写真（しゃしん）photo; 撮る（とる）take a photo.

## Scene B — At the community hall (9 minutes)

A visitor asks about drinking water and entering a room. A staff member grants one permission but asks the visitor to wait.

1. Play Scene B without opening the transcript. Listen for the permitted drink.
2. Replay and notice **今は** (*now, at present*) and **待ってください** (*please wait*).
3. Answer three audio-only questions, then compare with the transcript.

**Vocabulary to review after your first attempt:** 水（みず）water; 部屋（へや）room; 入る（はいる）enter; 待つ（まつ）wait.

## Review and transfer (5 minutes)

Compare **水は飲んでもいいですよ** (*Water is allowed*) with **今は入ってはいけません** (*You must not enter now*). These statements concern different actions, so they are not contradictory. In Scene A, **ここで使ってください** limits where the dictionary should be used; it does not grant permission to take pictures.

Try an ungraded written response: In two short Japanese sentences, ask whether you may use a book, then politely tell a visitor to wait here. One model is **この本を使ってもいいですか。ここで待ってください。** This is a writing transfer task, not a listening or speaking score.
