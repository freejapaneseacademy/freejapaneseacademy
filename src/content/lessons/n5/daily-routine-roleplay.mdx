## A day with a classmate

**Optional speaking practice.** You and a fictional classmate compare ordinary school days. Read the original written models aloud or practise with a partner. No microphone or speech recording is required; the page does **not** present unverified audio as genuine listening practice. If separately verified L06 audio is available, use it independently for listening.

### 1. Useful phrases

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 朝 | あさ | morning |
| 夜 | よる | evening/night |
| 何時 | なんじ | what time |
| 起きます | おきます | get up |
| 寝ます | ねます | go to bed |
| 食べます / 食べません | たべます / たべません | eat / do not eat |
| 飲みます / 飲みません | のみます / のみません | drink / do not drink |
| 行きます | いきます | go |
| 見ます / 見ません | みます / みません | watch / do not watch |
| 勉強します | べんきょうします | study |

**Grammar checkpoint.** **七時に起きます** uses **に** to mark a specific time; **学校に行きます** uses **に** for a destination. **朝ごはんを食べます** uses **を** for the object. **学校で勉強します** uses **で** for the place where the action happens. **ません** negates a habitual/nonpast action, not necessarily an action happening right now.

### 2. Model dialogue — a school day

Read A and B in alternating turns. The fictional B has a consistent routine.

**A:** 朝は何時に起きますか。  
*What time do you get up?*

**B:** 七時に起きます。  
*I get up at seven.*

**A:** 朝ごはんを食べますか。  
*Do you eat breakfast?*

**B:** はい、食べます。  
*Yes, I do.*

**A:** 学校に行きますか。  
*Do you go to school?*

**B:** はい、八時に行きます。  
*Yes, at eight.*

**A:** 学校で日本語を勉強しますか。  
*Do you study Japanese at school?*

**B:** はい、勉強します。  
*Yes, I do.*

**A:** 夜はテレビを見ますか。  
*Do you watch TV at night?*

**B:** いいえ、見ません。  
*No, I don't.*

**A:** 何時に寝ますか。  
*What time do you go to bed?*

**B:** 十一時に寝ます。  
*At eleven.*

**Interpretation:** The speaker says they get up at seven, eat breakfast, go to school at eight, study Japanese at school, do **not** watch television at night, and go to bed at eleven. These are model facts about a fictional person, not claims about the learner.

### 3. Notice the sentence structure

Compare **八時に学校に行きます** (I go to school at eight) with **学校で日本語を勉強します** (I study Japanese at school). In the first, the two **に** phrases mark time and destination. In the second, **で** marks where the activity happens and **を** marks what is studied. Do not replace **を** with **に** in **朝ごはんを食べます**.

To ask politely whether someone does an action, use the nonpast polite form plus **か**: **朝ごはんを食べますか。** An affirmative reply is **はい、食べます。** A negative reply is **いいえ、食べません。** Avoid answering a yes/no question with a contradictory verb ending.

### 4. Guided role switching

Use the interactive rehearsal cards below. Speak **A** first while hiding **B**, then reveal and compare. Switch to **B** and reply without reading the other side. Pausing to retrieve a form is useful; speed is not graded.

Substitute new information without changing the sentence pattern:

| Original | New situation | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 七時に起きます | Get up at six | 六時に起きます。 |
| 朝ごはんを食べます | Do not eat breakfast | 朝ごはんを食べません。 |
| 八時に学校に行きます | Go at nine | 九時に学校に行きます。 |
| 夜はテレビを見ません | Watch television | 夜はテレビを見ます。 |
| 十一時に寝ます | Go to bed at ten | 十時に寝ます。 |

### 5. Independent task — your own usual day

Imagine a classmate asks about your morning and evening. Produce **three to five connected short answers**: when you get up, what you eat or drink, where you go, what you study or watch, and when you sleep. Use one negative answer if appropriate. Example:

**A:** 朝は何時に起きますか。 **B:** 六時半に起きます。  
**A:** 朝ごはんを食べますか。 **B:** はい、食べます。  
**A:** 夜はテレビを見ますか。 **B:** いいえ、見ません。

**六時半** is read **ろくじはん**. The example is not a requirement to describe your actual life; inventing a routine is fine.

### 6. Self-review and written check

Check whether (1) your times are understandable, (2) **を／に／で** mark the intended roles, (3) **ます／ません** match your intended yes/no meaning, and (4) you can ask and answer politely. These are **self-reflection prompts**, not pronunciation evaluation.

The eight interactive questions assess **reading of the printed model and grammar**, not listening or speaking skill. Quiz attempts are saved in your browser. Optional typing and speaking reflections are not graded or transmitted.

Revisit the daily routine note in L05 and compare your own short routine with the L07 writing model. Authentic L06 routine-conversation audio must be checked separately for licensing, hashes and quality before it can be used as a recorded shadowing model.
