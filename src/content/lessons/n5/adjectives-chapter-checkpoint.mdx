## Mission: help a friend choose a café and a gift

This is an original fictional review board, not an advertisement or official JLPT test. First retrieve the forms, then read the reviews without translating every word. Finally answer **12 written questions**, compose your own descriptions and review the chapter's authentic audio separately when available.

### 1. Retrieve the forms

| Function | Japanese | Reading / meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| い-adjective, positive | <span lang="ja">このケーキはおいしいです。</span> | kono kēki wa oishii desu — This cake is delicious. |
| い-adjective, negative | <span lang="ja">このケーキはおいしくないです。</span> | kono kēki wa oishikunai desu — This cake is not delicious. |
| な-adjective, positive | <span lang="ja">このカフェは静かです。</span> | kono kafe wa shizuka desu — This café is quiet. |
| な-adjective, negative | <span lang="ja">このカフェは静かじゃありません。</span> | kono kafe wa shizuka ja arimasen — This café is not quiet. |
| い-adjective + noun | <span lang="ja">安いかばん</span> | yasui kaban — an inexpensive bag |
| な-adjective + noun | <span lang="ja">静かな店</span> | shizuka na mise — a quiet shop |

**Form rule:** In the negative, change the final **い** of an ordinary い-adjective to **くない**: <span lang="ja">高い → 高くない</span> (expensive → not expensive). For a な-adjective predicate, use <span lang="ja">静かじゃありません</span> (not quiet). Before a noun, use <span lang="ja">静かなカフェ</span>, but as a predicate use <span lang="ja">静かです</span> — no な before です.

**Two important exceptions:** <span lang="ja">いい → よくないです</span> (good → not good), and <span lang="ja">きれい</span> (clean / beautiful) is a **な-adjective** despite its final kana い: <span lang="ja">きれいな店</span> (a clean shop), <span lang="ja">きれいじゃありません</span> (not clean/beautiful).

### 2. Read the original review board

The speakers are fictional shoppers. The Japanese sentences are the evidence for the reading questions.

**Review A — a bakery (パン屋・パンや)**

<span lang="ja">このパン屋のパンはおいしいです。パンは少し高いです。でも、店はきれいです。</span>

*Kono pan-ya no pan wa oishii desu. Pan wa sukoshi takai desu. Demo, mise wa kirei desu.*

“The bread at this bakery is delicious. The bread is a little expensive. But the shop is clean.”

**Review B — a café (カフェ)**

<span lang="ja">このカフェは静かです。駅（えき）の近くです。コーヒーは安いです。</span>

*Kono kafe wa shizuka desu. Eki no chikaku desu. Kōhī wa yasui desu.*

“This café is quiet. It is near the station. The coffee is inexpensive.”

**Review C — two bags (かばん)**

<span lang="ja">赤いかばんは大きいです。でも、高いです。青いかばんは小さいです。安いです。</span>

*Akai kaban wa ōkii desu. Demo, takai desu. Aoi kaban wa chiisai desu. Yasui desu.*

“The red bag is large but expensive. The blue bag is small and inexpensive.”

**Review D — a notebook (ノート)**

<span lang="ja">このノートは安いです。でも、古いです。新しいノートは高いです。</span>

*Kono nōto wa yasui desu. Demo, furui desu. Atarashii nōto wa takai desu.*

“This notebook is inexpensive but old. The new notebook is expensive.”

**Evidence method:** For “Which bag is small?”, find <span lang="ja">青いかばんは小さいです</span> in Review C. Do not infer that a large bag must be better. For “What is the notebook's drawback?”, find <span lang="ja">でも、古いです</span> in Review D. The contrast word **でも** means “but.”

### 3. Controlled practice

Fill the blanks before starting the checkpoint:

1. <span lang="ja">おいしいです → おいし＿＿＿＿です。</span> (not delicious; **くない**)
2. <span lang="ja">静かです → 静か＿＿＿＿。</span> (not quiet; **じゃありません**)
3. <span lang="ja">＿＿＿＿カフェ</span> (a quiet café; **静かな**)
4. <span lang="ja">いいです → ＿＿＿＿です。</span> (not good; **よくない**)

**Common error:** <span lang="ja">きれいいカフェ</span> is not the standard form. Say <span lang="ja">きれいなカフェ</span>. Similarly, <span lang="ja">高いじゃありません</span> does not follow the usual い-adjective negative pattern; say <span lang="ja">高くないです</span>.

### 4. Guided transfer: recommend one place

A friend wants somewhere quiet and inexpensive. Use Review B to complete:

<span lang="ja">このカフェは＿＿＿＿です。コーヒーは＿＿＿＿です。駅の＿＿＿＿です。</span>

Model: <span lang="ja">このカフェは静かです。コーヒーは安いです。駅の近くです。</span>

“This café is quiet. The coffee is inexpensive. It is near the station.” This recommendation is based on explicit text evidence, not a real business listing.

### 6. Independent writing and optional speaking

Write **two to five connected Japanese sentences** describing a fictional café, food or object. Include one い-adjective, one な-adjective and at least one negative. You can use the ungraded writing box below.

Model: <span lang="ja">このカフェは静かです。ケーキはおいしいです。でも、安くないです。店はきれいです。</span>

“This café is quiet. The cake is delicious. But it is not inexpensive. The shop is clean.”

**Learner self-review rubric (not an automatic grade):** (1) Does each sentence clearly describe an identifiable thing? (2) Did you use an い-adjective and a な-adjective accurately? (3) Did you form at least one negative correctly? (4) Are **は**, **です** and punctuation appropriate? (5) Can you point to one sentence that expresses a contrast or reason? Other natural Japanese answers are welcome.

Optionally rehearse the model with a partner, taking turns describing and choosing. No microphone is activated; pronunciation is not scored. Review missed items tomorrow and again later. Browser-local quiz attempts show immediate accuracy, **not mastery**.
