## Mission: Help a visitor at the station

A visitor in a **fictional neighborhood** needs to find a small library. You will practise both sides of the conversation: first asking for help, then giving directions. You can read silently, type the lines, or say them aloud. **Speaking is optional; this page never activates a microphone or assigns a pronunciation grade.**

The separate L06 lesson provides the chapter's recorded street instructions when its approved audio is available. The exchanges below are **printed rehearsal models, not audio recordings**; do not treat them as evidence of listening ability.

## 1. Useful expressions

| Japanese | Reading | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| すみません。 | すみません | Excuse me; polite opener |
| 図書館はどこですか。 | としょかんは どこですか | Where is the library? |
| この道をまっすぐ行ってください。 | このみちを まっすぐ いってください | Please go straight along this road |
| 信号で右に曲がってください。 | しんごうで みぎに まがってください | Please turn right at the signal |
| 左に曲がってください。 | ひだりに まがってください | Please turn left |
| 銀行の隣にあります。 | ぎんこうの となりに あります | It is next to the bank |
| もう一度お願いします。 | もういちど おねがいします | Once more, please |
| ありがとうございます。 | ありがとうございます | Thank you |

**Action or location?** In **右に曲がってください**, the verb 曲がってください requests a turn. In **図書館は銀行の隣にあります**, あります states where the library is; it does not tell the listener to turn. The landmark after **で** in 信号で identifies where the turn happens. **左にあります** means “it is on the left,” not “turn left.”

## 2. Model A: Finding the library

> **旅行者（りょこうしゃ）:** すみません。図書館（としょかん）はどこですか。  
> Excuse me. Where is the library?
>
> **町の人（まちのひと）:** この道（みち）をまっすぐ行（い）ってください。  
> Please go straight along this road.
>
> **旅行者:** 信号（しんごう）で曲（ま）がりますか。  
> Do I turn at the signal?
>
> **町の人:** はい。信号で右（みぎ）に曲がってください。  
> Yes. Please turn right at the signal.
>
> **町の人:** 図書館は銀行（ぎんこう）の隣（となり）にあります。  
> The library is next to the bank.
>
> **旅行者:** ありがとうございます。  
> Thank you.

Notice the sequence: **start → go straight → turn right at the signal → locate the library next to the bank**. The last sentence describes the destination; it is not another turn.

## 3. Model B: Correct a misunderstanding

> **訪問者（ほうもんしゃ）:** 郵便局（ゆうびんきょく）はどこですか。  
> Where is the post office?
>
> **案内する人（あんないするひと）:** 公園（こうえん）までまっすぐ行ってください。  
> Please go straight as far as the park.
>
> **訪問者:** 公園で右に曲がりますか。  
> Do I turn right at the park?
>
> **案内する人:** いいえ、左（ひだり）に曲がってください。  
> No, please turn left.
>
> **訪問者:** もう一度お願いします。  
> Once more, please.
>
> **案内する人:** 公園で左に曲がってください。郵便局は右側（みぎがわ）にあります。  
> Turn left at the park. The post office is on the right side.

**Important:** Turning **left** at the park and finding the post office **on the right side** are not contradictory. One describes your action at the corner; the other describes the destination's position after the turn.

## 4. Three rehearsal rounds

**Round 1 — Visitor:** Ask **すみません。図書館はどこですか。** The guide replies **まっすぐ行ってください**. Repeat the exchange with 郵便局 (post office) or 銀行 (bank).

**Round 2 — Guide:** Use two actions and one location: **まっすぐ行ってください。信号で右に曲がってください。図書館は銀行の隣にあります。** Keep the location statement separate from the turn instruction.

**Round 3 — Repair:** The visitor asks **右に曲がりますか**. The guide replies **いいえ、左に曲がってください**. The visitor asks **もう一度お願いします**. Say the correction again slowly, then swap roles.

## 5. Independent role switch

Create a **six-line fictional exchange** with a visitor and a guide. Include a polite opening, a destination question, a straight-ahead instruction, one turn, a landmark location, and thanks. After writing it, swap roles and practise the other speaker's lines. You may compare your work with the model in the practice panel; the model is not a substitute for your own attempt.

Check five things: (1) a clear destination question; (2) the correct 右 or 左 for the intended turn; (3) 曲がってください for a turn; (4) にあります for the location; (5) polite repair or closing. No speech is recorded or scored. If you choose to speak, pause between clauses and do not assume a pronunciation grade.

## 6. Written knowledge check

The ten interactive questions test **written recognition** of the expressions, not hearing or oral fluency. Check the feedback after answering, then retry any items you missed. Attempts are stored locally when supported; the optional typed exchange is not uploaded or saved.
