## A weekend with choices

Imagine two friends planning a fictional weekend. They have a limited budget and want to choose an activity before buying anything. **Wanting an action**, **wanting an object**, and **going somewhere to do something** are related, but different.

> ミナ：来週（らいしゅう）の予定（よてい）はありますか。
>
> ユウ：土曜日（どようび）に映画（えいが）を見（み）に行（い）きたいです。でも、チケットがありません。
>
> ミナ：私（わたし）も映画を見たいです。チケットがほしいですね。
>
> ユウ：日曜日（にちようび）は博物館（はくぶつかん）に行きませんか。

**English:** Mina: “Do you have plans for next week?” Yu: “I want to go see a film on Saturday. But I don't have a ticket.” Mina: “I want to see a film too. We need tickets, don't we?” Yu: “Shall we go to a museum on Sunday?”

**Read for meaning:** Yu wants to **see** a film (an action), and Mina wants **tickets** (things). A museum is suggested for a different day. No reservation or purchase has actually been made.

## Twelve useful words

| Japanese (reading) | Meaning | Example in context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 予定（よてい） | plan; schedule | 来週の予定 — next week's plans |
| 来週（らいしゅう） | next week | 来週、行きます — I'll go next week |
| 旅行（りょこう） | trip; travel | 旅行したいです — I want to travel |
| 買い物（かいもの） | shopping | 買い物に行きます — I go shopping |
| 映画（えいが） | film; movie | 映画を見たいです — I want to watch a film |
| 博物館（はくぶつかん） | museum | 博物館に行きます — I go to a museum |
| 図書館（としょかん） | library | 図書館で本を読みます — I read at the library |
| チケット | ticket (event) | チケットがほしいです — I want a ticket |
| 切符（きっぷ） | ticket (transport) | 電車の切符 — train ticket |
| 地図（ちず） | map | 地図がほしいです — I want a map |
| カメラ | camera | カメラを買いたいです — I want to buy a camera |
| 予約（よやく） | reservation | 予約をします — I make a reservation |

**Word study:** チケット often means an admission or event ticket; 切符 commonly means a transport ticket. 予定 is your plan, while 予約 is a booking. A plan does **not** mean a reservation has been confirmed.

## Three meanings to keep apart

### 1. Want to do: 〜たいです

Attach **たい** to the verb's ます-stem: 読みます → 読みたいです (“want to read”); 見ます → 見たいです (“want to see”); 買います → 買いたいです (“want to buy”). The activity's object can be marked with **を**: **映画を見たいです。** (“I want to see a film.”)

### 2. Want a thing: 〜がほしいです

Use a **noun + がほしいです**: **地図がほしいです。** (“I want a map.”) **チケットがほしいです。** (“I want a ticket.”) Do not attach ほしい to a verb stem to mean “want to do.” The contrast is **本を読みたいです** (want to read a book) versus **本がほしいです** (want a book).

### 3. Go in order to do: 〜に行きます

With a movement verb, a verb's ます-stem plus **に** gives the purpose: **映画を見に行きます。** (“I'm going to see a film.”) **買い物に行きます。** (“I'm going shopping.”) **映画を見たいです** expresses desire; **映画を見に行きます** describes movement with a purpose. **映画を見に行きたいです** combines the two: “I want to go see a film.”

## Goal cards: what is wanted?

Read these original fictional cards.

> **Card A — ハル:** 夏休（なつやす）みに旅行（りょこう）したいです。地図（ちず）がほしいです。
>
> **Card B — ナオ:** 日曜日に図書館（としょかん）で本（ほん）を読みたいです。新（あたら）しい本がほしいです。
>
> **Card C — エミ:** 来週、友（とも）だちと買い物（かいもの）に行きます。カメラを買いたいです。

**English:** Haru wants to travel during summer vacation and wants a map. Nao wants to read at the library on Sunday and wants a new book. Emi is going shopping with a friend next week and wants to buy a camera.

**Guided retrieval:** Identify the desired **action** and desired **object** in cards A and B. In card C, find the phrase that states a **plan to go** and the separate phrase that states a **desire to buy**. Cover the English and point to the exact Japanese evidence.

## Common mistakes and social nuance

- **× チケットをほしいです** → **○ チケットがほしいです** in the introductory pattern.
- **× 本を読みほしいです** → **○ 本を読みたいです**. The verb stem takes たい.
- **× 映画を見ますに行きます** → **○ 映画を見に行きます**. Remove ます before に行きます.

These patterns normally express **your own wishes** or ask a listener about theirs. Avoid confidently declaring a third person's internal desire without evidence; **〜たがっています** is a later pattern for observed desires. A polite invitation such as **博物館に行きませんか** is a suggestion, not proof that the listener wants to go.

## Independent practice

Write three or four short sentences for a **fictional** weekend plan: one thing you want to do with **〜たいです**, one thing you want with **〜がほしいです**, and one destination or activity using **〜に行きます**. Add a time word if you can.

**Model:** 来週、図書館に行きます。本を読みたいです。新しい本がほしいです。 (“Next week I will go to the library. I want to read a book. I want a new book.”)

**Ungraded self-check:** Is the wanted object marked by が? Does a desired action use the correct verb stem + たい? If you used に行きます, does it clearly express a destination or purpose? Rehearse aloud optionally; no recording or pronunciation score is claimed.
