## A trip after class

A student writes a short note for a friend about getting from campus to a station. The places and people below are fictional. You will **read complete words**, not memorize a single reading for every character. The goal is to understand where someone goes, how they travel and when they return.

## Nine words on a travel note

| Written word | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">駅</span> | えき | station |
| <span lang="ja">電車</span> | でんしゃ | train |
| <span lang="ja">車</span> | くるま | car |
| <span lang="ja">学校</span> | がっこう | school |
| <span lang="ja">大学</span> | だいがく | university |
| <span lang="ja">家</span> | いえ | home / house |
| <span lang="ja">行きます</span> | いきます | go |
| <span lang="ja">来ます</span> | きます | come |
| <span lang="ja">帰ります</span> | かえります | return home |

**Reading clues:** 駅 is えき. In 電車（でんしゃ）, 電 means electricity and 車 means vehicle; the whole word means *train*. 車 by itself is usually くるま (*car*) in this lesson, but in 電車 it is read しゃ. 学校 is がっこう, with a small っ; 大学 is だいがく. 家 is いえ here. The movement words use kanji plus hiragana: 行きます（いきます）, 来ます（きます）, 帰ります（かえります）. The kana endings are part of the written verb.

## Original route note

> <span lang="ja">月曜日（げつようび）、学校（がっこう）から駅（えき）へ行（い）きます。駅（えき）から電車（でんしゃ）で大学（だいがく）へ行（い）きます。友（とも）だちは大学（だいがく）に来（き）ます。夕方（ゆうがた）、家（いえ）に帰（かえ）ります。</span>

**English:** On Monday, I go from school to the station. I go from the station to the university by train. My friend comes to the university. In the evening, I return home.

The day, friend and evening expressions are given with readings as supporting context, **not** new assessed kanji for this lesson. The note does not say the friend rides the same train.

## Read the movement correctly

- <span lang="ja">学校から駅へ行きます。</span> (がっこうから えきへ いきます。) — I go from school to the station. **から** marks the starting point; **へ** marks direction and is pronounced *e*.
- <span lang="ja">電車で大学へ行きます。</span> (でんしゃで だいがくへ いきます。) — I go to university by train. **で** marks the means of transport.
- <span lang="ja">友だちは大学に来ます。</span> (ともだちは だいがくに きます。) — My friend comes to the university. **来ます** presents the movement toward the relevant destination or viewpoint.
- <span lang="ja">家に帰ります。</span> (いえに かえります。) — I return home.

The particle **に** can also mark a destination. Do not confuse the reading of **車** in 車（くるま） with its reading in 電車（でんしゃ）. Similarly, **行** in 行きます is read い, not the reading it may have in another compound.

## Controlled practice

Cover the readings column. Read **駅・電車・車・学校・大学・家** aloud or silently. Reveal the readings and check each one. Then cover the meanings and match the six written words to station, train, car, school, university and home.

## Guided practice

Use the original note to underline the word that proves the student takes a train: **電車**. Circle **駅** as the first destination, and **大学** as the second destination. Find the final verb **帰ります** to show the student returns home. Explain why 車 alone cannot replace 電車 when the note explicitly says train.

## Independent use

Write a two- or three-sentence fictional journey using at least four words from the table. For example:

<span lang="ja">学校から駅へ行きます。電車で大学へ行きます。家に帰ります。</span>

(がっこうから えきへ いきます。でんしゃで だいがくへ いきます。いえに かえります。) — I go from school to the station. I go to university by train. I return home.

Self-check that each place and transport word has the intended meaning, **で** names transport, and the destination uses **へ** or **に**. This free writing is not automatically scored. Optionally read the sentences aloud; no recording or pronunciation grade is made.
