## A street sign is a reading task

**Can-do:** Understand and give basic movement instructions. In this written script lesson, you will read **actual Japanese words** rather than memorize isolated shapes. Imagine a fictional neighborhood with a station, a park and a library. You can use the signs to navigate even before you can read every kanji in a longer notice.

This lesson introduces ten **study characters**, not ten new pronunciation rules. Many kanji have multiple readings: the readings below are those used in the listed words. They are not exhaustive. No audio or pronunciation assessment is provided here.

## 1. Right and left are not interchangeable

| Character | Word | Kana | Meaning and visual cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">右</span> | <span lang="ja">右に</span> | みぎに | To the right; notice the 口-shaped lower-right portion |
| <span lang="ja">左</span> | <span lang="ja">左に</span> | ひだりに | To the left; notice 工 in the lower part |
| <span lang="ja">道</span> | <span lang="ja">この道</span> | このみち | This road or path |

Compare these two instructions:

- <span lang="ja">右に曲がってください。</span> (みぎに まがってください。) — Please turn right.
- <span lang="ja">左に曲がってください。</span> (ひだりに まがってください。) — Please turn left.

**Reading strategy:** Look at the whole word and the action verb **曲がる (まがる, turn)**. A single mistaken 右/左 changes the route. The two characters share a top stroke pattern but their interiors differ. Do not use a visual mnemonic as a substitute for checking the actual character.

## 2. Station and exit signs

| Character | Word | Kana | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">駅</span> | <span lang="ja">駅</span> | えき | Station |
| <span lang="ja">口</span> | <span lang="ja">出口</span> | でぐち | Exit; 口 here is read ぐち in the compound |

<span lang="ja">駅の出口は右にあります。</span>  
(えきの でぐちは みぎに あります。)  
“The station exit is on the right.”

**Notice:** **出口 (でぐち)** is a compound; the same 口 is read **くち** when it means “mouth” alone. The change in reading is a normal feature of Japanese vocabulary, not a spelling error. **駅の出口** means “the station's exit.” **右にあります** states its location, not an instruction to turn.

## 3. Park and library: learn the complete compounds

| Character | Useful word | Kana | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">公</span> | <span lang="ja">公園</span> | こうえん | Park (first character) |
| <span lang="ja">園</span> | <span lang="ja">公園</span> | こうえん | Park (second character) |
| <span lang="ja">図</span> | <span lang="ja">図書館</span> | としょかん | Library (first character) |
| <span lang="ja">書</span> | <span lang="ja">図書館</span> | としょかん | Library (second character) |
| <span lang="ja">館</span> | <span lang="ja">図書館</span> | としょかん | Library (third character) |

Do not assume **公** is pronounced こう in every word, or that **館** alone always means a library. The full written words **公園** and **図書館** are what you need for this route.

<span lang="ja">図書館は公園の左にあります。</span>  
(としょかんは こうえんの ひだりに あります。)  
“The library is to the left of the park.”

The particle **の** links the park to the relative position **左**; **にあります** locates the library. This is different from **左に曲がってください**, which asks someone to turn left.

## 4. Read a fictional neighborhood signboard

At the station exit, a sign displays:

| Sign | Kana support | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">駅　出口 →</span> | えき　でぐち | Station exit → |
| <span lang="ja">公園 ←</span> | こうえん | Park ← |
| <span lang="ja">図書館 ↑</span> | としょかん | Library ↑ |

Arrows on this **fictional learning sign** point from the reader's position at the sign. They are not a real map or walking instructions for an actual station.

**Evidence practice:** Which sign points left? **公園 ←**. Which sign names the library? **図書館 ↑**. Which word has three kanji? **図書館**. Reading the text and arrow together is more reliable than guessing from one character.

## 5. Trace, type and check

On paper, copy each **whole word** three times: **右・左・道・駅・出口・公園・図書館**. Start by observing the printed form, then cover it and reproduce it. If handwriting is inaccessible or uncomfortable, type the word with Japanese input instead.

For typing, enter **migi → みぎ → 右**, **hidari → ひだり → 左**, **kouen → こうえん → 公園**, and **toshokan → としょかん → 図書館**. Input-method conversion candidates vary; select the displayed word carefully. Roman letters are a typing aid, not an alternative Japanese spelling.

**Self-check rubric (ungraded):**

1. Can you distinguish 右 and 左 without a hint?
2. Can you read 出口, 公園 and 図書館 as complete words?
3. Can you write one location sentence with **にあります**?
4. Can you keep a **turn instruction** separate from a **location statement**?

## 6. Guided composition

Use this model and replace the destination or side:

<span lang="ja">公園は駅の左にあります。</span>  
(こうえんは えきの ひだりに あります。)  
“The park is to the left of the station.”

Then try: “The library is to the right of the station.” One suitable answer is **図書館は駅の右にあります。** This is an invented practice arrangement, not a claim about a real station.
