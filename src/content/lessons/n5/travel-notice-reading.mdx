## Reading a travel notice

**Can-do:** Plan a simple outing and read short itinerary details. This original fictional notice gives enough information to make a plan, but not every detail of a real journey. Your task is to identify **stated facts**, not guess missing information.

### 1. Find the labels first

| Word | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 集合 | しゅうごう | meeting / assembly |
| 出発 | しゅっぱつ | departure |
| 到着 | とうちゃく | arrival |
| 行き先 | いきさき | destination |
| 交通 | こうつう | transport |
| 運賃 | うんちん | fare |
| 片道 | かたみち | one way |
| 南口 | みなみぐち | south exit |
| 帰り | かえり | return trip |
| 切符 | きっぷ | ticket |

The label before the colon identifies the kind of fact. **集合** is when people meet, not necessarily when the train leaves. **行き先** names a destination; **到着** identifies the station and time reached.

### 2. Original fictional notice

> **日曜日の小旅行**（にちようびの しょうりょこう） — Sunday short trip
>
> 集合：午前八時四十分、青駅の南口
>
> 出発：午前九時、青駅
>
> 行き先：海の博物館
>
> 交通：電車
>
> 到着：午前九時三十分、海駅
>
> 運賃：片道四百円
>
> 帰り：午後三時、海駅から電車で帰ります。
>
> メモ：青駅の南口で友だちと会います。切符は駅で買います。

**Reading support:** 午前八時四十分（ごぜん はちじ よんじゅっぷん）8:40 a.m.; 青駅の南口（あおえきの みなみぐち）Ao Station's south exit; 午前九時（ごぜん くじ）9 a.m.; 海の博物館（うみの はくぶつかん）Sea Museum; 電車（でんしゃ）train; 午前九時三十分（ごぜん くじ さんじゅっぷん）9:30 a.m.; 海駅（うみえき）Umi Station; 片道四百円（かたみち よんひゃくえん）400 yen one way; 午後三時（ごご さんじ）3 p.m.; 帰ります（かえります）return; 友だち（ともだち）friend; 買います（かいます）buy.

**English:** Sunday short trip. Meet at 8:40 a.m. at Ao Station's south exit. Depart Ao Station at 9:00 a.m. Destination: Sea Museum. Transport: train. Arrive at Umi Station at 9:30 a.m. One-way fare: 400 yen. Return at 3:00 p.m. by train from Umi Station. Meet a friend at Ao Station's south exit. Buy tickets at the station.

All places, times and fares are **invented for this lesson**, not actual travel information.

### 3. Trace the time sequence

The meeting is **午前八時四十分** (8:40 a.m.). Departure is **午前九時** (9:00 a.m.), 20 minutes later. Arrival at Umi Station is **午前九時三十分** (9:30 a.m.). The return is **午後三時** (3:00 p.m.), not 3:00 a.m. The notice does not state when travelers reach home.

**Common mistake:** confusing **午前** (a.m.) with **午後** (p.m.), or treating the meeting time as the departure time. Always read the label beside the time.

### 4. Follow place words and particles

**青駅の南口** means “Ao Station's south exit.” **の** connects the station to its exit. **青駅の南口で友だちと会います** means “Meet a friend at Ao Station's south exit”: **で** marks the place of the action and **と** the companion.

**海駅から電車で帰ります** means “Return by train from Umi Station”: **から** identifies the starting point, and **で** the transport method. In **切符は駅で買います**, **で** instead marks the location of the purchase. The verb helps you interpret the particle.

**海の博物館** is the intended destination, whereas **海駅** is the arrival station. The notice does not specify the distance from the station to the museum. Do not assume they are the same building.

### 5. Guided evidence search

Cover the English translation and find the Japanese phrases that support these answers:

- *Meeting time?* **集合：午前八時四十分**.
- *Departure time?* **出発：午前九時**.
- *Arrival station?* **到着：午前九時三十分、海駅**.
- *One-way fare?* **運賃：片道四百円**.

Try to answer the twelve questions below without rereading the English. Every correct answer has an evidence phrase in the original notice.

### 6. Check what is NOT stated

A learner says, “The train leaves at 8:40.” Correct this using the **集合** and **出発** lines. Another says, “The museum is inside Umi Station.” The notice does not say that: it gives a **行き先** and an **到着** station separately. Finally, **片道四百円** states a *one-way* fare, not a guaranteed round-trip total.

### 7. Independent writing

Write three short Japanese sentences summarizing the meeting point, transport/destination and return trip. Compare with this model:

> 午前八時四十分に青駅の南口で会います。電車で海の博物館に行きます。午後三時に海駅から帰ります。

(ごぜん はちじ よんじゅっぷんに あおえきの みなみぐちで あいます。でんしゃで うみの はくぶつかんに いきます。ごご さんじに うみえきから かえります。)

“We meet at Ao Station's south exit at 8:40 a.m. We go to Sea Museum by train. We return from Umi Station at 3 p.m.”

**Self-check:** Is the meeting time right? Is the exit specified? Does で mark the means? Does から show the starting point? Are all claims supported by the notice? Free writing is not automatically graded.

### 8. Review

Reread the notice without the translation. Point to the evidence for each answer and optionally say the times aloud. This written L05 does not include audio or verified pronunciation; authentic itinerary listening belongs to L06.
