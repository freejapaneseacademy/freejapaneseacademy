## Mission: understand a Saturday outing

A friend needs help reading a fictional museum travel notice. Review the language, read the notice without the English first, and answer sixteen original written questions. This is not an official JLPT assessment.

### 1. How the particles work together

- <span lang="ja">午前九時に博物館へバスで行きます。</span> (ごぜんくじに はくぶつかんへ バスで いきます。) — I go to the museum by bus at 9 a.m.
- <span lang="ja">友だちと電車で駅に行きます。</span> (ともだちと でんしゃで えきに いきます。) — I go to the station by train with a friend.
- <span lang="ja">午後四時に家に帰ります。</span> (ごごよじに いえに かえります。) — I return home at 4 p.m.

**に** marks a specific time and can mark a destination; **へ** (pronounced え) marks direction toward a place; **で** marks transport; **と** marks a companion. Word order can change while the particles preserve the roles. In <span lang="ja">駅前で会います</span>, **で** instead marks the location of the meeting. In <span lang="ja">駅前を出発します</span>, **を** marks the point of departure. Do not write <span lang="ja">九時で出発します</span> for “depart at nine”; use <span lang="ja">九時に出発します</span>.

### 2. Travel vocabulary and script

| Word | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span lang="ja">出発</span> | しゅっぱつ | departure |
| <span lang="ja">到着</span> | とうちゃく | arrival |
| <span lang="ja">運賃</span> | うんちん | fare |
| <span lang="ja">片道</span> | かたみち | one way |
| <span lang="ja">往復</span> | おうふく | round trip |
| <span lang="ja">駅前</span> | えきまえ | in front of the station |
| <span lang="ja">切符</span> | きっぷ | ticket |
| <span lang="ja">時刻表</span> | じこくひょう | timetable |

Read kanji through complete words: <span lang="ja">電車（でんしゃ）</span> train; <span lang="ja">午前（ごぜん）</span> a.m.; <span lang="ja">午後（ごご）</span> p.m.; <span lang="ja">円（えん）</span> yen. <span lang="ja">九時半（くじはん）</span> means 9:30. <span lang="ja">四時（よじ）</span> uses よ, not よん.

### 3. Original fictional travel notice

All times, people and prices below are invented for learning, not actual transport information.

> **土曜日（どようび）の博物館（はくぶつかん）へのお出（で）かけ**
>
> ゆいは友（とも）だちとバスで行（い）きます。
>
> 午前（ごぜん）八時（はちじ）四十五分（よんじゅうごふん）に駅前（えきまえ）で会（あ）います。
>
> 午前九時（くじ）に駅前を出発（しゅっぱつ）します。
>
> 午前九時半（くじはん）に博物館に到着（とうちゃく）します。
>
> バスの運賃（うんちん）は片道（かたみち）三百円（さんびゃくえん）、往復（おうふく）五百円（ごひゃくえん）です。
>
> 午後（ごご）四時（よじ）に家（いえ）に帰（かえ）ります。

**English check:** On Saturday, Yui travels to the museum by bus with a friend. They meet in front of the station at 8:45 a.m., depart at 9:00 a.m., and arrive at 9:30 a.m. The fare is ¥300 one way or ¥500 round trip. They return home at 4:00 p.m.

Notice that meeting, departure and arrival are three different events. A ¥500 round-trip fare is not the same as buying two ¥300 one-way tickets. Find the exact Japanese line before answering.

### 4. Guided rehearsal

Cover the English. Locate <span lang="ja">駅前で会います</span> and <span lang="ja">駅前を出発します</span>. What happens in each? Locate <span lang="ja">片道三百円、往復五百円</span>. Which fare is for both directions? Then change the destination in this model: <span lang="ja">日曜日に図書館へ自転車で行きます。</span> (にちようびに としょかんへ じてんしゃで いきます。) — On Sunday I go to the library by bicycle.

### 5. Honest listening boundary

Chapter L06 contains original itinerary listening work. For genuine listening practice, use its real, rights-verified recording without the transcript first, then check the transcript. Printed evidence cannot establish listening comprehension. This checkpoint therefore awards a **written score only**, not a listening score; the chapter listening objective still requires separate verification.

### 6. Independent writing and optional speaking

Write two to five connected Japanese sentences for another fictional outing. Include day, destination, transport and a time. Optionally include a companion and return time.

Model: <span lang="ja">日曜日に友だちと電車で公園に行きます。午前十時に駅で会います。午後三時に家に帰ります。</span>

Reading: にちようびに ともだちと でんしゃで こうえんに いきます。ごぜんじゅうじに えきで あいます。ごごさんじに いえに かえります。

Meaning: On Sunday I go to the park by train with a friend. We meet at the station at 10 a.m. I return home at 3 p.m.

Self-review: (1) day and destination clear; (2) time に used appropriately; (3) transport で; (4) consistent polite verbs; (5) two to five connected sentences. Free writing is **not automatically graded**. Optionally rehearse aloud, changing destination and time; no microphone or pronunciation score is claimed.
