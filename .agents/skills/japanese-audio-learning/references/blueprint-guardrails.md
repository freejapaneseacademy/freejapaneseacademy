# Shared curriculum rules (Blueprint v1.0, 2026-11-01)

Source of truth: `FreeJapaneseAcademy_Master_Curriculum_Blueprint.txt` uploaded to the project. **Read exact chapter ID, Can-do, language focus and per-step objectives from that document**; do not use this summary to replace the chapter ticket. Document approval is **draft for pedagogical review**; targets are academy planning targets, not official JLPT lists.

- Audience: English-speaking beginners through N1. Level 0 (L0) before N5 is recommended but never a forced prerequisite for experienced learners.
- Each chapter: L01 context/Can-do; L02 word study; L03 grammar/function; L04 kana or kanji-in-words; L05 meaningful reading; L06 listening with **two original core audio assets**; L07 guided writing/typing; L08 optional speaking/shadowing; CHK mixed checkpoint (normally 10–25 original questions).
- L0 introduces **no kanji recognition targets**; prioritize kana, sound discrimination, IME, tracing. Later kanji is introduced via useful vocabulary, not exhaustive memorized on/kun lists.
- Each chapter needs at least one meaningful original reading and **two independent listening recordings**; for a not-yet-recorded asset write a production script/manifest and mark **unrecorded**. Text-to-speech placeholders do not count as reviewed recordings.
- Kana/kanji reading support: L0 temporary romaji and kana; N5 English + furigana; N4 on-demand furigana for unfamiliar kanji; N3 more Japanese paraphrases with English backup; N2/N1 Japanese-first where pedagogically appropriate. Render `<ruby>` correctly and do not reveal answers with furigana.
- Level-specific, unique normalized lexical entries; reuse the same ID across chapters, reviews and study centers. First exposure != mastery; new items and review items are distinct.
- JLPT evaluates language knowledge, reading and listening, **not writing or speaking**. Writing is taught additionally and speaking is **optional**. Academy-generated questions are **original JLPT-style practice**, never official JLPT items; no raw score may be described as an official scaled score or pass guarantee.
- Content status: `planned -> drafted -> internally-checked -> japanese-reviewed -> audio-rights-checked -> accessibility-verified -> assessment-validated -> published -> monitored -> revised`. No automatic publishing of drafted AI Japanese, translations, distractors, pronunciation claims, or answer keys. Named human/qualified reviewers must be identifiable in release metadata; do not fabricate sign-off.
- Scored practice must link every item to objective IDs and knowledge item IDs, include a versioned answer key and explanation for each option. No automatic grading of free composition or subjective pronunciation.
- Progress: browser-local Dexie/IndexedDB, never silently leak writing or recordings to third-party endpoints. Completion, proficiency and spaced recall are distinct. Speaking is never necessary for completion.
- Accessibility: native keyboard controls, labels, focus, responsive at 320/375/430/768/1024/1440px, contrast in light/dark themes, transcripts, correct `lang="ja"`, non-color-only feedback and screen-reader support.
- SEO: only finished, editorially approved pages are indexable; stable IDs, unique titles, canonical URLs, accurate schema and explicit attribution/rights.
