# Editorial review rubric

## Report fields
`artifactId`, `reviewDate`, `reviewerType` (AI-screen, Japanese reviewer, assessment editor, audio reviewer), `artifactRevision`, `overallStatus`, `blockers`, `majors`, `minors`, `notChecked`, `recommendation`.

For each issue log: exact line/question ID, Japanese excerpt, incorrect behavior or claim, proposed corrected wording, why it matters, affected objective/knowledge IDs, severity and confidence. Avoid stating a specific language error without illustrating it and supporting it with grammar rationale or relevant references.

## A. Instructional alignment
Can-do observable; examples fit the context; first exposure precedes assessment; amount of new material appropriate; L0/N5 support; chapter has real reading, two audio source tracks, writing rubric, optional speaking.

## B. Japanese linguistic correctness
Japanese natural in context; particles and conjugations fit time/aspect; polite/casual distinctions are intentional; ellipsis understood; nouns/classifiers and counters plausible; word senses and collocations precise; furigana matches text; romanization not misused; translations preserve intended nuance.

## C. Assessment quality
No multiple correct answers accidentally; no obvious giveaway in answer length; distractors plausible; no answer revelation in default captions/alt text; audio questions depend on content heard; answer key versions and reviewer decisions tracked.

## D. Web, access and ethics
Avoid collecting personal identifiers for self-introduction writing; local-first recording and draft persistence; captions/transcripts; mobile and keyboard handling; light/dark contrast; no invented licenses; no unreviewed Japanese copy indexed.

## Review decision rubric
`reject` if any BLOCKER; `revise` if MAJOR; `screened-only` if no detectable issue but no qualified human review; `ready-for-final-check` after human Japanese pass but other gates unresolved; `approved-for-publication` only once all independent signoffs and technical checks exist.
